Which customer decision would the conversation improve?

A useful business conversation has a job. A shopper might be deciding which product variant meets a practical need. Someone evaluating an online service might want to understand what is included before visiting the purchase page. These are hypothetical uses, not evidence of results for a particular business.

The strongest reason to explore an agent is a recurring gap between customer interest and understanding. If visitors already know what they need, another exchange may add little. If choices are confusing, the opportunity is clearer. Found First starts with that commercial distinction so the feature has a purpose beyond being new.

The business speaks for itself inside a separate experience

OpenAI describes Business Agent conversations as clearly labeled and separate from the customer's original ChatGPT conversation. A customer can choose to explore the business's offerings through its agent. That is different from the independent answer that may have introduced the business.

This distinction should inform how your brand presents the experience. The agent represents a commercial organization with particular offerings and limits. Its value is helping someone understand those offerings, rather than appearing to be a neutral comparison of every supplier. A clear business identity gives customers the context they need to evaluate what they are hearing.

Eligibility comes before a launch promise

As of November 16, 2026, the initial Business Agent rollout covers eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses. OpenAI considers the business category and intended use; claiming and verifying a business does not by itself establish agent eligibility.

Sponsored Business Agents are also rolling out to eligible Ads Manager accounts. OpenAI says Business Hub and Ads Manager settings currently do not sync. A proposal involving both therefore needs to identify the actual available experience instead of implying that one publication automatically enables every destination. Found First assesses fit and available access before discussing a managed deployment commitment.

Know where the conversation should stop

A helpful answer is not always a sale. A customer may need a clarification your published information cannot support, a bespoke quote or a conversation with your team. For that situation, a recognizable next step matters more than an agent that tries to answer everything.

OpenAI's policy requires accurate business information and prohibits invented offerings, credentials or guarantees. Our commercial view is that the same discipline makes the experience more useful: a brand should be comfortable with what is represented in its name. Availability, account support and individual exceptions deserve clear boundaries. Do not build the business case around a booking, transaction or integration that has not been confirmed for the specific experience.

Before you decide.

Is this an agent for our employees?+

No. The Business Agent discussed here is a customer-facing experience representing your business in ChatGPT. An internal employee assistant serves a different audience and purpose. The buying question here is whether prospective customers would benefit from an additional way to explore your offering.

How does it differ from a website chatbot?+

The location and customer journey differ. A website chatbot serves people already visiting your site; a ChatGPT Business Agent supports a business conversation within ChatGPT. An existing chatbot does not establish access to this feature, and the two experiences need not solve the same problem.

Will the agent make ChatGPT recommend our company?+

A Business Agent represents your business after a customer chooses that conversation. It does not purchase control over independent answers. Evaluate it on how well it helps interested customers understand your offering, while treating organic visibility as a separate objective.

Can we use the agent we already built elsewhere?+

OpenAI currently requires Sponsored Business Agents to be created in its Ads Manager builder and does not support bringing your own agent or running your own plugin there. Existing software therefore needs a separate compatibility discussion, not an assumed transfer.
