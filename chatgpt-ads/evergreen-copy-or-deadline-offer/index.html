When to put an offer deadline in ChatGPT ads

Put a deadline in a ChatGPT ad when missing it changes what the customer can buy or receive. Keep the evergreen proposition if the date only marks the end of your internal promotion calendar.

Trace the date to a customer commitment

Ask what expires, which orders qualify and what the business can still fulfill if demand arrives near the cutoff. Approve wording precise enough for someone ordering on the last eligible day. A deadline for submitting artwork and a deadline for approving a finished proof can fall days apart.

Compare messages while the offer is available

If you want to learn whether mentioning the date helps, run both messages during the same offer window and give both audiences access to the same terms. A comparison with a quiet period after the offer ends also captures the change in availability. Have evergreen copy ready for when the business closes the offer.

The print studio’s final foil run

Imagine a stationery studio with a foil-press booking, an artwork approval calendar and an order page for programs. The press booking fixes the last day a customer can approve a proof for foil finishing. One message could lead with that approval deadline, another with the foil finish. Both should take buyers to the proof timetable. If the studio secures another run and approvals can stay open, withdraw the original last-chance wording and use the finish-led message.
