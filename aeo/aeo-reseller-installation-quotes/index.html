AEO for resellers quoting equipment with installation

If your resellers sell equipment and a separate company installs it, use AEO to clarify what a buyer can settle with each business. Someone asking for a fitted price needs to know whose quote covers the work and whether placing the equipment order reserves an installation date.

Follow the signatures on the quote

Read the order form alongside the installer’s scope of work. Check who selects the model, who assesses site conditions and who accepts the booking. If the installer contracts directly with the buyer, put that fact beside the installation option. A partner logo next to the equipment price leaves the most important question open.

Give people a way to buy the complete job

Where a survey can change the equipment specification, send a buyer seeking installation to the survey desk first. Buyers who already have an installer and a confirmed specification can go straight to the reseller. Both routes belong on the product page because they lead to different commitments.

Two routes for a pottery kiln

Imagine a kiln reseller whose order sheet includes curbside delivery. Its installation partner’s survey form asks for doorway width, electrical supply and the proposed position. The reseller can answer a studio’s delivery question from the order sheet. For a request to supply and fit the kiln upstairs, the page offers the partner survey before checkout. If the studio’s own installer has signed off the model, the reseller takes the equipment order without arranging that visit.
