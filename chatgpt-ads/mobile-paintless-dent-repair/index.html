When to offer mobile paintless dent repair in ChatGPT ads

Choose a mobile paintless dent repair offer for ChatGPT ads only when your technician can qualify both the dent and the work location before dispatch. A photograph can help with the panel decision while leaving the parking situation entirely unresolved.

Define what the technician can evaluate remotely

Use the technician’s review notes to decide which pictures to request. A close view may show the surface, while a wider view locates the dent relative to an edge or body line. When the technician needs to inspect access behind a panel, invite the owner to the shop for that decision before accepting a mobile repair.

Check the particular parking space

An address inside the service area settles travel distance. It tells the dispatcher little about permission to work there, room to open a door or shelter required by the technician. Describe the site requirements alongside the mobile offer so buyers can choose a suitable location.

A door ding qualifies, but the office garage does not

In a hypothetical mobile shop, a technician reviews photos of a sedan door and marks the damage suitable for an on-site appointment. The dispatch sheet also requires unobstructed access beside that door. The owner’s reserved office space sits against a wall, and building management will not allow work in the aisle. The dispatcher offers the shop appointment. When the owner instead supplies a driveway photo showing the required workspace and confirms permission, the mobile visit becomes a service the dispatcher can actually schedule.
