A familiar name deserves a fresh look

You have worked to make your business worth finding. A changing search experience is a reason to look at that presence again, especially if it has stopped reflecting the company you have become.

Found First is for businesses ready to pursue a position worthy of the work they put into the company. You have grown. Your presence should have some ambition too.

Compete for the closer look

When someone is considering a purchase, a provider or an unfamiliar brand, attention is valuable. A business with something substantial to offer should take that competition seriously.

We want your presence to carry the weight of the business behind it. Confident. Recognizable. Worth a closer look. There is no prize for being the best-kept secret in your market.

Expect more from your presence

A brand can have years of experience behind it and still make a weak first impression online. That gap deserves attention.

Your reputation has a story. Your company has a reason to be chosen. Found First brings the expectation that your presence should stand up to both. Search may change its shape. Keep your standards high.

Questions about Google's AI features

Are AI Overviews and AI Mode part of Google Search?+

Yes. Google describes both as AI features in its Search experience.

Is Found First relevant if we already invest in search?+

Potentially. We can discuss your existing position and ambitions to determine whether an engagement fits your business.

Can you guarantee inclusion in Google's AI features?+

No. Google does not guarantee inclusion, and neither does Found First. We do not promise rankings, mentions or leads.
