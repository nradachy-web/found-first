ChatGPT advertising · Catalog campaigns and feed readiness

Which product photo belongs in ChatGPT ads?

Choose a catalog photo that accurately shows what the buyer receives. When a supplier image and the current item disagree, resolve the discrepancy before assigning that image to a product in ChatGPT ads.

Inspect the asset attached to the row

Open the image address from the merchant's exported row. The storefront may already have the corrected shot while the catalog still points to a supplier photograph. Compare the actual image with the current item specification and a warehouse sample. File names and thumbnails can survive an asset replacement and conceal the wrong finish or an accessory that no longer ships.

Decide what a reasonable buyer would expect

A styled image can help explain scale and use. Trouble starts when the most prominent object in the frame appears to be part of the purchase. Where the product name has to work hard to undo that impression, commission a clearer primary photo and keep the lifestyle scene as supporting material on the store.

A planter photographed on a stand

Imagine a ceramics brand offering a stoneware planter. Its supplier shot centers the planter on a fitted oak stand, while the packing list contains only the pot. The team opens the catalog image and sees that the stand fills much of the frame. It selects a standalone pot photo for the paid offer. If the packing list for a separate gift edition includes the fitted stand, the original shot can accurately represent that edition once the finish is checked.
