Keep AI search in house or hire an agency?

Choosing between an internal AI search function and an agency is a decision about responsibility, expertise and capacity. Found First supports organic AI visibility and related website improvements, while your business retains authority over its products and claims. The useful question is which work your team can consistently own and where outside judgment would help turn an important discovery problem into an approved improvement.

Separate business knowledge from specialist capacity

Your team understands the commercial priorities, customer relationships and internal constraints that an agency must learn. That knowledge belongs close to the decision. It does not follow that the same people have enough time to investigate how the business appears across AI search experiences or make every necessary website change.

An internal model works when the responsibility is real rather than added informally to an already full role. Agency support can be useful when a specific skill or capacity gap is holding progress back. The decision should be grounded in the work that needs doing, not a general preference for outsourcing or hiring.

Avoid a function with information but no authority

An employee or agency can identify an inaccurate public description and still be unable to resolve it. Product approval may sit elsewhere. Website access may be controlled by a separate team. A legal review may be needed for particular language. Ownership of AI visibility should include a practical relationship with the people who can authorize the response.

If your team already has strong search and editorial capability, the missing element may be coordination rather than another specialist. If there is no one to interpret the problem or carry approved changes through, a narrowly defined outside role can be more useful than an ambitious internal title without operating support.

Use Found First where commercial and technical judgment meet

Found First offers organic AI search visibility with website and content improvements supporting that goal. This combines the question of how the business is represented with the question of what a buyer finds after following a link. Those two experiences should tell the same story about what you offer and who it serves.

The arrangement can support an internal marketing lead rather than displace one. Your people approve the facts, supply product context and judge commercial fit. Found First can contribute focused expertise around discovery and public presentation. The most useful boundary is one where each party has a clear responsibility and neither assumes the other owns an unresolved decision.

Compare operating models against a real trigger

A product launch, repeated misclassification or a major website transition can provide a concrete reason to seek help. An undefined desire to keep up with AI rarely provides enough direction for either a new hire or an agency. Establish the business consequence first, then consider the combination of skills needed to address it.

It is also reasonable to change the arrangement as the organization develops. A team may start with specialist help and later own more internally, or retain internal strategy while commissioning implementation. The first conversation should make that choice clearer rather than assume the largest possible ongoing scope is automatically appropriate.

Before you decide.

Can our SEO lead own AI search as well?+

Yes, if the person has the expertise, capacity and authority required. Google states that existing search fundamentals remain relevant to its AI features. The important question is whether the expanded responsibility can be carried out well alongside existing commitments.

What must stay inside the company?+

Authority over product facts, commercial priorities and approved claims should remain with the business. Outside help can improve analysis and execution, but it should not invent the position or commit the organization to capabilities it has not verified.

Can Found First support a small internal team?+

That can be a useful fit when a clear gap exists. The discussion should identify the internal owner and the decisions that need help, so outside work adds capacity without leaving your team with another disconnected stream of recommendations.
