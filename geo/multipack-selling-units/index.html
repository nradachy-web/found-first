GEO for catalog offers sold by the pair or case

For multi-pack offers, make the GEO assignment answer one purchasing question in plain English. When the customer adds one item to the cart, how much usable product arrives? Start from the unit the merchant actually sells, then explain any packaging inside it.

Follow one order through the packaging

A pack count in the manufacturer catalog can describe an inner sleeve, a retail box or the carton sent to a warehouse. Match the order line to the relevant package label. A photo showing one piece is fine for explaining its construction, provided the adjacent offer text identifies the quantity supplied.

Compare like units in buying guides

Choose a unit the buyer can use to plan an order. For a maintenance team stocking gloves, pairs may be more useful than individual gloves. For a mixed carton, also explain how the contents break down by size if the supplier documents the split. A total count alone cannot answer whether everyone on a crew gets a usable pair.

What does a dozen gloves buy?

Suppose a workwear merchant's listing says “dozen gloves.” Its supplier sheet describes six pairs per sleeve, and the pick ticket shows that one cart item ships as one sleeve. The GEO editor can say “six pairs per order” and change a comparison that credited the offer with twelve pairs. If the pick ticket instead calls for two of those sleeves, list twelve pairs and explain the two-sleeve packing. The supplier's sleeve label stays accurate in both cases. The merchant's fulfillment unit changes the comparison.
