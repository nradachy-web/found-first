Good questions.

Clear scope. Useful evidence. A team that can carry the work through.

What does Found First actually do?+

We connect AI search research, website improvements, answer content and managed ChatGPT advertising. Growth adds recurring research, website optimization and publishing across 3 agreed channels. Expansion broadens distribution beyond those 3 through an agreed plan for relevant channels.

What does your research measure?+

We test a defined set of customer questions across selected model routes, retain the answers and review business mentions and source evidence. Published findings show the date, sample and search method. API observations do not reproduce every consumer app or guarantee the next answer.

Do ads influence organic AI answers?+

No. ChatGPT ads are labeled paid placements, separate from the answer. We measure paid performance and organic discovery separately. Ad availability and launch depend on eligibility, account access and an approved media budget.

How much does the service cost?+

Foundation starts at $2,000 per month, Growth at $3,500 and Expansion at $5,000. Advertising spend is separate. We confirm delivery allowances, term, support and ownership in your written scope. New websites and major rebuilds are scoped separately.

Will you publish our private research?+

Your detailed research belongs in your delivery workspace. Public insights use selected findings and appropriate context. Raw conversations, exact research prompts, source inventories and collection methods are not included in our public research articles.

What is in the free AI visibility check?+

Send your website and what you sell. Nick reviews an initial sample of how AI describes your business and replies with what we found and a useful next step. The full research study is a separate, scoped engagement.
