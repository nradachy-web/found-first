ChatGPT advertising · Hotels, tours and events

ChatGPT ads for hotel room block inquiries

Use ChatGPT ads for room blocks when your group desk wants inquiries from people choosing a hotel for an event. Give guests who already have a block a separate way to find their reservation link. Both buyers use the phrase “wedding rooms,” but only the organizer can bring you a new block.

Ask for the pattern of the stay

A total room count hides the information a group sales manager needs. Ask which night will be busiest, whether guests arrive early or stay late, and whether everyone needs the same room type. Let an organizer give an estimate. Your desk can use a rough nightly breakdown to decide whether to offer rooms, discuss other dates or decline promptly.

Name the terms that need a conversation

Use the page to explain who signs the agreement, who pays for individual stays and when unclaimed rooms return to inventory. Where those terms vary by event, identify what the desk will confirm in its proposal. Label the inquiry button “Request a room block” so the organizer knows a submission starts that review.

A wedding with an extra night on each side

Suppose a hotel’s group inventory sheet shows rooms on the wedding night, while its arrivals calendar shows a convention checking in the next day. An organizer’s request includes a farewell brunch. The sales manager can propose a block ending before the convention and flag the missing night. If the rooming estimate shows everyone leaves after brunch that day, the original block covers the stay and the manager can send terms.
