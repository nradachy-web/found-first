Questions worth asking.

Does a countywide reputation require multiple offices?+

No. A company can serve a wider audience through one location, remote delivery or planned travel. The public explanation should match the actual model so customers understand the relationship before making assumptions about access.

Should the county seat define the whole market?+

Not automatically. Richmond's role provides geographic context, while the county includes other communities and customer needs. A service business should choose its priorities according to actual operations and buyer fit rather than rely on one place to stand for every opportunity.

What if recognition is strongest in only one city?+

That can be a sensible starting point for examining broader discovery. The useful objective is to carry genuine strengths into relevant new consideration, while recognizing that reputation in one location does not prove equivalent awareness or service suitability elsewhere.
