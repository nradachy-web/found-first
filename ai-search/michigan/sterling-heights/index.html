Questions worth asking.

Can AI discovery replace supplier qualification?+

No. It can help a buyer identify or understand potential providers, while qualification remains a separate commercial and technical decision. The business should be presented accurately enough to support that next evaluation.

Should every local industrial firm claim defense expertise?+

Only firms with relevant, supportable capability should use that position. Being located in an industrial community does not determine the assignments a company can accept. A narrower accurate specialty is more useful to a buyer than an unsupported sector claim.

What if our customers use broad terms for specialized work?+

That makes the connection between the problem and the capability especially important. The buyer should be able to recognize the relevant service without already knowing the exact vocabulary your technical team uses internally.
