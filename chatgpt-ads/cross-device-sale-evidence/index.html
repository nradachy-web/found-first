ChatGPT advertising · Conversion measurement and sales outcomes

Crediting cross-device sales to ChatGPT ads

Credit a cross-device sale to a ChatGPT ads inquiry in your own reporting when a durable business record connects the two. If the only connection is that a mobile visit preceded a desktop order, record the sale and leave its source open.

Use a reference that travels with the purchase

A buyer may start a configuration on a phone and forward the resulting quote to a colleague who places the order. Follow the quote reference from the campaign-linked inquiry to the accepted order. That chain can connect a purchase across devices and even across people. Matching device types, locations or product preferences offers much weaker evidence.

Leave room for sales with unknown sources

In the budget review, show confirmed linked sales alongside orders whose source you could not establish. OpenAI describes conversion reporting as dependent on event setup and attribution. Its totals can differ from the sales ledger. An unexplained difference does not identify which individual orders crossed devices.

From a phone-generated proof to a desktop order

A badge printer might receive a mobile request for an artwork proof through its campaign landing page. Purchasing completes checkout on a desktop using a general office email. If the purchase order cites the unique proof reference in the original request, the owner has grounds to link the sale and count it once. If the buyer simply chose the same standard badge color as a recent mobile visitor, the owner leaves that order unattributed when assessing campaign revenue.
