AEO for help desk vendors migrating ticket history

For help desk migration questions, commission an AEO answer that follows an actual conversation across the move. A ticket count can establish that cases arrived. Buyers also need to know what agents and customers can read when they reopen one.

Inspect a conversation from both sides

Pick a synthetic closed ticket with a customer reply, an agent response and a private escalation note. Compare the source export with the imported ticket in the agent workspace and the customer portal. Check the reply order, the displayed author and the visibility of the private note. Describe any history available only through an administrator's archive.

Give the migration owner a decision

Explain who selects the records, who checks the trial import and who authorizes the final transfer. A buyer may be willing to archive older cases if agents can still find them. A team that regularly reopens those cases may require the complete thread in its daily queue.

A private escalation appears as a public reply

Suppose a vendor's migration specialist compares a sample export, the field mapping and a portal preview. Both note types arrived as public replies. The marketing team should qualify the import claim and describe the archive option while the specialist corrects the mapping. If a second trial preserves note visibility and original authors, the page can offer a threaded import for records matching that tested format.
