ChatGPT advertising · Conversion measurement and sales outcomes

Which month gets the sale in a ChatGPT ads report?

Use the first inquiry date to evaluate the ChatGPT ads spending that brought a buyer in. Use the confirmed sale date when answering how much business sales closed in a given month. The same transaction can belong in different months in those two views.

Keep the evidence behind the stage change

A CRM often tells you when a rep moved a deal to won. Check what that date represents in your own system. If reps enter updates after the fact, keep the documented acceptance date alongside the entry date. Use the source your team has agreed establishes a sale, such as an accepted order, and label a missing date as unknown.

Compare inquiries at the same age

When choosing a campaign budget, look at what became of each month's inquiries after the same amount of follow-up. Otherwise last month's buyers have had more chances to close than this month's. Keep open deals visible and date the review so finance can reproduce the comparison as more sales arrive.

A late update after month end

Imagine that a buyer inquires in March, accepts a print order in April and appears in a May batch of CRM updates. The signed order supports an April sale. The marketing director credits the outcome to the March inquiry group when reviewing acquisition, and excludes it from May's new sales. If the April document was an unaccepted estimate and the first approved order arrived in May, the sales month moves to May while the inquiry stays in March.
