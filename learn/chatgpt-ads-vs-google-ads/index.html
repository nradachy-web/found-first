The logo does not close the sale

A familiar platform can feel comfortable. A new one can feel irresistible. Neither feeling is a business case on its own.

We care about the company doing the advertising: its offer, its customers and the business it wants to become. A platform should earn its place in that picture.

Experience belongs at the table

Nick leads Modern Apex Strategies. Jeff leads Frontier App Labs. Found First brings their advertising and technology backgrounds to the business of getting your name seen.

That combination comes with a point of view. We take emerging opportunities seriously because the businesses behind them deserve serious attention. The novelty of a platform is only the beginning.

Compete with conviction

Your market is real. So are the companies trying to win the same customers. The standard for your advertising should be higher than keeping up with a headline.

Found First is built for businesses with an appetite to compete and an offer worth putting in front of people. Bring that conviction. The choice of channel should serve the business behind it.

Questions before choosing a channel

Do we have to choose one platform forever?+

No. A business can consider more than one advertising channel. Fit depends on the business, and an engagement starts with that conversation.

Does Found First bring Google Ads experience?+

Yes. Nick leads Modern Apex Strategies, where Google Ads is part of the agency's work. That experience does not establish results on another platform.

Can Found First guarantee advertising results?+

No. We do not guarantee delivery, leads or sales from either platform. The business case deserves a private discussion grounded in your situation.
