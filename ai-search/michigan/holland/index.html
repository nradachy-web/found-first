Questions worth asking.

Should a Holland business avoid seasonal positioning?+

No. A seasonal offer can be commercially important. The useful distinction is whether it accurately represents the whole business or only one part. Customers should also be able to recognize genuine year-round services when those are central to the company.

What if we operate only during part of the year?+

Then that reality should shape expectations. The business can have a strong position without implying continuous availability. Buyers planning ahead need to understand the nature of the offer and confirm the timing relevant to their intended purchase.

Can a downtown business attract organizational customers too?+

Yes, if it offers something those buyers need. A consumer-facing setting does not define the full customer base. The company should make its organizational offer understandable rather than expecting a buyer to infer it from the retail experience.
