Scoping a single-queue help desk pilot for ChatGPT ads

Advertise a single-queue pilot only if the buyer can move that queue through intake, assignment and closure without moving the rest of support. A small queue can still be a poor pilot when most tickets cross into teams working in the old system.

Trace the exits as well as the entrances

Ask for a routing diagram or a redacted set of ticket histories. Check where replies arrive, which team can close a case and where unresolved work goes at shift change. Select a queue whose team has authority over the task being tested. Otherwise the pilot mainly measures time spent copying tickets between systems.

Define what happens at the end

Describe how the buyer can retrieve the pilot's ticket records and who owns any cases still open. Agree on the operational question that will inform rollout. A pilot request reported as an ad conversion records interest in the offer. Sales needs a separate account of what the support manager learned from running it.

Accessory replacements versus disputed orders

Suppose a support vendor reviews a buyer's queue map and two sample histories. The accessories team can receive a broken-part report, authorize the replacement and close the ticket, making it a workable pilot for approval routing. Disputed orders spend much of their life in a finance queue that will stay in the old tool. If finance approval also turns out to be mandatory for replacement parts, the vendor revises the pilot with the finance owner before promising that a single queue will test the process.
