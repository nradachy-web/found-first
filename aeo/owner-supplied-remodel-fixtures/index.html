AEO for remodelers who accept owner-supplied fixtures

If your remodeling firm accepts some owner-supplied fixtures, publish the acceptance decision before a homeowner orders. The strongest AEO answer identifies what your team needs to review and separates permission to supply an item from agreement to install it.

Spell out what “supplied by owner” buys

Read across the selection sheet, the purchase list and the proposal. One may identify the person choosing a fixture while another assigns the actual purchase. If the homeowner chooses a model and your firm places the order, describe that arrangement separately from a customer bringing a boxed fixture to the job.

Use a question that changes the estimate

A useful question is whether the owner has a model number and delivery information available for review. An inspiration photograph may establish the look the owner wants. It gives your estimator little basis for committing to a particular installation or saying that receiving and storage are included.

A bath fixture already on the homeowner's shortlist

Suppose a remodeler's blank selection sheet asks for the model, supplier and expected arrival. Its sample proposal assigns receipt of owner purchases to the homeowner. The writer could explain how a homeowner with a model sheet sends it for review before ordering and how the proposal records an accepted item. If the item has already been ordered and the delivery requires the remodeler to receive it, the inquiry goes to the project manager to resolve that responsibility before the installation enters the scope.
