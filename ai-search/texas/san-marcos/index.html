Questions worth asking.

Should every San Marcos business mention the university?+

Only when it helps explain the actual customer situation. The university is meaningful local context, but a business serving unrelated customers should not force its identity around campus. The service and buyer remain the center of the decision.

Can AI search influence someone before they move?+

It can be part of their research while comparing local providers. That makes accurate service descriptions valuable before the customer has local experience. A business should still make practical availability clear when the person makes direct contact.

What if a parent or property manager is the purchaser?+

The person paying, arranging and receiving the service may be different. A useful business identity should make responsibility understandable across those roles. Otherwise a broadly accurate description can leave essential expectations unresolved.
