Compare the buying situation before the dashboard

A customer who discovers a product through a visual message may not have been actively considering that category. Another customer may be discussing a need and comparing possible solutions. Those are different situations, even when the same product could serve both people.

The comparison should therefore begin with what you need the advertisement to accomplish. For a design-led consumer product, visual demonstration may be central to interest. For a product with important fit questions, explanation may carry more weight. These are business considerations, not guarantees about how every person behaves on either platform. Found First helps evaluate the role your offer could plausibly play.

Do not assume a winning message transfers unchanged

An ad created for a particular Meta placement may rely on a sequence, visual style or familiar audience expectation. Moving the same assets elsewhere does not prove the proposition remains clear. The offer needs to stand on its own in the context where the customer encounters it.

Found First develops ChatGPT advertising around the actual product or service and its destination. Existing creative can inform the work, but the commercial question comes first. What does the buyer need to understand, and what should happen after the click? A new channel deserves a coherent answer rather than a copy of the last successful campaign.

Keep comparisons fair to the underlying outcomes

A cheap visit is not necessarily a good customer. A reported purchase may reflect a different attribution approach from another system. If one channel introduces buyers earlier and another captures a later action, a simple side-by-side total may conceal the distinction.

We help frame evaluation around outcomes your business recognizes, including whether the customer and order are worthwhile. That does not require assuming the platforms are equivalent or declaring a winner before sufficient evidence exists. ChatGPT advertising should earn its place through a defined commercial role, while established Meta activity should be judged on the work it actually performs for your business.

Add a channel when the offer has a reason to travel

A company may have a strong Meta program and still want to explore an additional route to consideration. Another may need to improve its offer or store before extending paid distribution. Both are legitimate positions. The existence of a new placement is not, by itself, a reason to divide attention.

Found First's consultation can clarify the customer decision, the current acquisition strengths and the gap you want ChatGPT to address. The recommendation is about fit within your business, not replacing a functioning channel because a new one has become available.

Before you decide.

Is ChatGPT advertising cheaper than Meta?+

There is no universal answer that should guide your budget. Costs and customer value depend on the offer, delivery conditions and outcomes. Found First does not substitute a broad platform claim for evidence about your business.

Can we use the same landing page for both?+

Yes, if it answers the relevant buying question and accurately continues each advertisement's promise. A shared page may need adjustment when the channels introduce different offers or ask visitors to take different next steps.

Should a brand with strong visual products consider ChatGPT?+

It can assess the opportunity, especially where shoppers need help understanding suitability or differences. Visual appeal remains useful, but the decision should consider the full buying journey. Product type alone does not establish channel performance.
