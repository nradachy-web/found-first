ChatGPT ads for roof replacement estimates after a storm

If your roofing company can estimate replacements after a storm, advertise the estimate and tell homeowners when someone can visit. A homeowner looking at a ceiling leak may need help well before your estimator can arrive.

Name the appointment the homeowner can book

Use the same appointment name in the sponsored message, the page heading and the form confirmation. Say which roof materials the estimator handles, whether the homeowner needs to attend and what they receive afterward. An invitation to request a written replacement scope gives someone comparing contractors a reason to respond.

Keep dispatch promises tied to the crew schedule

Review the storm page with whoever answers the office phone. If active leaks and temporary covers go to a different team, describe how those requests are handled before the estimate form. Phrases such as “storm response” can imply a truck is on its way when the office is only reserving an inspection.

A shingle crew with room for estimates

Suppose the estimator’s calendar has openings next week, the on-call roster is empty and the company has a sample shingle replacement proposal. The owner could run “Book a shingle roof replacement estimate,” show a redacted proposal and list the towns being visited. If a repair crew becomes available for temporary covers, give that service its own phone intake with current hours. Keep the estimate form for homeowners who can wait for a proposal.
