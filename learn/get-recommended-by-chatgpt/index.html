How to give ChatGPT a stronger reason to mention your business.

There is no submission that guarantees a ChatGPT recommendation. The practical work is making accurate, useful information about your business available, answering questions buyers ask, and building evidence that can be checked beyond your own claims.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

Before asking why a competitor gets mentioned, ask what the assistant is trying to answer. A broad homepage may not explain a specific integration, location, service limitation, or buying criterion. A useful source needs enough detail to establish a fit, including the cases where your offer is not appropriate.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Check what is being said

Test branded and non-branded questions. Save the response and any sources. Identify factual errors separately from simple absence.

02 / Publish the missing explanation

Create or improve the page that should answer the question. Use specific facts, a clear author or business identity, and supporting documentation.

03 / Develop corroborating evidence

Keep public profiles accurate and pursue relevant, earned mentions. Customer reviews and credible references should reflect real experiences, with permission where needed.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • What should I know before choosing [brand]?
  • Which option supports [unusual requirement]?
  • Compare [brand] with [alternative] for [use case].

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • A branded accuracy review.
  • An answer-content plan based on observed gaps.
  • A public evidence and business fact checklist.
  • A repeatable record of mentions and source citations.

Before you get started.

Can I pay to be recommended in an answer?

ChatGPT ads do not influence organic answers. Paid campaigns and organic visibility are separate projects with separate measurements.

Should I publish hundreds of AI-written pages?

Only publish a page when it helps a distinct buyer decision and contains useful, accurate material. Repeating the same content with different keywords is not a content strategy.

Why do results change between tests?

Wording, timing, model behavior, context, and available sources can differ. Record the conditions and interpret repeated observations rather than one response.
