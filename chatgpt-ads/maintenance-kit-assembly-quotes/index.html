Using ChatGPT ads to win maintenance kit RFQs

Promote maintenance kit RFQs through ChatGPT ads if your distribution team can take responsibility for delivering complete, labeled sets. Buying individual components is a different job for the customer. Make the assembly service clear enough that a maintenance planner can recognize what it would take off the storeroom’s hands.

Price the unit the storeroom will issue

Ask for a kit identifier, the approved parts list, the quantity of each item per kit and the number of complete kits required. Request a label example if equipment numbers or revision codes must appear on the package. Those details let purchasing compare quotes on the same delivered unit.

Decide who owns a missing line

Before advertising complete kits, agree with operations on what happens when a component is unavailable. Can the buyer supply it? Will purchasing accept a partial shipment? If the warehouse only picks loose parts by line item, advertise a consolidated parts quote. A single invoice still leaves assembly with the buyer.

A gasket holds up the shutdown kit

Suppose a plant shares its pump service checklist, approved parts spreadsheet and a photo of the bin label. One gasket on the list has a long lead time. If the plant can deliver its own approved gaskets to the distributor, the warehouse can quote assembly with customer-supplied material and identify that dependency. If the plant needs the available parts immediately, sales can offer a loose-parts shipment and let the planner decide who will finish the kits.
