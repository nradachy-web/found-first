ChatGPT advertising costs

A ChatGPT ads budget has two parts. Media is paid to OpenAI, with a minimum daily budget of $25 for accounts billed in US dollars, and the work around it is paid to whoever runs the campaign: management, a landing page and measurement. OpenAI publishes no performance benchmarks yet, so nobody can honestly quote your cost per lead before a test has run.

How OpenAI bills the media

Six rules decide what actually leaves your account. Each one is easy to miss when you plan from the daily figure alone.

  • The objective sets what you pay for

    A Views campaign is billed per 1,000 impressions and a Clicks campaign per valid click. A Conversions campaign optimizes toward an action but is still billed by click or impression. The objective cannot be changed after the campaign is created.

  • A daily budget is an average

    OpenAI averages it over a seven-day period. One day can spend up to twice the amount, and the budget week, Sunday through Saturday, cannot exceed seven times it. At the $25 minimum, OpenAI's two limits work out to up to $50 in a day and no more than $175 in a week.

  • A campaign-total budget is the stricter limit

    It caps spend across the whole campaign. OpenAI advises new advertisers to start with a daily budget so they can watch delivery first. A campaign switched from a total to a daily budget cannot be switched back.

  • You are charged after delivery

    Billing is postpaid. On a self-serve account the saved card is charged when unpaid spend reaches a payment threshold that OpenAI assigns, and again at month end for any balance. Adding a card can also place a temporary authorization hold.

  • Pausing is not instant

    OpenAI says ads may keep appearing for up to 24 hours after a campaign is paused, and that spend in that window is still billable. Reported spend can also trail delivery by about 7 to 8 hours.

  • Weak results are not refundable

    OpenAI says valid spend that has been delivered is generally not eligible for a refund, and that receiving fewer conversions or leads than expected does not change that. Budget what you are prepared to spend on learning.

Why nobody can quote your cost per lead

Asked what good performance looks like, OpenAI's own answer is that ChatGPT Ads has no benchmarks yet across advertisers, industries or campaign types. A cost per lead quoted before your test has run is a guess, including one from us.

The inputs move too. The platform is in beta, and OpenAI says delivery and spend can fluctuate as its systems change. Even the cost targets in Ads Manager are average goals, not guaranteed maximums. Results, OpenAI says, depend on the objective, the creative, the landing page, relevance to the user and the overall setup.

So fix what can be fixed. Decide what a qualified inquiry is worth to you, set the spending limit and let the test produce your own figure. Questions about the budget

What is the minimum budget for ChatGPT ads?+

OpenAI's minimum daily budget is $25 for ad accounts billed in US dollars, and it varies by billing currency. A daily budget is an average over a seven-day period, so a single day can spend up to twice that amount and a budget week no more than seven times it.

Are Found First's fees included in the media spend?+

No. Media is paid to OpenAI. Found First's ad management is part of a monthly program that starts at $2,000 for Foundation. Advertising spend is separate.

Can you predict my cost per lead?+

No. OpenAI says it has no performance benchmarks across advertisers, industries or campaign types yet, so any figure quoted before a test is a guess. We agree a spending limit and a definition of a qualified inquiry, then report what the test actually cost.

Do I pay each time someone converts?+

No. You are billed per 1,000 impressions or per valid click, depending on the campaign. Campaigns that optimize for conversions are still billed by click or impression, not per conversion.

Can I get a refund if the ads do not produce leads?+

OpenAI says valid advertising spend that has already been delivered is generally not eligible for a refund, and that receiving fewer conversions or leads than expected does not by itself change that. Set a budget you are prepared to spend on finding out.

How long should a test run?+

There is no standard length. It depends on how quickly your offer produces enough qualified inquiries to compare with your other channels. Agree the spending limit and the review date before launch, so the test ends with a decision.
