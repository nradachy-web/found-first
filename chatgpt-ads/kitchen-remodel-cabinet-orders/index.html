ChatGPT ads for kitchen remodelers who supply the cabinets

If your remodeling company measures, orders and installs cabinetry as part of a kitchen remodel, use the ChatGPT ad to offer a consultation while those choices are still open. “Cabinet installation” also describes a much later purchase, when boxes are already in the garage and the homeowner needs labor.

Show where the remodeler enters the job

Pair a finished kitchen photo with the measured plan or cabinet elevation that preceded it. Caption the change your team helped the homeowner make, such as shifting a drawer bank so it clears the range. That gives a buyer a concrete reason to bring you in before ordering.

Ask about the order before requesting a design brief

Give the inquiry form a plain question about whether the homeowner is collecting ideas, working from drawings or holding a confirmed order. Someone with drawings might still need the full service. Someone with delivered cabinets should learn immediately whether you accept outside materials and who resolves missing or damaged pieces.

When the cabinet order changes the offer

Picture a remodeler whose project agreement covers cabinet ordering and whose portfolio includes an elevation marked up after a site measure. The owner chooses a ChatGPT ad for kitchen planning and cabinet selection, with that elevation beside the consultation request. If the company adds an install-only crew, a homeowner with boxes on site could instead submit the cabinet schedule and delivery inventory for an installation estimate. The original design consultation still serves buyers who can change their layouts.
