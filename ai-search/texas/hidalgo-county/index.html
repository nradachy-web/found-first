Where will the customer need the expertise?

One location throughout

Several locations within one engagement

Questions worth asking.

Should every Hidalgo County office be described as offering the full service?+

Only if that is accurate. An office can provide valuable access without housing every specialty. Buyers need to distinguish what happens locally from work handled elsewhere before making a commitment based on convenience.

Can a local introduction and a regional specialist appointment support one brand?+

Yes, when the relationship is real and understandable. The company should remain clear about which team performs the work and where the customer must go. A strong shared identity can support that choice without erasing the practical differences.

What if the next provider is an independent business?+

The buyer should understand that the recommendation leads to a separate provider. Referring a customer can be valuable, but the original company should not appear to own or deliver the other business's capability merely because the services complement each other.
