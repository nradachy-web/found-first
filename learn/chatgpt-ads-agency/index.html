ChatGPT advertising with a plan beyond the click.

Found First manages ChatGPT advertising around a specific buyer context, a relevant offer, and a measurable next step. We connect account setup, creative, landing pages, and reporting so each test has a clear purpose.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

Conversation creates context. Someone researching alternatives to a product needs a different proposition from someone ready to book a consultation. An ad can earn attention and still fail if the page asks the visitor to restart their research. The campaign and its destination need to answer the same question.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Confirm fit before allocating spend

Review advertiser eligibility, product restrictions, market availability, and the commercial goal. Define a budget and the evidence needed for a useful evaluation.

02 / Build a coherent test

Match the conversation context to an offer and a dedicated page. Explain the product, show credible evidence, and make the intended action simple.

03 / Judge the actual business outcome

Review reported delivery and cost alongside landing-page behavior and qualified inquiries. Record changes so creative, targeting, and page tests remain interpretable.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • What problem is the buyer actively trying to solve?
  • Why is this offer useful at this point in the conversation?
  • What makes a lead qualified enough for our team to pursue?

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • Eligibility review and account setup support.
  • Campaign hypothesis, context planning, and ad creative.
  • A landing page and conversion measurement plan.
  • Spend and outcome reporting with next-test recommendations.

Before you get started.

Do ads influence ChatGPT's organic answers?

No. Sponsored placements are separate from the answer. Buying an ad does not buy an organic recommendation.

Who pays the ad spend?

Media budget and management fees are discussed separately. Scope, access, and payment arrangements are agreed before launch.

Can every industry advertise?

Availability and policy eligibility vary. We review the current OpenAI rules for your business before proposing a campaign.
