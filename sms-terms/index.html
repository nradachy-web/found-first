SMS Terms

How Found First internal text alerts work.

Effective November 6, 2026

The program.

Found First Internal Alerts is run by Be Found First LLC. These are short operational texts, such as a client work plan being ready for review or an internal job that needs a person to look at it. They contain no marketing.

They go only to Found First staff who have agreed in writing, as one group text. There is no public sign-up, and we don't currently text clients or the public.

Frequency and cost.

Message frequency varies, usually a few a day, and is capped at 8 a day. Msg and data rates may apply.

Stopping and getting help.

Reply STOP to stop receiving alerts. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help, or email jeff@befoundfirst.ai.

Carriers.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy.

Mobile numbers are used only for these alerts. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

Changes.

If we change these terms, we'll update this page and its effective date. If we start texting clients, that will be a separate program that asks for their consent first.
