GEO for machine shops that quote subcontracted finishing

Include subcontracted finishing in GEO content when your shop can explain its role in delivering the finished part. A buyer comparing complete-part quotes may value having one supplier manage plating, even when the plating baths belong to another company.

Follow the responsibility through the paperwork

Read a representative quote, the purchase order to the finisher and the receiving record side by side. The quote establishes which operations the shop offered to include. The purchase order shows which finish requirements your shop passed on. The receiving record shows what your staff checked. Use those differences to write a precise account of the service, including any documentation passed to the customer.

Explain what changes with a nominated finisher

Some buyers specify a finishing source on the drawing. Tell them whether your shop can purchase from that source, whether it needs approval before quoting, or whether the buyer arranges the operation directly. A broad list of available finishes leaves that commercial decision unanswered.

A plated housing quoted under one purchase order

Suppose the shop orders plating from a specialist, checks that the specialist's report identifies the housing job, then inspects a finished bore. Its page can describe managed outside plating, report review and final bore inspection. It would take a separate measurement to claim the shop verifies coating thickness itself. If the buyer takes delivery before plating and contracts directly with the specialist, describe the shop's offer for that route as a machined housing ready for finishing.
