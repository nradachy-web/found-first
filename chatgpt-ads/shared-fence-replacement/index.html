ChatGPT ads for fence contractors quoting shared fence lines

Offer an estimate for a defined fence segment when a replacement involves adjoining yards. Your estimator needs to know which run the caller wants replaced, who can arrange access and whether the neighbors are discussing the same project.

Draw the run before quoting the yard

Put a simple plan view beside a completed-fence photo and label the back run, side return and gate. Invite the caller to mark the requested segment on their own sketch. Specify whether your usual proposal includes removing old panels, hauling debris and reconnecting a gate. Those details can matter more to the discussion than another photo of a fence style.

Give an early inquiry somewhere to go

A caller may have agreement on the fence style while access through the adjoining yard is still uncertain. Explain what you can prepare from the caller’s side and what requires a joint site meeting. Keep the request focused on estimating work your crew could perform. Boundary disputes have to be settled elsewhere.

The rear run is agreed, but the side gate is new

Imagine a fencing owner reviewing a marked lot sketch, a rear-yard photo and a sample removal scope. Both households want the rear run replaced and can arrange access, so the owner offers a joint measurement for that segment. If only the caller wants a new side gate, draw it as a separate option on the proposal. The neighbor can then review the shared work without being asked to approve an unrelated addition.
