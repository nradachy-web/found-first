ChatGPT advertising · Catalog campaigns and feed readiness

ChatGPT ads for flooring sold by the carton

For flooring sold in full cartons, make the carton the purchase unit in your ChatGPT ads catalog offer. A per-square-foot figure helps contractors compare materials, but the advertised amount and quantity should still describe an order the store will accept.

Read quantity the way the warehouse does

Compare the manufacturer's carton label, the estimator on the product page and the unit on a sales order. The estimator may accept an area and round it up to whole cartons. If the merchant's catalog pairs a square-foot price with a carton title, a buyer cannot tell what the displayed amount buys. Keep coverage and selling unit explicit wherever the store presents the offer.

Check each format within a range

Different plank widths or tile formats in the same design can have different carton coverage. A conversion copied from a sibling item can produce a believable but incorrect carton total. Get the coverage for the specific product from its packaging or packing specification before briefing the team preparing the feed.

One mosaic sheet versus a sealed carton

Imagine a tile showroom whose supplier list gives a per-sheet amount for a mosaic, while its pick ticket requires a sealed carton. The online cart increments by cartons. The buyer requests a carton offer that states how many sheets it contains and checks the cart total against the carton sales record. If the showroom also breaks cartons and accepts individual sheet orders, a per-sheet offer could work for that separately orderable item.
