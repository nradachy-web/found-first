How ChatGPT ads work

ChatGPT ads are labeled paid placements kept separate from answers. They may appear for eligible Free and Go users; OpenAI excludes accounts identified as under 18. Availability and formats can change, and buying ads does not influence organic answers.

ChatGPT ads: official guidance reviewed October 21, 2026
Who sees themEligible Free and Go users. Accounts identified as under 18 are excluded; eligible logged-out experiences can also contain ads.
Where they appearClearly labeled placements separate from the answer. Formats and availability can change.
How you payAvailable objectives can bill per valid click or per thousand impressions. A conversion objective does not mean payment per conversion.
Budget controlsMinimums depend on billing currency. Daily budgets are average targets; campaign-total budgets control the total.
ReportingAvailable account metrics and separately verified inquiries. Unknown attribution stays unknown.
Do ads change answers?No. Paid placement does not buy an organic recommendation.

Check readiness before launch.

  • Confirm legal-entity country, account access and policy eligibility.
  • Prepare approved creative and a working destination.
  • Confirm billing, media budget, schedule and launch authorization.
  • Agree how to evaluate qualified inquiries.

Current account settings control what is available. A connected reporting account is not a live-campaign acceptance check.

For anyone researching AI search and answer engines.

How answer engine optimization works

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of making a business easy for AI systems to find, understand, verify, and cite when they answer a question. It builds on ordinary SEO: crawlable pages, useful content, consistent business facts, and credible third-party mentions. There is no secret file. Google states that no new machine-readable files, AI text files, markup, or special structured data are needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that inclusion is never guaranteed.

access

Can the system reach you?

Check content access, indexing and useful page content. Retrieval can vary by provider; an accessible page is eligible to be considered, not guaranteed to be used.

clarity

Do you answer the question?

Specific answers, useful comparisons, and first-hand evidence give a system clearer material to retrieve and cite.

consistency

Do your facts match?

Name, services, locations, hours, and prices should agree everywhere they appear. Conflicting facts make your business harder to identify accurately.

corroboration

Does anyone else say so?

Accurate profiles, legitimate reviews and relevant references help buyers verify claims. Source studies can show which pages are retrieved or cited in the tested answers.

What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?+

Answer engine optimization is the work of making a business easy for AI systems such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini to find, understand, verify, and cite when they answer a question. It builds on ordinary SEO: crawlable pages, direct answers to real questions, consistent business facts, and credible third-party mentions. No AI system guarantees inclusion.

How do ChatGPT ads work?+

ChatGPT ads are labeled paid placements kept separate from answers. They may appear for eligible Free and Go users; OpenAI excludes accounts identified as under 18. Availability and formats can change, and buying ads does not influence organic answers.

Can my business advertise on ChatGPT?+

Eligibility depends on your legal entity’s country, account access, products and current OpenAI policies. We check the current availability list and account requirements before proposing launch. Signup alone is not confirmation that an account can advertise.

How much do ChatGPT ads cost?+

Our proposed monthly service fees are $2,000 for Foundation, $3,500 for Growth and $5,000 for Expansion. Advertising spend is separate. Final allowances, billing periods and terms are confirmed in the written scope. Confirm the ad account’s currency and current minimums: a daily budget is an average delivery target, while a campaign-total budget controls total spend.

Do ChatGPT ads change what ChatGPT says about my business?+

No. OpenAI runs ads on separate systems from the chat model, and advertisers cannot shape, rank, or alter ChatGPT’s responses. Buying an ad does not earn a place in an organic answer. We manage the work together and measure organic visibility and paid performance separately.

How is AEO different from SEO?+

SEO aims to rank a page for a keyword and win a click. AEO aims to have your business named, and your facts used, inside a generated answer. The tactics overlap heavily, and Google says no special files, markup, or schema are required for its AI features. The difference is what you optimize: the entity and the claim, not just the page.

How long does AEO take?+

Timing depends on the work, your starting point and platform behavior. We agree a delivery and review cadence, retain evidence and report observed changes. No assistant promises indexing, inclusion or recommendations on our schedule.

Should we stop running Google Ads or SEO?+

No. AEO builds on your SEO, and ChatGPT ads are one more place to meet demand, not a replacement for search. Nick’s agency runs Google Ads every day, so we will tell you plainly where each dollar does the most good.

What is in the free AI visibility check?+

Share your business, service area and website if you have one. We review an initial set of customer questions and reply with observations and suggested priorities. A wider source study, ongoing testing and implementation belong in an agreed scope. There is no cost or obligation for the initial check.
