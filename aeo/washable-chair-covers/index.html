Washable upholstery claims in AEO for chair brands

Use AEO for upholstery care when the care requirement could change which chair or fabric a shopper chooses. If a buyer needs a washable seat, the product answer should name the upholstery option, the removable part and its permitted cleaning method.

Read the care label for the finished cover

A fabric swatch describes the textile. The sewn cover can also include backing, piping or a lining, so the swatch alone cannot settle whether the finished cover can go in a washing machine. Check the supplier's instructions for the cover as sold. A zipper gives access to the insert but says little about washing.

Make the scope visible at fabric selection

An answer about a seat cushion can easily read as a claim about the whole chair. Specify how the fixed arms and back are cleaned if that affects the choice. Give the same level of detail for any alternative fabric you propose to a shopper whose priority is regular washing.

One chair frame with two care labels

Suppose a chair's linen seat cover has a zipper and a spot-clean instruction. The performance twill cover on the same frame has a machine-wash label, while the upholstered arms are fixed on both versions. Recommend the twill option to a shopper who needs to wash the seat cover. If they need to launder every upholstered surface, neither version meets their requirement. A fully slipcovered chair is a candidate only once its complete cover has matching care instructions.
