ChatGPT advertising · Landing page decisions after the ad

Should ChatGPT ads open a booking calendar or a contact form?

Send ChatGPT ad traffic to a calendar when choosing a time secures the service described in the ad. Use a short inquiry form when someone must review the job before they can commit staff, space or equipment.

Read the confirmation the customer gets

Make a test booking and compare the receipt with the staff schedule. Does the customer own that time, or does the desk still have to call and approve it? If review is routine, label the action “Request a time” and explain how the customer will receive a decision. A calendar full of selectable times can otherwise imply a commitment the business has yet to make.

Give each offer its own route

A business can support both paths. Link an ad for a defined appointment directly to its appointment type. Send an ad for custom work to a form that asks what needs review. A general contact page adds a needless sorting step when the ad has already identified the service.

A studio offering headshots and team portraits

Suppose a portrait studio compares its headshot ad with its session menu and room calendar. A solo headshot fits a standard slot, so the ad opens that session with the available times visible. For an on-site team portrait, the producer needs the head count and location to assign a crew. That ad opens a brief inquiry, and the producer offers dates after checking those details.
