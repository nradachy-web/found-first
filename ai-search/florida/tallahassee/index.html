Questions worth asking.

Should a Tallahassee business position itself primarily for students?+

Only when that audience fits the actual offer. A campus provides local context, but the business may serve households, professionals or organizations whose needs are different. The customer relationship should drive the decision.

Can one company serve temporary and continuing customers?+

Yes, if the services are designed for both. The distinctions should be understandable so a short-term buyer does not assume a continuing obligation, or a recurring customer assume the service is merely temporary.

Is AI visibility relevant to a business with a stable local customer base?+

It can be when new customers struggle to recognize the same fit that existing customers already understand. The useful question is whether discovery accurately communicates the service relationship, not whether the company is new to the area.
