ChatGPT ads for a Shopify refund analytics integration

Name Shopify in an analytics ad when the vendor can show exactly what its connector brings into the report. Choose the reporting question first, then trace a sample refund from source record to displayed result.

Follow the refund past the import screen

A connection badge tells a buyer little about whether yesterday's refund changes yesterday's report or the original sale date. Publish a small worked reconciliation with the source dates, the imported fields and the report's date rule. Label any manual adjustment. A buyer deciding whether to retire a spreadsheet needs to know what reconciliation work survives the switch.

Use the report's grain to set the offer

A refund total by order may support a finance reconciliation. It cannot by itself explain returns by item. Have the marketing team review the connector's field dictionary with the analyst who owns the report before commissioning an ad for merchandising teams.

One order, two different shirts

Suppose an analytics vendor's sample export contains an order for two shirts and a later refund for one of them. Its connector log records the order identifier, refund date and total, and its report groups totals by refund date. The vendor sponsors a preview of daily refund reconciliation and includes that example. If a subsequent connector test also ties the refund to the returned item, the team can prepare an item-level offer after checking the item report against the sample. Until then it keeps product return analysis outside the offer.
