ChatGPT advertising · Offer qualification and buying readiness

ChatGPT ads for auction marketplaces that approve bidders

Test a sale-specific ChatGPT ad while a new bidder still has time to qualify and place a bid. Once the approval window closes, even a visitor who wants the exact lot has little reason to register for that sale.

Work back from the last usable bid

Ask the marketplace operator for the sale terms, the bidder application cutoff and the hours someone actually reviews applications. A catalog can remain public after bidder registration closes. Use the last point at which a newcomer can reasonably get approved as the boundary for the offer.

Give a registration its sale context

Agree on what buying readiness means before buying media. For this offer, an approved newcomer who bids on the advertised sale is a stronger signal than an existing bidder opening another catalog. ChatGPT conversion reporting depends on the event configured. A registration event alone cannot tell the auction director which of those things happened.

The catalog stays open after the bidder desk closes

Suppose a rare-book marketplace publishes a Sunday auction catalog. Its terms require advance approval, and the desk finishes reviewing new applicants on Friday. Early in the week, the paid offer can invite collectors to apply to bid on the maps in that sale. By Saturday, switch to the next map auction if consignments and registration are confirmed. If the marketplace can only offer a notice when another sale is scheduled, describe that as an alert and judge it separately.
