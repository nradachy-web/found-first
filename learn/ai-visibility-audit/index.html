An AI visibility audit for the question your website cannot answer.

An AI visibility audit examines how your business appears in AI answers. For a brand with serious ambition, it is a chance to confront a simple question: are you part of the conversation, or watching it happen without you?

Your homepage is your version of the story

Your homepage gives you the whole stage. The market does not.

You know the care, experience and effort behind your business. Your website has space to explain all of it. Being proud of your brand and knowing where it stands are different things. You deserve to know both.

Confidence is better when you can back it

A strong reputation should make you demanding. If your company belongs in more conversations, you should know where it stands today.

Found First takes that question seriously. Your brand has a position in the market whether you are happy with it or not. A clearer view gives you something more useful than another comfortable assumption.

For the owner who would rather know

This is for the founder ready to challenge the picture in their head. The operator who would rather see an uncomfortable truth than keep admiring the homepage.

You do not need another pep talk about how fast everything is changing. You need a clearer view of your own business. Bring the questions you actually want answered, including the ones nobody has given you a straight answer to.

A few things worth knowing

What is an AI visibility audit?+

It is an assessment of how your business appears in AI answers. The scope of a Found First engagement is discussed privately.

Does an audit itself improve visibility?+

No. An assessment shows a position; it does not change it. Found First does not guarantee mentions, rankings or leads.

Can it tell me everything every customer sees?+

No. An audit is a view of the answers examined, not a record of every person's experience. We keep that distinction clear.
