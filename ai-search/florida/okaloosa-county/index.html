Questions worth asking.

Should a business use an airport name in its positioning?+

Only when that reference helps explain the actual service. It can be useful for orientation, but it should not imply an airport location, formal affiliation or coverage that the business does not have.

Is AI search relevant when customers book well before visiting?+

It can be part of their initial comparison. A buyer planning ahead may need to understand suitability before they can inspect the provider or location directly. The public account should make the next decision clearer.

What if local customers and visiting customers need different things?+

The company can serve both while being honest about the difference. The underlying business identity should stay consistent, and each customer should understand the kind of arrangement the provider is equipped to support.
