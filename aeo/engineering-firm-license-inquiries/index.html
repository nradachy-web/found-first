AEO for engineering firms answering licensing questions

When an owner asks “Are you licensed for our project?”, publish only the credential your engineering firm can document. A useful AEO answer identifies the holder, the issuing body and the jurisdiction, then tells the owner how to confirm who could take the proposed assignment.

Keep the credential attached to its holder

Give the editor a current licensing record and the approved staff profile it supports. Check whose name appears on the record before choosing between an individual biography and a firm qualifications page. A person's credential supports a statement about that person. A separate claim about the business needs evidence bearing the business's name.

Leave the staffing decision with the project team

A credential listing answers one part of a shortlist question. To handle the rest, invite the owner to identify the project location and discipline so a principal can confirm the proposed team. A staff member's past involvement in a similar project does not establish their availability for the next one.

A qualification request names the business

Suppose a prospect asks for the firm's credential, but the marketing folder contains an engineer's current individual record and an old proposal listing several offices. Publish the individual's credential in the biography and route the firm-level question to the principal for verification. If the principal supplies a current record naming the business in the requested jurisdiction, the qualifications page can identify that record separately. The office list establishes neither credential.
