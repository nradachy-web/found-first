Questions worth asking.

Should industrial businesses use broad cargo terminology?+

It can provide useful context when accurate, but it should not hide important limits. A provider may be well suited to one material, asset or operating condition and unsuitable for another. The actual capability should remain the basis for consideration.

Can a local provider compete for customers based elsewhere?+

Yes, when location and specialized capability make it relevant to their work. Those buyers may know the industrial need but have limited knowledge of Brownsville providers. A clear service identity can help them decide who merits further evaluation.

What if our best work is difficult to describe briefly?+

That is a meaningful discovery challenge. The answer is not to reduce the business to an empty superlative. Buyers need an intelligible account that preserves the distinction they will examine more closely once they make contact.
