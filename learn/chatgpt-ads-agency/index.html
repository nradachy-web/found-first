The channel is new. Your offer still has to sell.

A customer comparing options needs a reason to consider you. The product, service or expertise behind the ad must be clear enough to earn that attention, and the destination must give the person somewhere useful to go. A clever headline cannot carry an unclear business proposition.

Found First connects those decisions. We work on the advertising message and creative, manage campaigns, and consider how the landing experience supports the next step. The aim is a coherent introduction to your business, whether that next step is an inquiry, a booking, a product purchase or a sales conversation.

We also keep the channel in context. An established acquisition program should not be overturned simply because another platform is available. ChatGPT advertising needs a defined role, a suitable offer and a commercial reason to receive your attention.

Who should consider managed ChatGPT ads?

The strongest conversation starts with a real customer need and an offer you can explain.

  • A service business with a clear buying decisionYou know the work you want more of and can explain who is a good fit, where you serve them and what happens after an inquiry.
  • An ecommerce or online-service brandYour product or service solves a recognizable problem, and the customer has a clear route from interest to purchase.
  • A growth team exploring another channelYou want an experienced partner to own campaign decisions while evaluating the opportunity alongside existing marketing.

Fit also depends on current advertiser eligibility, policies and audience availability. We discuss those constraints against your actual business.

A click is the start of the commercial question

Traffic has different value for different businesses. A specialist contractor needs relevant jobs in its service area. An online store needs purchases that make commercial sense. A B2B team needs inquiries that can become credible opportunities. Those are the outcomes that give campaign numbers meaning.

We keep impressions, clicks, reported conversions and actual customer outcomes distinct. More activity can be encouraging without proving that more valuable business followed. The right measurement conversation starts with what success means for your company and what evidence is available to judge it.

Before you hire a ChatGPT ads agency

Can you make our ad appear whenever someone asks a specific question?+

No. OpenAI selects delivery using multiple signals. Advertiser context hints are not exact-match keywords and do not guarantee a particular conversation. We manage the advertising work without promising control the platform does not provide.

Will this reach everyone who uses ChatGPT?+

No. OpenAI says ads may appear for eligible Free and Go users; its paid Plus, Pro and business plans do not show ads. The potential audience must be considered alongside your buyer and current platform availability.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?+

No. Campaign management is an accountable service, not a promise of a particular result. We discuss the business objective and how progress should be assessed before presenting activity as success.

Do we need to replace our other advertising?+

Not automatically. ChatGPT ads can be evaluated alongside your existing channels. The right decision depends on your customers, offer, current performance and appetite for a developing advertising environment.
