Questions worth asking.

Should every Mount Pleasant business target students?+

No. A university presence does not define every company’s intended customer. Businesses should choose audiences based on actual services and commercial goals. Residents, organizations, visitors and students can have different needs even when they share the same area.

What if customers need help before they know the right service name?+

The company should be understandable through the problem it addresses. A new customer may describe symptoms or a desired outcome rather than professional terminology. The commercial question is whether that need matches the business’s actual capability.

Can AI discovery support a long-term local relationship?+

It can help a prospective customer understand the company before the relationship begins. The experience after contact determines whether that relationship continues. A strong first impression should set a suitable expectation rather than promise loyalty on the customer’s behalf.
