Field notes/Choosing an AEO agency

Choose an AEO agency that can explain the work and the evidence.

The right agency should make the buying decision easier to understand.

Choosing an answer engine optimization agency means evaluating how it will improve your business's organic AI discovery and public representation. The proposal should explain the actual service, its connection to your website and the evidence used to judge progress. Found First combines marketing and website expertise around that job. A persuasive pitch should make the work clearer without promising control over independent AI answers.

Ask what business problem the service addresses

AEO, GEO and AI visibility are overlapping terms, but an acronym does not explain the work. The agency should connect the service to a real issue: an unclear offer, inaccurate representation, weak public product information or another discovery gap that matters to prospective customers.

Found First provides organic AI search visibility and supporting website/content improvements. We also manage ChatGPT advertising as a separate paid service. That distinction matters when comparing proposals. An agency should not suggest that buying advertising purchases an organic recommendation, or sell a large content program without explaining why the pages help a buyer make a different decision.

Examine what the proof actually proves

A screenshot can show that a business appeared in an answer at a point in time. It does not establish broad market share, a qualified inquiry or a sale. A before-and-after claim also needs a basis for attributing the change to the agency's work rather than merely noting that it happened later.

Found First's published diagnostic research should be read as a dated, scoped observation. It is not a client performance guarantee. When evaluating any agency, ask whether the evidence belongs to the service being sold and whether its limitations are visible. Results from another channel or another organization should not be quietly repackaged as proof of AEO performance.

Look for website judgment rather than a technical shortcut

Organic AI discovery depends on a public business explanation people can use. An agency should understand the offer, product or service boundaries and the destination a prospect reaches. A technical checklist alone cannot make an unclear business proposition compelling.

Google says ordinary search foundations apply to its AI features and no special AI file or schema is required. That is a reason to question universal shortcut claims. Found First connects organic visibility with practical website and content improvements. The proposed scope should explain why a change serves the business, while private implementation methods can remain private.

The important buying question is how those capabilities address your situation, not simply whether the founders can describe a fast-changing category confidently.

It does not promise that both founders attend or that a finished audit is delivered free. If there is a fit, the proposed scope and commercial terms are discussed privately before you commit.

Questions before choosing the engagement

Should an AEO agency guarantee recommendations or rankings?+

No agency should promise control it does not have over independent platform answers. A service can be accountable for agreed work and evidence without guaranteeing that a particular business appears in every relevant response.

How do I compare proposals without seeing the private methods?+

Compare the business problem, service scope, responsibilities, evidence and commercial terms. You should understand what you are hiring the agency to do even when its internal processes and tools are not public.

Do we need separate AEO and GEO agencies?+

Not simply because the labels differ. Ask what each proposed engagement actually does and whether it addresses a distinct need. Found First treats those terms within the broader organic AI visibility service.

Is Found First the right choice for every company?+

No. Fit depends on the offer, website, priorities and evidence available. The consultation should establish whether our organic visibility, website or paid advertising services address your actual need rather than assume every company requires the same program.
