AEO for firms with a reputation worth knowing.

Your expertise should carry weight before the first introduction. Found First helps professional firms compete for attention in AI search with a presence that respects the reputation behind the name.

You earned the reputation

Years of judgment, difficult work and relationships sit behind a respected firm. A prospective client may encounter none of that before deciding who deserves a closer look.

Found First is for firms that expect their digital presence to reflect the caliber of the people inside. The expertise is already there. Give the name a stronger presence.

Authority can have a backbone

Professional does not have to mean forgettable. A firm can speak with conviction, have a distinct point of view and respect the standards of its profession.

The right clients should understand why your firm merits attention. A reputation is too valuable to flatten into interchangeable marketing. We want the presence to feel as considered as the work.

Let the name carry weight

For a specialist firm, being considered by the right people matters. A thoughtful introduction can open a door that another forgettable impression leaves closed.

You did not build that reputation overnight. It should have a recognizable voice and a presence worthy of the people who earned it. Found First brings that expectation to the work.

Questions from professional firms

Does this apply to a specialist practice?+

Yes. We can discuss fit around the firm's focus and ambitions. A narrow specialty can be central to the business you want prospective clients to understand.

Who remains responsible for professional claims?+

Your firm remains responsible for approving claims and meeting the standards of its profession. Found First does not give legal or compliance advice.

Will you promise new clients or professional outcomes?+

No. We do not guarantee rankings, AI mentions or new clients, and we do not put outcome promises into a firm's content.
