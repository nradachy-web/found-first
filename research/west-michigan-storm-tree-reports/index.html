How often do West Michigan thunderstorm wind reports mention trees?

In Kent, Ionia and Montcalm counties, 48 of 64 NOAA thunderstorm wind event reports mentioned a tree or trees during 2021 through 2025.

Found First Research · November 25, 2026

48 of 64 reports mentioned trees.

A storm report can record a measured wind gust, damage seen on a road, or both. We read the event-level records for three West Michigan counties and counted how often the words “tree” or “trees” appeared. They occurred in 75% of the thunderstorm wind reports in the five-year window.

One report described tree limbs down along M-91 between Greenville and Turk Lake. Someone searching for help after a storm may describe exactly that obstruction. A page promising general “tree care” leaves them to work out whether the crew handles storm cleanup. Event 973855 in NOAA’s 2021 detail file.

These are counts of reports. A single storm can create several local event records. An event may affect many trees, and fallen trees may never reach a weather observer. County totals alone cannot tell a contractor where the next call will come from.

What belongs on a storm cleanup page?

A homeowner with a tree across a driveway starts with a different question from someone seeking routine pruning. A service page can state the area the crew covers, what it can remove and how a caller verifies current availability.

AEO for tree service companies. For a commercial example selected by Found First, see Ole Man RB’s storm damage cleanup page and its homeowner guide on who to call when a tree falls on a house or driveway. The counts come entirely from NOAA reports.

How to reproduce the count

We used the November 19, 2026 revisions of NOAA’s five annual Storm Events detail files. We selected Michigan county records for Ionia, Kent and Montcalm where the event type was Thunderstorm Wind. We counted unique event IDs and searched each event narrative for the whole word “tree” or “trees,” ignoring capitalization. A report mentioning only limbs or branches scored as no match. The broader episode narrative was excluded to avoid assigning a regional description to every local event. The sample included 4 empty event narratives.

The files cover significant events that reached the National Weather Service. This measure describes the language of those records. It does not estimate fallen trees, insurance claims, service calls, jobs or AI citations. NOAA Storm Events Database · Field definitions.
