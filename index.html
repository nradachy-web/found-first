For anyone researching how to advertise on ChatGPT.

How ChatGPT ads work

ChatGPT ads are sponsored placements that appear below a ChatGPT response when the conversation is relevant to the advertiser. They are clearly labeled, visually separated from the answer, and shown only to adults on the Free and Go plans. Advertisers buy them through OpenAI’s self-serve Ads Manager Beta, pay per view or per valid click, and never see users’ chats. Ads do not change what ChatGPT says.

ChatGPT ads at a glance, from OpenAI’s Help Center as of September 2026
Who sees themAdults on the Free and Go plans. No ads on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Edu accounts, in Temporary Chats, or for accounts identified as under 18.
Where they appearBelow the end of a response, labeled as sponsored and visually separated from the answer. One or more ad units can appear when there is a relevant match.
What an ad containsAdvertiser name, logo, a title, a description, a landing page, and an image asset.
How ads are matchedThe context and intent of the current conversation, plus the ad’s landing page, title, copy, and advertiser context hints. Context hints are not exact-match keywords and do not guarantee delivery.
How you payViews (per 1,000 impressions), Clicks (per valid click), or Conversions (billed per valid click or per 1,000 impressions). A relevance-weighted second-price auction picks the ad. OpenAI suggests starting CPC bids at $3 to $5 USD.
Minimum budget$25 USD per day, varying by billing currency. Choose a daily budget or a campaign-total budget.
TargetingCountry, and for most campaigns states, cities, markets, and postal codes where available. Platform targeting across iOS, Android, and desktop web. Custom audiences need at least 25,000 matched users for inclusion.
ReportingImpressions, clicks, spend, click-through rate, average CPC and CPM, and conversions. UTM parameters persist on ad clicks for your own analytics.
What is off limitsNo ads near personal health, mental health, or political conversations. No political advertising. Regulated verticals such as health and finance are approved case by case.
Do ads change answers?No. Ads run on separate systems, and advertisers cannot shape, rank, or alter ChatGPT’s responses. Seeing an ad is not an endorsement.

Can your business advertise on ChatGPT?

  • Your legal entity is based in a country where Ads Manager is available. As of September 2026 that includes the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, most of the EU, Japan, Korea, India, Brazil, Mexico, and others.
  • Your products or services are allowed under OpenAI’s ad policies. Health, finance, and legal advertisers are reviewed case by case. Political ads are not allowed.
  • You have a landing page worth sending people to, or you want one built.
  • You can commit a test budget and a few weeks to learn. OpenAI says there are no cross-advertiser benchmarks yet.

New self-serve accounts may be limited to their home country until identity verification and a spend threshold are met.

  1. Feb 9, 2026Test begins for US users on the Free and Go plans.
  2. Feb to Apr 2026Focused pilot with a limited set of advertisers.

For anyone researching AI search and answer engines.

How answer engine optimization works

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of making a business easy for AI systems to find, understand, verify, and cite when they answer a question. It builds on ordinary SEO: crawlable pages, useful content, consistent business facts, and credible third-party mentions. There is no secret file. Google states that no new machine-readable files, AI text files, markup, or special structured data are needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that inclusion is never guaranteed.

access

Can the system reach you?

Blocked crawlers, slow pages, and thin sites end the conversation early. Answer engines only quote what they can fetch and index.

clarity

Do you answer the question?

Specific answers, useful comparisons, and first-hand evidence give a system clearer material to retrieve and cite.

consistency

Do your facts match?

Name, services, locations, hours, and prices should agree everywhere they appear. Conflicting facts make your business harder to identify accurately.

corroboration

Does anyone else say so?

Reviews, directories, press, and partners carry weight. Answer engines lean on third-party sources when deciding who to recommend.

What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?+

Answer engine optimization is the work of making a business easy for AI systems such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini to find, understand, verify, and cite when they answer a question. It builds on ordinary SEO: crawlable pages, direct answers to real questions, consistent business facts, and credible third-party mentions. No AI system guarantees inclusion.

How do ChatGPT ads work?+

Ads in ChatGPT are sponsored placements shown below a response when the conversation is relevant. Each ad includes the advertiser name, logo, a title, a description, a landing page, and an image. OpenAI matches ads using the context of the current conversation and the advertiser’s landing page, copy, and context hints. Ads are labeled, kept separate from the answer, and shown only to adults on the Free and Go plans.

Can my business advertise on ChatGPT?+

Probably, if your legal entity is based in a country where OpenAI’s Ads Manager is available, which as of September 2026 includes the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, most of the EU, Japan, Korea, India, Brazil, and Mexico, and your products are allowed under OpenAI’s ad policies. Health, financial, and legal advertisers are reviewed case by case. Political ads are not allowed. We confirm eligibility before any work starts.

How much do ChatGPT ads cost?+

You set the budget. OpenAI’s minimum daily budget is 25 US dollars, campaigns can be billed per 1,000 views or per valid click, and OpenAI suggests starting cost-per-click bids around 3 to 5 dollars. Ads are chosen in a relevance-weighted second-price auction, so relevance and landing-page quality matter alongside your bid. Our management fee is quoted separately after the free visibility check.

Do ChatGPT ads change what ChatGPT says about my business?+

No. OpenAI runs ads on separate systems from the chat model, and advertisers cannot shape, rank, or alter ChatGPT’s responses. Buying an ad does not earn a place in an organic answer. That is why we treat AEO and ChatGPT ads as two different services.

How is AEO different from SEO?+

SEO aims to rank a page for a keyword and win a click. AEO aims to have your business named, and your facts used, inside a generated answer. The tactics overlap heavily, and Google says no special files, markup, or schema are required for its AI features. The difference is what you optimize: the entity and the claim, not just the page.

How long does AEO take?+

Weeks for technical fixes and business fact cleanup, months for earned mentions and citations. AI systems recrawl and re-evaluate on their own schedules and none of them promise inclusion. We report monthly on which customer questions name your business so you can see movement, or the lack of it, in plain numbers.

Should we stop running Google Ads or SEO?+

No. AEO builds on your SEO, and ChatGPT ads are one more place to meet demand, not a replacement for search. Nick’s agency runs Google Ads every day, so we will tell you plainly where each dollar does the most good.
