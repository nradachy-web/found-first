ChatGPT ads or Google Ads? Start with the buying moment.

Google Search ads reach people expressing intent through a search query. ChatGPT ads can reach people within a relevant conversation. Evaluate each channel against your buyer, offer, available measurement, and evidence from a controlled test.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

A promising new channel is not a reason to stop a working one. Search can capture immediate demand while conversational research may reveal a more nuanced need. The question is whether each channel produces enough useful opportunities at an acceptable cost for your business.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Match the message to context

An urgent search and an exploratory comparison need different pages and calls to action. Identify the decision the person is making before adapting creative.

02 / Establish a fair comparison

Use consistent lead-quality definitions and account for different sales cycles. Keep test dates, offers, and important changes documented so the comparison has context.

03 / Allocate using actual evidence

Review qualified outcomes alongside cost and volume. Expand where evidence supports it and investigate gaps before moving budget solely because a platform is new.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • Is this buyer researching, comparing, or ready to act?
  • Does the landing page continue the conversation?
  • Are we comparing the same quality of inquiry across both channels?

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • A channel-fit assessment.
  • Campaign and landing-page hypotheses.
  • Shared qualification rules across sources.
  • A practical comparison of observed test outcomes.

Before you get started.

Should we stop Google Ads to try ChatGPT ads?

Review existing performance and the available test budget first. Preserve proven demand capture while evaluating a new channel.

Can the same landing page serve both?

Sometimes. It must answer the intent behind each visit. More specific conversations may need additional detail or a different offer.
