ChatGPT advertising · Creative and message testing

Should ChatGPT ads lead with your branded service name?

Lead with a branded service name if buyers recognize it and use it to ask for a specific piece of work. When the name needs translating, lead with the deliverable and keep the signature name close enough that buyers can recognize it on the booking page.

Check whose vocabulary it is

Look at how prospects describe the service before anyone sends them a brochure. A phrase repeated in signed proposals may simply be the label your staff entered. A phrase used unprompted in an inquiry is better evidence that some buyers know it. Neither tells you that every new prospect shares that familiarity.

Swap the order, preserve the explanation

A fair comparison gives both versions the same plain-language description. Put the name first in one headline and the deliverable first in the other. Keep the sample, terms and contact action consistent so that extra information in one version does not decide the test.

A color consultant’s Room Pairing service

Suppose a design firm sells “Room Pairing.” Its sample palette shows coordinated paint colors for adjoining rooms, but the name on its own could suggest a furniture layout. The marketing team can compare “Room Pairing for adjoining paint colors” with “Coordinated paint colors for adjoining rooms,” retaining the service name beside the same sample palette. If incoming referrals already ask for Room Pairing by name, retain a name-led version for that referral offer and evaluate new-business copy separately.
