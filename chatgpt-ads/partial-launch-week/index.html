How to compare a partial ChatGPT ads launch week

Judge a partial launch week against the hours the ChatGPT ads campaign was scheduled to run. Comparing its total with a full prior week can create a shortfall before the campaign has had a comparable opportunity to deliver.

Use the scheduled opening, even if the first impression came later

The project kickoff, scheduled start and first recorded impression answer different questions. Use the agreed start and any documented holds to mark the hours the campaign could reasonably have run. Starting the clock at the first recorded impression quietly removes any opening hours with no delivery, which may be precisely the problem you need to see.

Match the trading pattern

Compare like weekdays and operating hours where the records allow it. Extending an opening weekend's pace across a working week assumes demand is similar on those days. When no comparable period exists, report what happened during the launch window and defer the full-week judgment.

A rehearsal space launches on Friday afternoon

Consider a rehearsal studio whose new campaign began on Friday after its booking calendar reopened. The owner has the launch approval, the calendar reopening time and a prior campaign report covering a full week. To decide whether the launch needs attention, he asks for the prior Friday afternoon and weekend on their own. If delivery looks comparable over those hours, he waits for a complete trading week. If the agreed Friday start passed and all the impressions arrived on Sunday, he investigates the quiet opening.
