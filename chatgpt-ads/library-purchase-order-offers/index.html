ChatGPT advertising · Offer qualification and buying readiness

ChatGPT ads for library services bought by purchase order

A purchase-order offer is worth testing in ChatGPT ads when a librarian can take the quoted service through the institution's actual purchasing process. The person who defines a collection project can be an excellent prospect even if someone else must authorize the order.

Distinguish a project estimate from an order

Ask the provider to walk through a blank quote, its vendor registration packet and its order acceptance terms. Identify what the department can specify itself, what purchasing will need and whether the provider can wait for an issued order before work begins. Use that sequence to choose the action worth advertising.

Give the internal sponsor something to carry forward

A buyer preparing a request may need separate lines for collection, scanning and file delivery, each with assumptions another department can check. The advertised deliverable can be a procurement-ready estimate if the provider can actually produce it. Conversational relevance is one possible ad delivery signal. It establishes neither spending authority nor approved vendor status.

A local history room seeks approval to digitize its negatives

Imagine a library's requisition form requires a supplier record and a quote tied to the collection inventory. The scanning provider accepts purchase orders and can complete vendor registration, so the paid offer invites a librarian to request an itemized project estimate. If this library buys exclusively through an existing supplier agreement and the provider has no permitted route under it, another estimate cannot unblock the order. Qualify that condition before assigning staff to count and grade the negatives.
