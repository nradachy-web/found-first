How contract manufacturers can answer first article questions with AEO

If buyers ask whether you “provide first articles,” use AEO to explain exactly what they can request with a quote. They may need a physical part, a documented inspection against their drawing, or a formal opportunity to accept the first article before the balance ships.

Put a deliverable behind the phrase

Ask quality for a blank inspection report cleared for public use. Describe which fields the report contains and whether buyers can submit their own required form for review. A buyer can then compare your standard deliverable with its receiving requirements without needing access to another customer's report.

Separate the report from the release decision

Identify where the buyer should specify that acceptance is a condition of shipment. An inspection record accompanying an order and an approval required before dispatch involve different scheduling decisions. The quote should make clear what will be submitted, who reviews it and which shipment depends on the response.

A report in the box or a review before dispatch

A hypothetical bracket supplier receives a marked drawing and a blank customer inspection form. The purchase specification asks for the completed form with the first delivery, so sales can quote that documentation after quality reviews it. If the specification instead requires written acceptance of a submitted part before the balance ships, sales should quote the sample submission separately and make the remaining shipment date conditional on that acceptance.
