Questions worth asking.

Should a technical company lead with its credentials?+

Credentials can be relevant, but the customer still needs to understand their practical significance. A strong description connects genuine expertise with the type of work the company accepts, without assuming every buyer can interpret specialist terminology.

Can local visibility matter for a regional supplier?+

Yes, particularly when access, collaboration or onsite support affects the decision. The company's reach can extend beyond Lubbock while retaining a clear base. Geographic relevance and technical suitability should reinforce each other.

What if our strongest capability is not our most familiar service?+

That is a useful reason to examine discovery. A company can remain associated with earlier work even after its expertise develops. The objective should reflect proven current capability and the customers the business is prepared to serve now.
