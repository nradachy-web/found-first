How to choose an AEO agency worth trusting.

Choose an AEO agency by its evidence, proposed work, and ability to execute. Ask to see how it selects questions, records answers, diagnoses gaps, ships improvements, and connects visibility to useful business outcomes.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

A polished dashboard is easy to demonstrate. It is harder to explain why a certain page should be built, what evidence it needs, and who will implement it. A strong proposal makes that reasoning visible and defines the practical scope before the engagement begins.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Inspect the evidence

Ask whether a case study describes SEO, Google Ads, AEO, or ChatGPT ads. Check dates, measurement definitions, and the agency's actual role. Experience can transfer without becoming proof of a different channel.

02 / Inspect the delivery plan

Identify the pages, technical work, research, creative, and reporting included. Confirm who handles implementation and what information your team must provide.

03 / Inspect the working relationship

Meet the people responsible for decisions. Agree ownership, access, review cadence, and fees in the proposal. Look for reporting that can explain a disappointing result as clearly as a good one.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • Show me a sample deliverable and the decision it supports.
  • Which claimed results came from this exact channel?
  • Who will make and implement the next important change?

What the work can include.

  • Evidence-backed priorities and inspectable outputs.
  • Reporting definitions and access requirements.
  • A practical starting point before a larger commitment.
