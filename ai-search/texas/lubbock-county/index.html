Questions worth asking.

Can a Lubbock County business serve a regional audience from one location?+

Yes. A specialist facility, remote service or planned travel model can support customers beyond the county. The public description should explain the actual arrangement so regional reach does not imply additional premises.

Should regional access be the main reason to choose?+

It can support the decision, but expertise and service fit still matter. Convenient access does not explain what the business can do for the customer. The strongest identity connects the practical location with a substantive commercial capability.

What if our customers travel but our staff do not?+

That is a legitimate model and should remain clear. A business can be regionally important as a destination provider without offering onsite work throughout the region. Generated discovery should preserve that distinction rather than create the wrong expectation about delivery.
