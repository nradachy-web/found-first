ChatGPT advertising · Campaign diagnostics and reporting

Should a one-day spike set the forecast for ChatGPT ads?

Keep the peak day in your ChatGPT ads results. Before using it to forecast another period, establish whether the circumstances that made it unusual are likely to recur.

Show how much the forecast depends on the peak

Use daily campaign impressions to calculate the typical pace across comparable operating days and the average with the peak included. Show both alongside the actual total. Name the assumption behind the higher projection, such as another period with the same pattern of unusually strong delivery. If there is no evidence for a recurrence, the higher projection is a scenario.

Check the calendar before attributing the spike

Compare the peak with the business calendar, campaign schedule and any changes in what was advertised. A coincidence may suggest a useful comparison without explaining why the ad was served. If the same promotion has run before, look at those dates as well as ordinary trading days.

A ticket launch in an ordinary month

Consider a film society whose campaign report shows a conspicuous impression peak on the day festival tickets opened. Its coordinator has the daily report and the programming calendar. For a month of regular screenings, she bases the working forecast on comparable screening days and retains the festival day in the historical total. If the next period contains another festival launch and earlier launches also showed elevated delivery, she includes a separate launch-day estimate. She can then show trustees exactly which part of the forecast depends on the event.
