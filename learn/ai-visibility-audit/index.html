AI visibility audits

An AI visibility audit records what AI systems such as ChatGPT and Google's AI features say when asked your customers' questions: whether your business is named, how it is described and which sources the answer uses. A useful one hands you the questions, the saved answers with their dates and models, and a short ranked list of fixes. It describes a dated sample, not what every customer will see.

[ definition ]

An AI visibility audit is a dated, repeatable record of how selected AI systems answer a defined set of customer questions about a business and its market, reviewed for mentions, accuracy and sources, and turned into a ranked list of work.

A score without the answers behind it is not an audit. If you cannot read what the AI said, you cannot check the conclusion.

What a useful audit checks

Ten checks. The first four are about how the answers were collected, because a careless collection makes everything after it unreliable.

  • The questions come from real buying situationsProblems, comparisons, objections and a few checks of your business by name. The list is written down in the wording that was used.
  • Each question is asked more than onceAnswers vary from one run to the next. One answer is an anecdote, so the audit repeats each question and says how many times.
  • More than one AI system is coveredEach is named, with the model and whether it searched the web. Anything that could not be collected is shown as unavailable, not counted as zero.
  • Every answer is saved in fullWith its date, so anyone can read what was said and check the conclusion drawn from it.
  • Mentions are read, not only countedWas the business offered as an option, used as a source or mentioned as a caution? Was it confused with a similarly named company?
  • Sources are listedWhich pages each answer cited or linked, whether your own pages are among them, and which outside sources keep appearing.
  • Other businesses are recordedWhich ones are named for the questions where you are not, and what those answers cite.
  • Business facts are checkedThe name, locations, phone numbers, services and availability given in the answers are compared with the real ones.
  • The site can be reached and readCrawling is allowed in robots.txt and by any CDN in front of the site, key pages are indexed and can show a snippet in Google, and OpenAI's search crawler, OAI-SearchBot, is not blocked.
  • Findings become a short, ranked listEach item says what to fix, the evidence for it and who does it. An observation that has not been confirmed as a real gap is labeled as unconfirmed.

Crawling and indexing requirements are from Google Search Central and OpenAI's crawler documentation, checked November 11, 2026.

The test of an audit is whether you could check its conclusions yourself. Ask for each of these before you pay for one.

How to run a first check yourself

You can learn a good deal on your own, with no tools. It will not replace a full audit, but it will tell you whether you need one.

  1. Write the questions

    List what a customer asks before they know your name: one question for each problem you solve, a few comparisons and one or two about your business directly.

  2. Ask each one more than once

    Use a new chat each time, so earlier messages do not steer the answer, and use every assistant your customers are likely to use.

  3. Save the whole answer

    Copy the text and its links into our free tracking sheet with the date, the assistant and the exact question.

  4. Read before you count

    Note whether you were named, in what role, which sources were cited and whether your details were right. Then decide what, if anything, needs fixing first.

What an audit cannot tell you

An audit is a sample of something that moves. Answers change with the wording of the question, the date, the model and the context of the chat. Consumer apps may also use different systems, context and settings from the route an audit tests. An audit records what was returned on the days it ran.

It also cannot say why. A count shows that one business was named more often than another, not what caused it. A missing citation does not prove a listing is missing, and a mention count is not a share of the market.

That is why the audit is the start of the work, not the result. The fixes it points to are covered in answer engine optimization services, and how to tell whether they changed anything is in Measuring AI marketing results.

Questions about AI visibility audits

Is the free AI visibility check a complete audit?+

No. It is an initial sample. Send your website and what you sell, and Nick reviews how AI describes your business and replies with what we found and a useful next step. The full research study is a separate, scoped engagement.

Can a visibility score prove market share?+

No. A score from a selected set of questions describes that sample on that date. It is not the share of all AI users who see your business, and it should always come with the questions and answers behind it.

Can I run a first check myself?+

Yes. Write down the questions your customers ask, put each one to the assistants they are likely to use more than once, and save the full answers with the date. Our free tracking sheet has a column for each thing worth recording.

Which AI systems should an audit cover?+

The ones your customers are likely to use, each named in the report with the date it was asked. A system that could not be collected should be shown as unavailable, not counted as zero.

How often should an audit be repeated?+

When you need to know whether something changed: after you correct business facts or publish new pages, and at a steady interval after that. Repeat the same questions in the same way, or the two rounds cannot be compared.

Will an audit improve my visibility?+

Not by itself. It shows where to work. The changes that follow, such as correcting business facts, fixing crawl problems and publishing clearer answers, are separate work, and no one can guarantee they will change what an AI system says.
