What has changed in your business?

Questions worth asking.

Can an established business reposition without abandoning existing customers?+

Yes. The aim is to clarify the relationship between established services and newer capabilities. Existing customers should still recognize what they value, while the intended new buyer can understand why the business belongs in a different comparison.

Should we describe every corridor we serve?+

Only when those distinctions help customers understand availability or the nature of the service. A list of place names does not explain expertise. The geographic account should remain subordinate to the actual service relationship.

What if our reputation is stronger than our public description?+

That is a meaningful reason to examine AI discovery. The company may need its public account to carry more of the substance that current customers already know. The objective is an accurate first impression, not an invented reputation or an inflated service claim.
