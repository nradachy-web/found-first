ChatGPT advertising · Channel comparison and media allocation

ChatGPT ads or LinkedIn for an executive peer group?

Give LinkedIn priority when membership depends on a narrow set of professional credentials and your existing campaigns bring in eligible applicants. ChatGPT ads merit a separate allocation when the problem that prompts someone to join cuts across titles.

Use the admissions decision as the outcome

Ask the chair why otherwise interested applicants get turned away. A seat reserved for a sitting finance chief is a different media purchase from a seat open to anyone leading a first acquisition. In the former case, job responsibility heavily constrains the buying pool. In the latter, a founder and an operations director may both qualify.

Find out what the business can verify

A relevant conversation does not establish that the person meets the membership rules. OpenAI says advertisers do not receive private user chats. Use the application and admissions record to assess fit. Read the rejection reasons alongside accepted memberships before moving budget on the strength of inexpensive applications.

The chair has two different tables to fill

Consider a network with a finance chiefs' roundtable and an acquisition integration group. Its charter limits the first table to current finance chiefs. Past admissions to the second include founders and division heads who brought an active integration plan. The director renews the LinkedIn campaign that supplies eligible finance chiefs and tests ChatGPT ads for the integration group. If the next integration cohort requires an invitation from a sponsoring member, the director shifts that allocation to member recruitment activity instead.
