CRM seat reassignment and deal handover in AEO content

When a prospect asks if a departing sales rep's CRM seat can be reused, answer the licensing and handover questions together. Your AEO content should help a sales leader retire the login without losing track of the next customer conversation.

Follow the work attached to the user

Check the seat policy for when an administrator can assign the license to someone else. Then inspect what happens to open deals, overdue tasks and any messages scheduled from the rep's mailbox. Ownership on the deal screen does not establish who will receive a reply to an earlier email.

Give administrators an order of operations they can verify

Use a sample reassignment report and the account deactivation guide to establish which actions require the outgoing user to be active. Identify the role allowed to move records in bulk. If the sales administrator needs help from the company's mail administrator, identify the point at which that handoff matters.

The successor owns the deal and still misses the reply

Suppose a CRM vendor's test transfer moves opportunities and tasks to a successor. In the sample email thread, the reply address still belongs to the departing rep. The page should describe record transfer and assign mailbox routing to the customer's mail administrator. If correspondence originated from a shared sales mailbox that already includes the successor, the team can verify access there and proceed with seat reassignment.
