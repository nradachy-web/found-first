The shortlist is the first sale

You have a product with a point of view. A team that can defend it. Customers who understand why it matters. The next buyer still has to put you in contention before any of that gets a chance to work.

Found First is for B2B and SaaS brands that want a presence with enough weight to merit the closer look.

Let the product punch at its weight

A category leader can feel inevitable before the buyer sees a demo. A challenger has to make its own case for belonging in the room.

We like challengers with substance. The specialized product. The sharper alternative. The team that knows exactly why a customer should choose it. Put some weight behind that conviction.

Give the sales team a name worth opening with

A strong product deserves to be more than the option someone discovers after signing with the competition. Make the first impression count while the choice is still open.

The feature tour, the demo and the sales pitch all have their moment. We care about the presence that precedes them. Your team should get the chance to show why the product belongs on that shortlist.
