Tracing ChatGPT ads phone leads through a transfer

Count a phone booking from ChatGPT ads only when your records support both the campaign connection and the appointment. A tap on a phone number, an answered call and a confirmed booking each answer a different business question.

Follow the transfer to its destination

Start with the evidence your website and phone system actually retain. A phone-link event shows an attempt to call. Reception's log may establish that someone reached the business. To check the handoff, look for a call reference or intake record that the booking desk can match to its appointment entry. Similar times alone can point to the wrong person on a busy line.

Report the last step you can verify

Preserve a confirmed phone inquiry even if the appointment outcome is missing. Likewise, a genuine booking with an unknown acquisition source still belongs in the sales ledger. Showing both gaps gives the owner a reason to improve reception records before making a budget decision from incomplete bookings.

A storage inquiry goes to the reservations desk

Imagine that a warehouse operator can trace a campaign inquiry to an answered call. Reception's intake slip says the caller requested pallet space and was transferred. The reservations ledger contains the same intake reference beside a confirmed booking, so the operator counts one phone booking. If the transfer ended in voicemail and a later reservation has only a similar timestamp, the operator keeps the original inquiry open for follow-up and leaves the reservation source unresolved.
