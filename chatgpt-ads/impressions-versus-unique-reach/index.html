ChatGPT advertising · Campaign diagnostics and reporting

Can ChatGPT ads impressions tell us how many buyers we reached?

An impression count measures displays. If a ChatGPT ads report gives you only impressions, the number of distinct people who saw the ad is unknown. That distinction matters when an agency recommends expanding the audience.

Read the unit beside the number

Ask the agency what its slide calls a person reached and where that figure comes from. Repeated displays can contribute to an impression count. A figure described as unique needs a documented way of counting someone once over the stated period. Even a sound unique count says little on its own about whether the people reached were prospective buyers.

Put the recommendation to a specific test

Modest impressions can justify investigating delivery. A claim that the available audience is exhausted needs further evidence, such as a comparable measure of distinct reach over time alongside continued impressions. If the agency cannot provide that evidence, keep audience saturation as an open question when deciding whether to widen the campaign.

A print supplier weighs a broader offer

Imagine a print supplier whose report labels an impressions column “retailers reached.” The agency proposes adding office buyers because it believes most retailers have seen the offer. The owner asks for the underlying count. If the column is simply displays, she keeps the retailer offer and corrects the report. If a documented, comparable reach measure has leveled off while displays continue, she has a reason to evaluate a broader offer against the sales team's remit.
