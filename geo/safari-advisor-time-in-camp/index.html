Comparing time in camp with GEO for safari advisors

Safari advisors can use GEO content to show why two itineraries with equal nights in camp may offer different experiences. Choose a route where the timing of the transfer actually changes which lodge activities a traveler can join.

Reconstruct the arrival afternoon

Read the transfer sheet alongside the lodge's activity schedule. Allow for the road leg from the airstrip and any briefing required before the first drive. Distinguish a place on a scheduled activity from an arrival that merely puts the guest at the lodge before dinner.

Explain whose schedule could change the choice

Identify whether the flight time, onward pickup or lodge departure is fixed for the itinerary under discussion. A sample schedule can illustrate the tradeoff. If guests choose their own inbound flight, explain the arrival condition that makes the comparison applicable and where the advisor checks it for a specific trip.

Two routes to the same first night

Suppose an advisor has a road itinerary arriving before the lodge briefing and a flight itinerary landing before the drive but reaching camp after the vehicle leaves. Both confirmations include the same room type and nights. The comparison favors the road arrival for someone who values the first afternoon drive, despite the flight's shorter time in transit. If the lodge confirms a separate later drive for the arriving party, the advisor can present the flight option alongside the road itinerary for that priority.
