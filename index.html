A few clear answers.

What is AEO?+

Answer engine optimization is the work of making your business easier to discover and understand in AI-powered search and answers. It builds on useful content, sound technical SEO, and clear, credible business information. Inclusion and rankings are not guaranteed.

How is AEO different from ChatGPT ads?+

AEO focuses on organic discoverability. ChatGPT ads are paid placements. Buying an ad does not buy a place in an organic answer or change what ChatGPT says about your business. Read OpenAI’s advertising overview.

Can this work alongside our current marketing?+

Yes. We start by understanding what you already run, then recommend where AI visibility or advertising can complement it. The scope, responsibilities, and budget are agreed before work starts.
