ChatGPT advertising · Conversion measurement and sales outcomes

Which ChatGPT ads inquiries count as qualified leads?

Agree on what sales needs to confirm before a ChatGPT ads inquiry earns qualified-lead credit. Count every genuine inquiry at intake, then use the buyer's stated need and the seller's ability to serve it to make the qualification decision.

Write a rule a second reviewer can apply

Start with a real reason your business accepts or declines work. A commercial kitchen supplier might require a named facility and a request for equipment it sells. A corporate email address alone establishes neither. Record the detail that satisfied the rule so a marketing manager can review the sales decision against the original inquiry.

Give an incomplete inquiry an owner

When the form omits an essential fact, leave qualification open and assign someone to ask for it. At review time, show the unresolved group alongside qualified and declined inquiries. A pile of unanswered requests calls for a different spending decision than a pile of documented requests for services you cannot provide.

A replacement oven for which kitchen?

Imagine that a restaurant equipment dealer receives a request for a replacement oven. The form names a model the dealer carries, but the installation address is blank. The rep checks the email reply and finds the address of a restaurant within the dealer's service area. That reply supports qualified-lead credit and a sales visit. If the buyer replies that the oven is for a home kitchen, the rep records the reason for declining and marketing checks whether the offer clearly says commercial installation.
