AEO for vacation rentals with fixed changeover days

Use AEO to explain your rental’s changeover rule when guests see open nights but cannot select the stay they want. Give the accepted arrival and departure pattern for the property and season, then send travelers to the booking calendar for current inventory.

Show how dates must fit together

A minimum length and a permitted arrival day answer different questions. A guest might satisfy the length requirement and still choose the wrong first night. Put the pattern in words alongside the date picker, including whether departure is fixed too. Give each home its own answer if neighboring properties use different schedules.

Publish the rule separately from available dates

Google describes ordinary search requirements for its AI features. For the rental site, a sensible editorial choice is to publish the standing changeover policy in readable text. Keep open dates and any booking exceptions in the reservation system, where a traveler can test the whole stay.

An open week that a Sunday arrival misses

Consider a beach house whose summer rate plan accepts Saturday arrivals and departures. A traveler wants a Sunday-to-Sunday stay of the same length. The operator can explain why moving both dates to Saturday may produce a bookable stay. If the manager releases a short gap with flexible arrivals, only the dates covered by that exception should get a different answer. The next full week still follows the house’s summer pattern.
