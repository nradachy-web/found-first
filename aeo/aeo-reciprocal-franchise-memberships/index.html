AEO for franchises offering reciprocal membership benefits

If your franchise offers reciprocal benefits, write the AEO answer for the particular benefit a member wants to use. Identify the branch that can admit the visitor, the evidence it can accept and the operator that controls changes to the membership agreement.

Write the answer at the point of redemption

Use the membership terms, participating location list and guest check-in instructions together. A directory can establish that a club participates while leaving open whether the visitor can reserve a court, attend a class or bring a guest. Put those practical distinctions on the host club’s page beside its booking instructions.

Identify the home operator on account questions

A member reading the host page may also need to freeze or renew a plan. Look at the membership confirmation to find the team that can make that change. Give the reader a way to identify their home club, especially when several nearby franchisees use the same brand name.

A visiting player asks to book a court

At a fictional racket club franchise, the membership card names the issuing club. The network guide grants open-play entry at participating clubs, while the host’s reservation sheet limits advance court bookings to its own members. The host page can invite the visitor to check in for open play. For an advance booking it gives the host’s guest reservation route instead. If the same player wants to pause the membership during travel, the confirmation email identifies the issuing club as the team that changes the account.
