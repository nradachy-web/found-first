Choosing an AEO agency should not require a translator.

Choosing an AEO agency means choosing who you trust with your brand. Found First brings a clear point of view, high standards and people you can speak to directly. You should know who is standing behind the pitch.

By Found First/Updated November 12, 2026

On this page

  • Meet the people behind the name

You have heard enough impressive words

An acronym is not a credential.

You can fill a meeting with impressive terminology and leave without a single reason to believe. You deserve to leave knowing who you spoke to, what they stand for and why you would trust them with your name.

Meet the people behind the name

Found First brings together Nick Radachy of Modern Apex Strategies and Jeffrey Davis of Frontier App Labs. Two founders with their own businesses, their own standards and a shared interest in where brands get found next.

The names on the brand are the names behind the work. Put difficult questions in front of us. We are comfortable having them attached to our answers.

Bring high expectations

This is your reputation and your business. You should be demanding about the people you put beside it. The right relationship should make you feel understood and taken seriously.

We like clear opinions and owners who know why their business deserves more attention. Bring your expectations, your skepticism and the things previous agency pitches left unanswered. You will not need a glossary.

Questions worth asking us
