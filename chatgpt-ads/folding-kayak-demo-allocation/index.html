ChatGPT advertising · Channel comparison and media allocation

ChatGPT ads or Meta for a folding kayak launch?

Keep launch money behind a proven demonstration on Meta if the buyer needs to see one person fold and carry the kayak. Give ChatGPT ads a share when a different purchase obstacle, such as fitting the packed boat in an apartment, can be resolved with specific product information.

Identify the moment that made the sale possible

Review demo-day notes, dealer emails and the questions customers asked just before ordering. Seeing a rigid hull fold can change a shopper's view of the whole product. Confirming packed length helps someone who already understands the design choose a model. The same order can depend on both, but only one may still be holding up the launch.

State what the next media purchase buys

Renewing distribution for an existing film buys more opportunities to see the mechanism. A ChatGPT ad can offer a route to a model comparison when the ad, page and conversation are relevant. Put separate spending decisions against those jobs. Views of the fold sequence and visits to the size chart are intermediate evidence, so check what customers actually ordered.

The launch meeting turns on the dealer's question log

Suppose dealers report that prospects leave until a staff member folds the boat unaided. The launch buyer backs the existing Meta film and checks dealer orders during the promotion. Now suppose demonstration visitors understand the fold but keep asking which model fits behind a compact car's rear seat. With measured bag dimensions and a model comparison ready, the buyer diverts part of the next distribution buy to ChatGPT ads for that choice.
