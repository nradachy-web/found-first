Advertising jeans by inseam and wash in ChatGPT ads

Treat an inseam as a specific offer whenever length is the selling point of your ChatGPT ad. A denim line can include tall sizes while the photographed wash is available only in regular length.

Read the assortment across a single row

Start with the wash in the photograph and follow it through the order grid to the waist and length combinations sold together. A list of every wash beside a separate list of every inseam hides the gaps. The useful question for a buyer is whether the pair pictured comes in their length.

Distinguish the garment measurement from the styling

A cuff rolled for a studio shoot can make a full-length jean look cropped. Use the finished inseam from the garment specification, and show the hem unrolled if length is central to the offer. A model’s height can help someone interpret the photograph, but it cannot establish the length of the jeans.

The tall cut comes in rinse only

Imagine a denim merchant reviewing a pale vintage wash for a tall straight-leg ad. The wholesale line sheet groups both washes under one style name, but the retail order grid shows the long inseam only in dark rinse. The merchant uses a dark rinse photograph and opens the style with that wash selected. If a later cut brings the long inseam to both washes, a side-by-side wash choice becomes useful. The merchant can keep the tall-fit message and let the shopper choose the finish.
