Big footprint. Bigger presence.

AEO for brands that expect to matter in every market.

Your brand may cover the map. That does not mean it owns the conversation. Found First helps multi-location businesses pursue an AI search presence worthy of the name above every door.

Every location has something to prove

A customer choosing a business near home is making a local decision. To that customer, your entire network comes down to whether your brand feels like the right choice in their market.

The sign above the door is a start. We want the presence behind it to carry weight when that customer is deciding where to go.

Think beyond the head office

The boardroom sees a network. A customer sees a choice. Found First is for brands that understand the difference and want to compete where those choices are made.

A regional group, a franchise, an expanding chain: the challenge is to feel substantial close to the customer. Your next location should inherit more than a logo.

Make the map mean something

You have invested in people, premises and a name worth carrying into another market. Your online presence should have the same appetite for territory.

Big enough to cover the map. Distinct enough to matter in the neighborhood. That is the standard we want growing brands to hold themselves to. A footprint is something to build on, never a reason to get comfortable.

Questions from growing brands

Is Found First relevant to a franchise network?+

Yes. Franchise and multi-location businesses can discuss fit with us. The conversation starts with the brand and the markets it wants to compete in.

Do all locations have to be identical?+

No. A shared brand can include different local realities. Your business remains the reference point for deciding whether an engagement makes sense.

Can you guarantee which location an AI answer names?+

No. Platforms control their answers, and we cannot guarantee a particular business or location will be named.
