AEO for equipment rental depots that only dispatch deliveries

If a rental depot only loads delivery trucks, commission an AEO edit that answers whether customers can visit before they plan a trip. The town can be an accurate answer to where equipment comes from and a misleading answer to where someone can collect it.

Read the address in context

Put the depot’s role beside each address customers encounter while choosing a rental. A yard labeled as a branch under a heading promising pickup sends a different message from the same yard listed as a dispatch point. Keep directions to a public counter with the rentals that counter can actually release.

Give delivery customers a usable next step

Answer the question that follows a closed gate. Which job sites can the depot deliver to, and what information does the team need to check a specific site? A billing address does little to establish whether a truck can serve the equipment destination.

The generator yard has a sign but no counter

Imagine a rental company whose generator page invites customers to visit the nearest yard. Its yard instructions assign the street entrance to freight drivers, and its reservation sheet marks generators for delivery only. The writer removes the pickup invitation from that page and asks for the delivery address. If the company later opens an appointment desk there for contract signing, the location page can offer that visit. Generator collection still needs a separate change to the release policy before the equipment page offers pickup.
