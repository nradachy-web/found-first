ChatGPT advertising · Landing page decisions after the ad

Direct file or edition selector for ChatGPT ads promoting downloads?

Use a direct download when the ChatGPT ad names one self-contained file that buyers can recognize and use on its own. Use a short selection page when a buyer could reasonably download the wrong edition, format or companion file.

Let the ad wording determine how much choice remains

For a named edition, check the downloaded file itself. Its cover, filename and edition label should still identify it after the visitor closes the browser. A general resource-library page adds work if the ad has already settled the choice. Label the file type in the ad or on the download control so opening a document feels expected.

Build the selector around a feature buyers recognize

An internal version code is a weak label for people who have never seen your catalog. Use an observable difference such as connector shape, control-panel layout or compatible model name, with a small illustration when it helps. Put the corresponding download beside each choice.

A loom manufacturer distributing threading guides

Picture a loom manufacturer comparing its ad copy with two guide covers and photos from the equipment manual. Both looms share a model name, but one uses a sliding lever and the other a rotating handle. An ad for the model’s threading guide should open a selector showing those controls. For an ad specifically offering the rotating-handle edition, the buyer has already made that distinction. A direct file bearing the handle illustration and edition name is the shorter usable route.
