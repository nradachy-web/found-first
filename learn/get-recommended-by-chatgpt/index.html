Field notes/Getting recommended by ChatGPT

Help ChatGPT search understand your business.

Organic ChatGPT visibility starts with an accurate business worth understanding.

Found First helps businesses improve their organic visibility in ChatGPT search by strengthening the public information buyers use to understand them. The work connects website access, clear product or service positioning and credible evidence. The objective is relevant consideration for the business you actually operate. A mention, a cited page and a recommendation are different observations, and none is a guaranteed or permanent position.

Being named is useful only in the right context

A company can appear in an answer and still be misunderstood. A software vendor described as a consultant, a specialist presented as a generalist or a provider associated with an obsolete offer can attract the wrong expectations. More mentions would not necessarily solve that problem.

The relevant question is whether a prospective buyer encounters a correct reason to consider your business. Found First connects that question to the actual offer, intended customer and next step. This makes organic visibility a commercial decision rather than a competition to appear in every possible conversation.

Search access and model training are different choices

OpenAI documents separate crawler controls for search and model training. OAI-SearchBot concerns ChatGPT search discovery, while GPTBot concerns content that may be used in training. A business should understand that distinction rather than assume it must make one all-or-nothing decision about AI access.

Technical access is only part of the issue. A reachable page can still explain the business poorly. The useful work connects the website’s availability with accurate information about the offer. It does not sell crawler access as a guarantee that a particular answer will include the company.

Give a buyer a coherent account of the business

A prospect should be able to identify what the company sells, whom it serves and what supports its claims. Public information should preserve meaningful differences between products, services and delivery models. A broad promise to help everyone can make a strong specialist business harder to recognize.

ChatGPT search can use current web sources and show citations. OpenAI also notes that search results and citations can be incomplete or wrong. That makes a useful source destination important: a reader should be able to verify the relevant claim and continue evaluating the business without encountering a contradictory or outdated story.

Keep the evidence distinct

These observations answer different questions about discovery.

Keep the evidence distinct
ObservationWhat it establishes
Business mentionThe company was named in that answer
Source citationA page was used or linked as supporting information
Relevant recommendationThe answer proposed the business for that particular situation
Qualified inquiryA person with an appropriate need contacted the business

A citation alone does not establish endorsement, a lead or a sale.

Choose organic visibility work for an organic problem

Found First provides organic AI visibility and supporting website and content improvements. This is separate from managed ChatGPT advertising: OpenAI says ads do not influence its answers. Claiming a business profile or considering a Business Agent is also a distinct decision from improving broad organic representation.

Questions about organic ChatGPT visibility

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?+

No. Found First can improve the information and website experience the business controls, but the platform determines its answers. Any observed appearance should be understood in its actual context rather than sold as a permanent ranking.

Will advertising make the organic answer favor our company?+

No. OpenAI states that ads are separate and do not influence answers. Paid distribution can be a relevant service, but it should not be purchased under the impression that it buys organic endorsement.

Do we need to reveal our private operating methods?+

No. Buyers need an accurate account of the offer, fit and supporting evidence. Proprietary implementation methods and commercial terms can remain private while the public website gives suitable prospects enough information to evaluate the business.
