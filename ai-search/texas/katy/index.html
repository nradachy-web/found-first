The City of Katy describes its location across Harris, Waller and Fort Bend counties. Questions worth asking.

Does serving Katy mean serving all three counties?+

No. The city's location across county boundaries does not determine a company's service area. A business can cover a defined portion of the area or accept selected projects more widely, depending on its actual operations.

Should the county or city lead our positioning?+

Use the geography that helps customers understand the service. City language can aid recognition, while county context may clarify a broader footprint. Neither should imply universal coverage when the business has meaningful limits.

Can broad visibility be a poor fit for recurring services?+

Yes. A recurring service relationship depends on reliable delivery, not just interest. If inquiries consistently come from locations or job types the company cannot support efficiently, the apparent reach may be less useful than a more focused presence.
