Questions worth asking.

Should a Berrien County company emphasize its county or its city?+

Use the geographic description that matches the decision. A place customers visit needs a clear location. A service delivered across several communities needs an accurate coverage explanation. Neither label should substitute for describing the offer.

What if our brand is known by different names locally?+

That can be a useful issue to examine. Familiar shorthand may work inside one community and confuse an outside buyer. The company should remain recognizable without depending on an introduction from someone who already knows its history.

Is broader visibility sensible for a small team?+

It can be, if the target work and service reach fit its capacity. A focused team may benefit more from stronger recognition for a defined specialty than from presenting itself as available for every request throughout the county.
