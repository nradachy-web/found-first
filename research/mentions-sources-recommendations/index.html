Being named is not enough.

A mention can flatter a report and still tell you very little. Found First looks beyond the headline number.

A logo in an answer is not the finish line.

A business can be named without being the business a customer chooses. The distinction matters when the sale depends on trust, fit and what the answer actually says.

Our Rooter-Man Austin research found the same business in service shortlists, useful guidance and availability cautions. A mention count alone would flatten those very different appearances into one number.

We are interested in the story behind the score.

Found First combines original AI-search research with the judgment of people who build and market businesses. The ambition is a stronger competitive presence. The standard is evidence that means something.

That is why our public findings are specific about what they show. Being named is observable. Winning the customer is a different outcome. We do not pass one off as the other.

The full picture belongs in a private conversation about your business and the market you want to win.
