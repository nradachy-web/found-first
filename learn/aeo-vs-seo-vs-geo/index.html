SEO. AEO. GEO. Make the name they remember yours.

SEO concerns visibility in search results. AEO and GEO are terms used for visibility in AI-generated answers. Found First focuses on the business behind those labels and its ambition to be seen, considered and chosen.

Your customers do not buy an acronym

They buy the business they trust to solve a problem or deliver something they want. The language around search will keep evolving. Your reason for being considered should be stronger than the latest label.

Found First is for businesses that want a position worth defending. The terminology can take a back seat to the name you are building.

A stronger presence is the point

A respected name, an excellent product or a standout service deserves attention. Getting that attention has always been competitive. AI gives ambitious businesses another reason to take their presence seriously.

We bring advertising and technology experience to that challenge. Your company is the thing that matters. Where you want to compete should set the tone.

Make the name count

The most memorable thing about your marketing should be your brand. The one a buyer recognizes, considers and chooses to look at more closely.

We want more businesses with substance in that position. A bigger presence for a name that deserves it. That is the ambition behind Found First, however the industry chooses to label the work.

Questions behind the acronyms

What do SEO, AEO and GEO stand for?+

Search engine optimization, answer engine optimization and generative engine optimization. They describe related ambitions around being found in search results and AI answers.

Which term does Found First use?+

We use AEO when discussing visibility in AI answers. The useful conversation is about your business and whether our work is a fit.

Can any of these services guarantee that we are named?+

No. Found First does not guarantee rankings, AI mentions, leads or sales under any of these labels.
