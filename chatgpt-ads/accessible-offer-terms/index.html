Where to put offer terms on a ChatGPT ads landing page

Place any condition that could change a visitor’s choice beside the advertised offer, before the booking or purchase button. Publish the details as readable page text so visitors can review them on a phone, with a keyboard or with a screen reader.

Identify the sentence that changes the decision

Compare the ad wording with the offer sheet staff actually use. Circle restrictions that would cause the desk to turn someone away, such as excluded days or a membership requirement. Put those conditions in the offer summary. Longer details can sit in an expandable section with a descriptive label that visitors can reach and operate by keyboard.

Read the offer without the artwork

Try selecting the restriction as text, enlarging it on a phone and reaching it from the offer heading with a screen reader. A flyer may look sharp on a desktop while its fine print disappears at mobile size. Keep the artwork if it helps recognition and give the restriction a readable place in the page itself.

A weekday pottery pass inside a promotional poster

Imagine a studio advertising an open-studio pass. Its printed flyer and front desk redemption sheet limit the pass to weekday afternoons, but the page shows only the flyer above “Reserve.” The studio puts the weekday window in text beside the button and links to a usable schedule. If the desk later accepts the pass at every open session, the summary can drop that restriction and point buyers straight to available sessions.
