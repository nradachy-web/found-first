Field notes/Choosing an AEO agency

How to choose an AEO agency

Choose an AEO agency on evidence you can inspect: the customer questions it tests, the AI answers it saves, the specific changes it proposes for your site and how it reports afterwards. Walk away from guaranteed placements, secret methods and visibility scores with nothing behind them. No agency controls what an AI system says, so the honest ones sell the work and the record, not the outcome.

Eight questions to ask before you sign

Ask every agency on your list the same eight, ours included. A good answer is specific and comes with something you can look at. A red flag is usually a promise in place of a document.

Questions for an AEO agency, with what a good answer and a warning sign sound like
AskA good answerA red flag
Which customer questions will you test, and can I read the answers?A written list drawn from your real buying situations, and every AI answer saved in full with its date and model.A proprietary prompt set you cannot see, a few screenshots, or tests of your brand name only.
How do you count a mention?With a denominator and a role: named in so many of so many answers, as an option, a source or a caution.A single visibility score or percentage with no sample behind it. Every mention treated as an endorsement.
What exactly will you change?Named pages, business facts and technical fixes, each tied to something the research found, and a named person who makes the change.A volume of content with no link to any finding, and no word on who carries the changes out.
What do Google and OpenAI say about that tactic?A link to the platform's own documentation, and a plain statement of where the method is the agency's judgment.A special file, markup or submission presented as required. A paid listing package presented as the fix.
What do you guarantee?The work, the record and the reporting dates. Nothing about what an AI system will say.Guaranteed mentions, rankings or leads, or a promised date by which they will arrive.
Where did your case study results come from?The channel, the dates, what was measured and the agency's part in it. A baseline is called a baseline.Search advertising or SEO results offered as proof of AI visibility. Before and after figures with no method.
What does your report look like in a bad month?The same as in a good one. Mentions, paid results and confirmed sales on separate lines, with missing data shown as missing.A metric that changes from report to report, or one blended number that always goes up.
Who does the work, and what do I keep if we stop?The people you met. You keep the pages, the accounts and the research records, and the terms say so.No clear answer about who does the work. Accounts in the agency's name, or content you lose when you cancel.

Get these in writing

A proposal that covers these eight leaves little to argue about later.

  • The scopeWhat is researched, built, published and reported each month, in plain terms.
  • The fees, and what is billed separatelyAdvertising spend, third-party tools and one-off projects such as a rebuild.
  • Who does the workNames, and who your point of contact is.
  • What they need from youAccess, approvals and facts only you can confirm, such as service areas and availability.
  • Who owns whatYour website, content, ad accounts, analytics and research records should remain yours.
  • The term and how to end itLength, notice and what is handed over when the work stops.
  • The reporting datesWhen you will see results, in what format, and an example report.
  • What is not promisedA clear line that rankings, mentions and leads are not guaranteed.

Questions buyers ask

Can any agency guarantee that AI will name my business?+

No. AI systems choose their own answers, which vary with the wording, the date and the model. Google says indexing and serving is not guaranteed, and OpenAI says advertisers cannot shape, rank or alter ChatGPT's responses. Treat a guarantee as a reason to keep looking.

Do I need a specialist AEO agency, or can my SEO agency do this?+

Your SEO agency may be able to. Google says its SEO best practices remain relevant for AI features, so a good SEO team already does part of the work. Check whether they also test and save AI answers, because that is the part ordinary SEO reporting does not cover.

Does a Google Partner badge or an SEO case study prove AEO skill?+

No. It shows experience in that channel. Modern Apex Strategies, which Nick leads, is listed in the Google Partners directory, and that credential is about Google Ads. AEO needs its own evidence and measurement.

How long before AEO shows results?+

No one can tell you honestly in advance. Fixes to crawling and business facts can be confirmed when they ship. Whether and when AI answers change is something to measure, not to promise, so be wary of a quoted number of weeks.

What should I request before signing?+

A written scope, the fees and what is billed separately, who does the work, what access they need, who owns the pages, accounts and research when you stop, and an example of the report you will receive.
