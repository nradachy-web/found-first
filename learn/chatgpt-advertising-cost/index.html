Field notes/ChatGPT advertising costs

Understand the full cost of managed ChatGPT advertising.

A clear investment decision starts with knowing what each cost pays for.

ChatGPT advertising costs include media spend and any professional services you hire to manage the channel. Creative, measurement and website work may also affect the engagement, depending on what the business already has. Found First discusses its fees privately, but the cost categories should be clear before you commit. The useful question is what investment supports a suitable offer and a credible way to judge results.

Media spend buys distribution; management buys accountable work

The amount paid to the advertising platform is different from the fee paid to the people managing the campaign. A proposal should make that distinction understandable. Otherwise, two quotes can appear comparable while one includes substantially different responsibilities or leaves important work with your team.

Found First provides managed ChatGPT advertising around the offer, creative, campaign decisions and measurement. The proposed scope and commercial terms are discussed privately before commitment. We do not publish a rate card, and we do not disguise a service fee as advertising reach. You should know what work is included, what media investment is separate and which business decisions remain yours.

Read the billing model separately from the business outcome

OpenAI's current guidance describes impression and valid-click billing, including conversion-oriented campaigns that are not billed per completed conversion. That distinction matters: choosing an outcome for optimization is not the same as paying only when your business receives a qualified lead or sale.

Platform costs can also depend on the available opportunity and campaign conditions. A minimum daily campaign budget, where applicable, is not a recommendation for what your company should invest. Found First evaluates the channel against your actual offer rather than using a platform minimum or a generic industry average as a substitute for a commercial plan.

The starting condition of the business affects the scope

A company with clear creative, a strong destination and reliable outcome information presents a different engagement from one whose website leaves the offer unclear. The same applies to an established catalog versus a changing product range that needs better public explanations. These differences can affect the work required before distribution is useful.

Found First can discuss supporting website and content improvements where they address a real obstacle. That does not mean every advertiser needs a rebuild or every campaign requires a large creative project. The scope should follow the identified need, with optional work distinguished from what is necessary to support the agreed offer and customer journey.

Compare cost with the customer relationship you want

A low cost per click can still produce unsuitable inquiries. A more expensive inquiry can be worthwhile if it becomes an appropriate customer, but that cannot be assumed in advance. Your business's margins, fulfillment requirements and sales process determine what acquisition value means.

A free 30-minute consultation with Nick can clarify the offer, current marketing and the outcome that would justify investment. If there is a fit, Found First can discuss scope and fees directly. The conversation does not promise a finished audit or a guaranteed return. It helps establish whether managed ChatGPT advertising deserves a defined role in your business.

Questions before choosing the engagement

How much does Found First charge for ChatGPT Ads management?+

Fees are discussed privately after the proposed scope is understood. You receive the commercial terms before committing. Media spend, management and any agreed website or creative work should be clearly distinguished rather than hidden inside an unexplained total.

Does a conversion campaign mean we pay only for leads?+

No. OpenAI's current campaign guidance distinguishes optimization objectives from billing. A conversion-oriented campaign can still charge for eligible clicks or impressions. A reported conversion is also not automatically a qualified lead or completed sale.

Can you tell us the exact budget that will guarantee results?+

No. Found First does not guarantee delivery, customer volume or a return. The budget discussion should reflect the offer, business economics and available evidence, with uncertainty stated honestly.

Can we discuss cost before agreeing to ongoing work?+

Yes. The consultation is an opportunity to understand fit and discuss the engagement. Private pricing does not mean committing before you know the scope or commercial terms.
