Questions worth asking.

Should an appointment-led business still describe itself as retail?+

If retail is accurate, it can remain useful. But the broader category should not obscure the specialized relationship or lead buyers to expect a casual purchase when the actual offer involves planning.

Can AEO help explain a premium service without discussing prices?+

Yes. Buyers can evaluate suitability, complexity and the nature of the relationship without a public price discussion. The business should make the reason for choosing it understandable rather than relying on an unexplained premium label.

Does a Park Avenue identity replace a wider Winter Park position?+

No. A district can explain a destination, while the business may serve customers throughout the city or beyond. The actual purchasing relationship should determine which geography matters most.
