Questions worth asking.

Can a narrow manufacturer benefit from local visibility?+

Yes, when local proximity affects collaboration, inspection or service delivery. The useful audience may still be much narrower than all nearby buyers. Geography should support the production specialty rather than replace it as the reason to choose.

Should we pursue every related manufacturing category?+

Only where the capabilities genuinely overlap. Appearing suitable for work the business does not want can consume sales attention. A disciplined objective distinguishes adjacent technical language from the actual projects the company can accept.

What if purchasing staff use less technical terms than engineers?+

Both perspectives matter. A buyer may begin with a broad operational need and involve technical evaluators later. The business should be understandable at the beginning without making claims that fail the more detailed review.
