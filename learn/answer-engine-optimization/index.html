Answer engine optimization that starts with the buyer.

Answer engine optimization helps a business become easier to discover and accurately describe in AI-generated answers. Found First combines question research, technical improvements, answer content, and credible evidence to strengthen that foundation.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

A search ranking tells you where a page appears. It does not tell you whether an assistant recommends your business, understands your product, or repeats an outdated claim. Start by looking at the answer a prospective buyer actually receives. The gap between that answer and the decision you want to help them make is the work.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Map the buying conversation

Group questions by discovery, comparison, cost, and readiness to act. Separate branded questions from questions where someone has never heard of you.

02 / Make the source worth using

Give each important question a useful home. Add clear explanations, current facts, attributable evidence, and links to the next decision. Fix crawl and indexing problems that prevent discovery.

03 / Track the answer and the outcome

Repeat a documented set of questions, preserve source links, and note changes. Then examine identifiable referral traffic, inquiries, and sales separately from mentions.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • Which providers solve [problem] for [type of buyer]?
  • What should I compare before choosing [category]?
  • Is [brand] suitable for [specific requirement]?

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • A question map organized by buying stage.
  • A prioritized technical and content backlog.
  • Answer pages with evidence and a clear conversion path.
  • A repeatable visibility record and business outcome review.

Before you get started.

Can you guarantee a ChatGPT recommendation?

No. Assistants choose their own answers and can vary by wording, date, and context. Our work improves the material they can find and use; we report observed results.

Does AEO replace SEO?

The foundations overlap. Keep useful SEO work and extend measurement to generated answers, business mentions, and citations.
