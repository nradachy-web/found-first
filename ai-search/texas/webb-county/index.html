What remains after the immediate transaction?

Questions worth asking.

Should a Webb County facility service call itself a logistics business?+

Only when that accurately describes its role. A company can serve logistics customers while providing maintenance, equipment or property expertise. The buyer should recognize the service being purchased instead of inferring freight movement from the customer industry.

Can a local provider sell to a purchasing office outside the county?+

Yes. The decision maker and service location can be different. A clear identity should connect the local operating responsibility with the customer's wider organization without suggesting the provider accepts work at every site that organization owns.

What if we handle one local site for a much larger customer?+

That can be a substantial relationship on its own merits. The company should be understood for the work and territory it actually supports. A recognizable customer category does not need to become a claim of national coverage or responsibility for unrelated operations.
