GEO for wholesale nurseries with downloadable catalogs

Keep the downloadable catalog if landscape buyers use it to mark up planting lists or share options with a crew. For GEO, build a public index around the decisions buried in the catalog, with enough context to explain what the nursery is offering that season.

Give each entry a useful destination

A button labeled “download catalog” leaves a designer hunting for the right section. An index entry can identify the plant group, list the stock forms covered and name the section to open. Include the printed section title as well as any page reference so a buyer can navigate a saved copy whose PDF viewer counts the cover.

Keep availability claims within their source

A catalog may describe the grower’s planned assortment long after an individual block sells through. Identify the edition on the index and explain how a buyer checks availability for a delivery window. A stock list with its own update schedule needs a distinct label.

Finding trees supplied for bare-root planting

Picture a nursery whose spring catalog groups trees by variety, while a landscape firm needs to assemble a bare-root order. Symbols next to individual entries identify bare-root stock. An HTML index can collect those catalog entries under a bare-root heading, spell out the symbol and point to the variety pages. When a variety sells through, it still belongs in the spring assortment. If the nursery wants the index to accept orders against current stock, that entry also needs the current availability record.
