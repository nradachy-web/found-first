A beautiful room does not explain the assignment

A portfolio image can attract attention without answering whether the company handled a complete renovation, a particular trade or part of the design. A prospective customer needs that context to understand the relevance of the example. Otherwise, visual quality can create an assumption about your role that the first consultation has to correct.

For a kitchen remodeler, a useful project story can explain the kind of scope undertaken and the company's contribution. A whole-home renovation specialist needs a broader account of project responsibility. Found First helps connect approved examples to the business you want to sell, without borrowing another team's work or turning one attractive project into a universal capability claim.

Clarify how much help the homeowner can expect with design

Some customers arrive with a settled plan. Others need help defining what the project should become. Those are different starting points, and your website should make the company's role clear. Design services, coordination with an outside designer and construction from an approved plan are not interchangeable promises.

If the business offers a more involved planning relationship, that value should be visible before the customer requests a consultation. If it does not, the website should not imply otherwise. We improve the public explanation so a prospective homeowner can recognize whether their stage and expectations fit the service, leaving detailed scope and decisions for the actual project discussion.

Before you decide.

Will this attract only large remodeling projects?+

That depends on the business's chosen positioning, actual capabilities and the prospects who respond. No project mix can be guaranteed. The service can make the desired scope and working relationship clearer so homeowners have a better basis for deciding whether to inquire.

Can we use project photography from a designer we worked with?+

Any use should reflect the permissions available and accurately attribute your company's role. A shared project does not make every contribution your own. The marketing can still be persuasive when it explains precisely what your team did and why that experience is relevant.

What if we do both design-build and construction-only work?+

Both can be explained if they are current offerings. The important distinction is what the homeowner brings to the engagement and what your company provides. Clearer information can help those two conversations coexist without making every prospective client assume the same level of involvement.
