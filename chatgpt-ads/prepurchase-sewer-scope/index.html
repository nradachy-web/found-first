ChatGPT ads for pre-purchase sewer scope inspections

Advertise a pre-purchase sewer scope if your plumbing business can coordinate entry and deliver the evidence a buyer needs by their review deadline. An opening on the technician’s calendar covers only the visit. The written findings may be what the buyer is waiting for.

Put a sample deliverable on the inspection page

Show an anonymized excerpt of the inspection record you actually provide. Identify whether the customer receives video, annotated stills, written observations or a combination. Describe how the technician records a section the camera could not reach. A clear sample lets a buyer or agent decide whether to book before exchanging a string of emails.

Work backward from the review deadline

Ask for the property location, the deadline for findings and the person coordinating entry. Explain how the office checks access and confirms both the visit and report timing. Keep active backups on the service-call path so a homeowner seeking drain clearing can reach the right desk.

A camera opening ahead of a buyer’s deadline

Suppose a plumber has a sample narrated video, a technician opening on Thursday and a report reviewer back on Monday. A buyer needs written findings on Friday. The owner can advertise inspection requests with a deadline check and decline that particular booking up front. If the reviewer can sign off Thursday afternoon and the seller confirms entry, the office can offer the same visit with a written delivery time.
