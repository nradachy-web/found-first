ChatGPT ads for hardwood refinishing before move-in

Advertise pre-move hardwood refinishing when the house can stay empty through sanding, coating and the wait before furniture returns. Ask for the key handoff and the moving date before offering a refinishing date. “Empty this weekend” gives the scheduler only half the information.

Show the point when the movers can enter

A room can look ready in an after photo while the finish still has use restrictions. On the page behind the ChatGPT ad, show how the crew uses the selected finish’s care sheet to plan foot traffic, furniture delivery and rugs. Describe who will confirm those dates once the floor and finish are known.

Find out who else needs the hall

Invite the owner to mark rooms and any through-route to stairs or an exterior door on a simple floor plan. Ask about appliance deliveries or trades who need that route. A vacant house with a kitchen crew carrying cabinets across the only hallway may need a different work sequence.

The hallway on the mover’s route

Take a floor refinisher preparing a ChatGPT offer for homes between owners. She checks a buyer’s possession email and hallway sketch against the finish manufacturer’s guidance for bringing furniture back. The buyer gets the keys early enough for sanding, coating and the specified waiting period. The contractor can offer a site measure tied to the move-in window. If possession slips and the movers must cross the hall too soon, the estimator can discuss a later vacancy before asking the buyer to reserve dates.
