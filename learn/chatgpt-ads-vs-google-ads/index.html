ChatGPT ads vs. Google Ads

Google Search ads match the keywords you choose to what a person types into a search. ChatGPT ads are matched to the context of a conversation and shown below the answer, clearly labeled. They reach different moments, so keep the search campaigns that already pay and test ChatGPT ads as a separate, capped experiment.

Both are auctions for attention at the moment someone is looking for help. Almost everything else differs: what sets the ad off, where it sits, how you pay and what you can measure.

Buying the ad does not buy the answer

OpenAI is explicit that ads do not influence what ChatGPT says. Ads run on separate systems from the chat model, and advertisers have no ability to shape, rank or alter a response. An ad is a paid placement under the answer. It is not a mention in it.

That makes paid and organic two different jobs. Whether ChatGPT names your business in the answer itself depends on what AI systems can find and verify about you, which is the work of answer engine optimization. Found First manages both and reports them separately, so a good week for the ad is never mistaken for a better answer.

Why the organic side needs its own measurement

An ad report shows impressions and clicks. It cannot show which customer questions already lead AI to name you and which do not. Our first published study found that gap can be wide inside a single business.

How to test ChatGPT ads without breaking what works

A fair test has a fixed cost, one question to answer and the same scoring as the channel you already trust.

  1. Keep the search campaigns that pay

    Fund the test with new or spare budget. Pausing campaigns that already produce qualified leads removes the baseline you need to compare against.

  2. Fix the objective and the budget

    Choose views, clicks or conversions before you build. A campaign's objective cannot be changed later. Start with a daily budget, as OpenAI advises new advertisers, and cap the total.

  3. Score both channels the same way

    Use one definition of a qualified lead. Add UTM parameters to the ChatGPT landing page URLs so your analytics can separate the visits, and count inquiries, not clicks.

  4. Decide on your own numbers

    OpenAI publishes no benchmarks yet, so compare cost per qualified inquiry over the same dates. Expand what earns it and stop what does not.

Check these before you launch in ChatGPT

Each one can stop a campaign from running or make its results unreadable.

  • Your business is in an available countrySelf-serve Ads Manager requires the legal entity that advertises and is billed to be based in a listed country. The United States is listed.
  • Your category is allowedOpenAI does not allow political advertising. Sensitive or regulated categories such as health and financial services must meet stricter eligibility criteria.
  • Your topic is one where ads can appearAds are not eligible to appear near sensitive topics, including personal health, mental health or politics.
  • The landing page continues the conversationSomeone arriving from a detailed exchange expects the specific answer, not a general home page.
  • Conversion measurement is in placeSet it up in Ads Manager and tag your URLs before the first impression, not after.
  • The media budget is approvedAt least $25 a day for US dollar accounts, paid to OpenAI and separate from any management fee.

Questions about running both

Should we stop Google Ads to try ChatGPT ads?+

No. Keep the search campaigns that already produce qualified leads and pay for the ChatGPT test separately, with a limit you set in advance. Move budget only when your own results support it.

Do ChatGPT ads use keywords like Google Ads?+

Not in the same way. You write context hints for each ad group that describe the conversations, topics or keywords where your offer is relevant. OpenAI says the hints guide matching, but they are not exact-match keywords and do not guarantee delivery in specific conversations.

Will advertising in ChatGPT get my business named in its answers?+

No. OpenAI states that ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers and that advertisers cannot shape, rank or alter responses. Being named in an answer is separate work, and we measure it separately.

Can the same landing page serve both?+

Sometimes. It must answer the intent behind each visit. More specific conversations may need additional detail or a different offer.

What does a ChatGPT ads test cost?+

Two things. Media is paid to OpenAI, and the minimum daily budget is $25 for accounts billed in US dollars. Found First's ad management is part of a monthly program that starts at $2,000 for Foundation. Advertising spend is separate.
