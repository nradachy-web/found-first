Your product deserves the spotlight

The materials. The design. The detail that made you start the brand. Those choices matter, but a shopper has to encounter the product before they can appreciate the difference.

Found First is for brands with something worth bringing into view. Give people a reason to take a closer look while they are still deciding what to buy.

Let them compare

A confident brand can stand next to the alternatives. It knows what it is, who it is for and why a customer would care. That conviction should carry into the way the business shows up online.

The standout product. The specialist collection. The challenger ready to be taken seriously. We want more weight behind your name. Make the comparison interesting.

You did not build the brand to blend in

There is a difference between another item in a catalog and a brand someone means to buy. The product can earn that preference only after it earns a closer look.

We like businesses with character. A point of view in the product and the confidence to stand behind it. Found First wants your presence to feel as deliberate as the choices that made the brand yours.

Questions from ecommerce brands

Is Found First relevant to a niche store?+

Yes. We can discuss your brand, audience and ambitions to determine fit. A focused collection can have a strong reason to be considered.

Is this only for large catalogs?+

No. Catalog size alone does not decide fit. The conversation centers on the business you want to build and the customers you want to reach.

Can you guarantee our products are named in AI answers?+

No. Platforms control their answers. Found First does not guarantee product mentions, rankings or sales.
