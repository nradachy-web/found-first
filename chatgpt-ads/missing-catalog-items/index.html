ChatGPT advertising · Catalog campaigns and feed readiness

Why are intended products missing from a ChatGPT ads feed?

When expected products are missing from a ChatGPT ads feed, first establish whether the retailer actually included them. OpenAI makes feed campaigns available to eligible advertisers. A gap in your own export tells you nothing about a platform decision on that product.

Compare lists using the same identifier

Start with the merchandising team's list of products approved for paid promotion. Match it against the exact export supplied for the campaign, using individual item identifiers on both sides. Comparing a count of product families with a count of sellable sizes can hide genuine omissions and make a complete assortment look incomplete.

Locate the point where the evidence ends

For an absent row, examine the merchant's publication settings and export rules. For a row that is present, preserve its identifier and the submitted file version for the person investigating the campaign. An item with no reported delivery and an item absent from the source file are different findings. Neither alone establishes rejection.

A distributor's new label misses the export

Suppose a kitchenware distributor approves its enamel bakeware for advertising. The buyer's assortment sheet includes each pan, but the supplied catalog contains none. The export includes items labeled bakeware, while the new line was entered under ovenware. Merchandising confirms the line belongs in the paid selection and corrects the rule. If the exact pan identifiers already appear in the submitted file, changing that rule has no evidentiary basis. The buyer instead asks the campaign manager to investigate those specific rows.
