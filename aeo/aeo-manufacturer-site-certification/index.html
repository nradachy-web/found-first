AEO for manufacturers answering site certification questions

Use AEO to answer “Is the facility quoting this work certified?” with the facility name, the scope on its certificate and a way to check the current document. Buyers comparing suppliers need to connect that evidence to the work under consideration.

Follow the address on the certificate

Start with the certificate held by the quality team. Check the named organization, covered locations, scope wording and validity information before writing the public answer. Place those details near the capability they qualify. A logo in the site footer gives a sourcing manager little to take back to a supplier review.

Account for jobs assigned after the inquiry

Some manufacturers choose the facility only after reviewing capacity and project fit. If that describes your company, explain when the proposed site will be identified on the quote. Give buyers a way to flag a facility-specific purchasing requirement before that decision is made.

The brochure covers both plants

Suppose a manufacturer's brochure lists two plants and a shared assembly service. Its current certificate names only the east plant. Marketing can attach the certification answer to the east plant's assembly page. If an inquiry goes to the west plant, sales should identify that site and confirm what evidence it can supply. If quality later provides a current certificate covering both locations and the relevant work, marketing can broaden the answer to match.
