ChatGPT advertising · Channel comparison and media allocation

Do ChatGPT ads add first-time class bookings alongside Meta?

Give ChatGPT ads a trial alongside Meta only if the workshop operator can tell whether it sold more first-time seats overall. Both channels can report activity around a booking the studio would have received anyway.

Count people at the registrar's desk

Use completed, paid bookings from the registration system. Identify returning students from prior rosters, remove refunds and assign each first-time student to the studio they booked. OpenAI's reported conversions depend on event setup and attribution. Adding them to Meta's reported bookings cannot settle how many additional students enrolled.

Give the new channel a fair comparison

Choose comparable workshops with room to sell and a similar history of first-time attendance. Keep Meta support and class terms steady. Follow changes from each studio's own baseline. A comparison is much harder to interpret if one studio adds weekend dates or if customers routinely switch between the studios.

Two photography studios run the same darkroom course

Suppose their schedules, waitlists and prior rosters show similar enrollment patterns. The operator promotes only the east studio's course page in a ChatGPT ad and keeps its regular Meta promotion at both locations. If first-time paid bookings rise at the east studio while the west stays steady, another trial is warranted. If east gains as west loses students from the same postcodes, the operator treats the result as a shift in venue and keeps the existing media split.
