Before you decide.

Will you publish legal tint percentages?+

Only where a specific current requirement is verified from an appropriate authoritative source and belongs in the content. General marketing should not invent a universal legal percentage. The service can explain the business's offering without turning the page into unsupported legal guidance.

Can one business market automotive and building tinting?+

Yes, if both are actual capabilities and the differences remain clear. Each audience should understand the relevant service and how to begin an inquiry. A shared brand can support separate buying contexts without forcing them into one generic installation message.

Can we use manufacturer performance claims?+

Approved claims need to retain the relevant product and conditions. A statement about one film should not become a promise about every option your business sells. Found First helps present that information clearly while preserving the factual boundaries your team and suppliers can support.
