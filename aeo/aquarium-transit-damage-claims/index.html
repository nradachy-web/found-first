AEO for aquarium retailers handling shipping damage

For a retailer shipping glass aquariums, the first AEO answer after a damaged delivery should say who takes the report and what evidence they need. Base it on how your orders are fulfilled so the buyer can act even if the carrier has already left.

Write for the moment the box is opened

Use your actual claim form to identify useful photos, such as the shipping label, carton and damaged panel. If staff need the packaging for a carrier inspection, say so while the buyer still has it. Avoid telling someone to move a damaged tank for a better photograph or mail broken glass back without instructions.

Choose the route by when the problem appeared

A crushed corner at delivery and a suspected seam fault after months of use call for different information. Let the buyer describe the timing if the cause is uncertain. Support can decide which process applies without asking a shopper to diagnose a manufacturing defect or establish fault.

The carton looks fine but the side panel is cracked

Imagine an aquarium shop whose freight account requires the retailer to open transit claims. Its unboxing leaflet asks customers to keep the carton and submit photos to the shop. A buyer who finds a cracked side panel as they open an intact box should get that reporting route, even with no visible carton damage. If their original delivery was sound and a seam issue appeared during use, the shop's product support form can collect the model, purchase reference and description for review.
