Questions worth asking.

How does an organizational engagement differ from a private booking?+

An organization may involve several decision makers and a broader allocation of responsibility. A private customer may be choosing for a single occasion. A provider offering both should preserve the practical differences so each buyer understands the service relationship being considered.

Can a College Station business seek year-round relationships?+

Yes. The objective should follow the company's real services and customers. A university setting does not require every business to define itself around a narrow occasion or audience when its operating model is broader.

What if a family researches the service from outside the city?+

They may need a clearer explanation because they lack local familiarity. The business should be understandable in terms of who receives the service, who arranges it and what responsibility the provider takes on, so direct contact starts with realistic expectations.
