ChatGPT advertising · Campaign diagnostics and reporting

Did a shift in creative mix lower ChatGPT ads click-through?

A campaign's click-through rate can fall while each ad's own rate holds steady. Before replacing ChatGPT ads creative, check which ads accounted for the impressions in each period.

Rebuild the campaign rate from counts

Lay out impressions and clicks for each ad across the two periods. Divide total clicks by total impressions to reproduce each campaign rate. An average of the individual percentages gives a seldom-shown ad the same influence as one that accounted for most of the displays. Keep newly introduced ads identifiable because they have no earlier rate to compare.

Test the effect of the mix

For ads present in both periods, apply each earlier click-through rate to that ad's later impression count. Add the implied clicks and divide by the later impressions for those same ads. If that estimate comes close to the later observed rate, the new distribution can explain much of the movement. Where an individual rate also falls, investigate that ad on its own evidence.

A brochure and a sample request

Imagine a flooring manufacturer running a brochure ad and a sample-request ad. Its saved reports show the sample ad historically gets fewer clicks per impression. It now accounts for most impressions, while each ad's own rate looks similar across periods. The manager keeps both messages and asks whether the shift serves the campaign objective. If the mix stayed similar and the brochure ad lost clicks per impression, she directs the review to the brochure ad instead.
