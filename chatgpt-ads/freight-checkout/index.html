ChatGPT advertising · Catalog campaigns and feed readiness

Do freight-only products fit a ChatGPT ads catalog campaign?

Use freight items in a purchase-focused ChatGPT ads catalog when the store can take the order on the terms advertised. When the seller must review access, calculate delivery and obtain approval before accepting the purchase, evaluate the item as a quote offer.

Trace the order beyond the cart

The presence of an add-to-cart button settles little for oversized goods. Try a representative delivery address, choose the applicable delivery service and follow the steps up to the order commitment. Read the shipping terms beside that flow. A charge for the product followed by an unknown freight bill is materially different from an order with a disclosed delivery cost.

Group products by fulfillment method

A single category can contain parcel items, freight items with published delivery terms and pieces that require a custom carrier arrangement. Give the catalog manager a selection based on how those orders close. If a customer must consent to a later quote, the campaign owner needs to know what the initial submission represents.

A kiln that requires a loading dock

Suppose a pottery supplier wants to promote electric kilns. Its freight sheet lists dock delivery, but a studio buyer choosing a residential address reaches a form asking for a liftgate quote. The supplier leaves that model out of a general buy-online assortment. If a smaller kiln ships under a fixed delivery schedule and checkout presents the applicable total before the buyer orders, that model can be considered separately. The change in checkout terms matters more than the shared kiln category.
