AEO for dash camera retailers selling installation separately

For a dash camera retailer, AEO copy should answer whether the camera arrives ready to plug in and whether anyone has agreed to fit it. Name the hardware in the order and explain the separate step that creates an installation appointment.

Read the contents list against the service listing

A hardwire kit in the box can easily sound like installation is included. If your store sells the cable and refers the labor to a workshop, identify who takes the booking and when the buyer will hear from them. Keep that answer visible where shoppers choose the kit as well as in the order confirmation.

Use the appointment record as the test

Only describe a fitting slot as booked when the system used by the installer records it as confirmed. A request for a call, a preferred day and successful payment for a camera can all exist while the workshop still needs to approve the job.

A parking mode kit with an optional fitting

Picture a retailer whose packing slip lists a camera and hardwire cable. Its workshop referral form asks for the vehicle trim, and its booking email names the fitting location. A shopper buys the kit and enters a preferred day. The order answer should confirm the hardware purchase and say the workshop will review the request. If a booking email has also been issued, the shopper can use its location and time. The dispatch notice for the parcel still answers only when the equipment is on its way.
