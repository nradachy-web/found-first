What should AEO clarify in a ceramic coating quote?

If your detailing shop sells ceramic coatings, use AEO to tell buyers exactly which surfaces each package covers. A customer comparing two quotes for the same coating may be looking at different amounts of preparation and different areas of the vehicle.

Name surfaces the way the installer does

Start with the work order. A shop that coats painted body panels and the outward-facing surfaces of wheels can say exactly that. Phrases like “entire exterior” leave buyers guessing about glass, trim, wheel barrels and surfaces behind badges. List a surface as an option only if the shop can quote and perform the work.

Describe the finish the customer is starting with

Buyers sometimes expect the coating appointment to remove existing swirls. State whether the package includes polishing, whether correction requires a separate assessment and whether existing marks can remain visible. Use a product care sheet to answer maintenance questions about the specific coating offered.

The wheels shown in the package photo

Imagine the shop's package photo shows freshly coated wheels off the car. The invoice behind the photo includes a separate wheel removal and barrel coating service, while the standard estimate lists wheel faces only. The marketing team captions the photo with the actual scope. Someone asking for coated faces can select the standard visit. Someone wanting the inside of each wheel coated gets a revised estimate and a slot that accommodates wheel removal.
