ChatGPT advertising · Regional campaigns and multibranch operations

Routing ChatGPT ads inquiries when branch territories overlap

Use the address where the work will happen to assign a regional ChatGPT ads inquiry. If a city belongs to several operators, the ad can invite a service-area check. Give the buyer a branch name once you have enough information to make the assignment.

Find the smallest useful location detail

Compare the territories in the operator agreements with the choices on your inquiry form. A town name may settle most requests. Where a street divides the territory, ask for the street address only when the town leaves a real ambiguity. A head office mailing address can send a job to the wrong crew.

Give disputed addresses one owner

Ask the marketing partner to walk an address on the boundary through intake. Which office will the buyer see, and who follows up if neither operator accepts the job? Keep an unresolved inquiry with one regional contact. Sending both branches the same request leaves the buyer to sort out two callbacks.

A cleaning inquiry across the river

Suppose a cleaning network has a signed territory map, a facilities inquiry form and a dispatch directory. A facilities manager works downtown but wants a warehouse cleaned across the river. The warehouse address puts the quote with the east branch. If the manager supplies only the billing address, regional intake asks for the site address before forwarding the scope. The ad can keep the shared city name in either case.
