Questions worth asking.

Should a technical company avoid specialist terms?+

No. Specialist language can be essential for the right audience. The goal is to make the business understandable at the point of discovery while preserving the distinctions an informed evaluator will need later.

Can AEO help with complex, low-volume engagements?+

Yes, if accurate consideration by a focused group of buyers matters commercially. A business does not need a mass-market offer for a misunderstanding about its expertise to be costly.

Does a university setting establish our technical authority?+

Your company’s own capabilities establish the commercial case. A local university provides context, while the buyer needs a clear understanding of your actual work, experience and any relationships you legitimately hold.
