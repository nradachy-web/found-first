ChatGPT advertising · Catalog campaigns and feed readiness

Which product categories should feed your ChatGPT ads?

Base the product selection for ChatGPT ads on what each item is, even when your store organizes departments around how people shop. A collection such as Coffee Bar can work well for browsing and still be too broad for a campaign meant to sell espresso machines.

Translate departments into inclusion rules

Review the store's category tree with the person who buys the merchandise. For every department proposed for paid promotion, inspect a few entries at its edges. A machine, a replacement handle and a cleaning brush may all belong under Espresso. Only one matches a brief for complete machines. Record which product types qualify when preparing the merchant's export.

Check the exceptions in both directions

Look for complete products filed under gifts or seasonal collections as well as accessories mixed into equipment. The question is whether the selection matches the commercial brief. Renaming a category in your store does not establish how OpenAI classifies a product or where an ad will appear.

Travel equipment hidden under gifts

Imagine a coffee retailer briefing an agency on portable espresso makers. Its Coffee Gear collection includes travel cases, while a working hand-powered brewer sits under Gifts for Travelers. The buyer compares the collection export with the purchasing sheet, includes the brewer and excludes the empty cases. If the brief later expands to replacement cases, the buyer can prepare a separate selection with case-specific descriptions. Moving cases into the original selection would otherwise change the offer the merchant approved.
