Questions worth asking.

Should a countywide industrial business name every target sector?+

Only sectors relevant to its actual capabilities. A shorter, accurate account can be more useful than a broad list that leaves fit unresolved. The buyer needs to understand why the company belongs in the assignment being considered.

Does countywide reach mean all services are available everywhere?+

Not automatically. Different assignments can justify different operating footprints. A company should preserve those distinctions so the buyer understands which relationship is available, especially when on-site work and remote expertise coexist.

Can a niche provider compete with a broader firm?+

A niche can be an advantage when it closely matches the customer’s requirement. The company should be understood for the depth it offers and the responsibility it accepts. Broad recognition is less important than being considered for work where the specialty matters.
