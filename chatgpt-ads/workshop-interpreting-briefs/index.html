ChatGPT ads for on-site workshop interpreting

For a language firm selling on-site interpretation, make a workshop planning session the advertised offer when the format affects staffing or equipment. A buyer who already has a complete assignment brief can go straight to an availability request.

Ask where the conversation happens

A language pair and a venue address leave out a crucial detail. The instructor may speak from a lectern, walk between worktables or take questions from several groups at once. Each format asks something different of the interpreter. Name the workshop types your team supports and tell the organizer what to bring, such as the run of show and a room sketch.

Make the planning session worth attending

Promise a defined work product you can actually supply, such as an annotated agenda showing where interpretation is needed and who owns the sound equipment. OpenAI says advertisers do not receive private user chats. Give the organizer a place to describe the workshop directly, including what participants need to hear and when they need to speak.

A dealer training day changes rooms

Imagine an agency reviewing a furniture maker's bilingual training agenda and classroom floor plan. For a seated presentation with scheduled questions and confirmed venue audio, it can offer an interpreter availability check against the existing brief. If the agenda shifts to simultaneous upholstery demonstrations in separate rooms, the agency would offer a planning consultation first. Its marked agenda would flag the overlapping sessions so the organizer could stagger them or request separate coverage.
