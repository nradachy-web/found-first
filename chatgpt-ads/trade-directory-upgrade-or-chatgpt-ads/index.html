ChatGPT advertising · Channel comparison and media allocation

Renew a trade directory upgrade or test ChatGPT ads?

A supplier can move the optional directory upgrade into ChatGPT ads when the basic listing preserves the lookup buyers need and the upgrade has little evidence of generating introductions. Read the renewal agreement before treating its entire fee as available media budget.

Separate supplier verification from discovery

A purchasing manager may check an association directory after an engineer has already specified the supplier. That lookup is commercially useful even if it never produces a referral click. Ask buyers where the directory entered their review. Then ask what extra visibility the upgrade purchased over an ordinary listing.

Compare introductions on equivalent terms

Set the directory's featured referrals beside accepted briefs that began elsewhere. Read enough detail to distinguish a named supplier lookup from a new specifier asking for help. For a ChatGPT ads trial, choose one comparison the product team can substantiate and use qualified sample requests as evidence of introductions.

A packaging supplier gets its renewal notice

Consider a supplier whose contract includes a member listing and an optional featured tile. Purchasing checklists call for the listing, while referral emails from the tile come from existing distributors looking for paperwork. The buyer keeps the listing and assigns the tile budget to ChatGPT ads for comparing grease-resistant paper stocks. If the renewal also gives featured suppliers access to a specifier sample program and shipping records tie that program to new accounts, the buyer renews the upgrade and seeks test funding elsewhere.
