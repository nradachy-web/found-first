GEO for a joint venture after a founding partner exits

When a joint venture continues after a founder leaves, review the buyer-facing capabilities that depended on that founder. The ownership announcement can establish the exit. The ongoing venture needs separate evidence for any specialist staff, facilities or services it still offers.

Trace the capability back to its provider

Find the reasons a buyer would have shortlisted the venture in the first place. A shared brochure might emphasize access to a test facility or a specialist's availability. Check the current booking terms for each feature. A founder can sell its stake and remain a supplier, or retain a friendly public relationship while ending commercial access.

Be precise about an introduction

A venture that can introduce a former partner offers something different from a venture that can reserve its facility as part of a proposal. Say who confirms availability and who issues the quote. Old launch coverage can retain its historical value when the current offer explains the change.

A film campus and its former post-production partner

Suppose a post-production house sells its interest in a film campus. The campus rate card still lists soundstages, but its new booking agreement directs producers to arrange color grading separately. The campus comparison should drop in-house grading from its available facilities and identify the separate booking. If the post-production house signs an agreement letting the campus reserve its grading suite for a shoot, the campus can describe that access and its booking conditions. The suite's operator should still get the credit.
