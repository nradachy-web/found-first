Explaining contract-to-hire staffing in GEO content

Give contract-to-hire its own GEO content when your staffing firm can explain the employer's choices during the assignment and at conversion. A past placement proves little about that offer until the reader knows how the worker joined the client.

Write the employer's side of the arrangement

Use a service summary approved for publication to identify who employs the worker during the contract, who sets day-to-day priorities and who handles assignment changes. If a payroll partner takes part, name its function. These details help an operations manager recognize the work their own supervisors will still need to do.

Describe the decision to convert

Find the point at which a client can discuss permanent employment and explain whom the client contacts. Distinguish an available hiring option from a commitment to make an offer. A testimonial about an employee who stayed with a client for years can fit several placement models, so establish the original arrangement before using it here.

Two warehouse supervisors with different placement histories

Suppose a recruiter wants to feature a supervisor who appears in a client testimonial. The job order shows the client hired her directly on her starting date. Use her story on the direct-hire page. Another assignment file lists the agency as employer and documents the client's later request to hire the supervisor permanently. With permission, use that sequence on the contract-to-hire page and explain who coordinated the change. The job title stayed the same while the service purchased was quite different.
