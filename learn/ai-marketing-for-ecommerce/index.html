Help AI-assisted shoppers understand why your product fits.

Ecommerce AEO helps shoppers and answer systems understand product fit. Accurate specifications, useful buying guides, availability, policies, and authentic customer evidence give a product a stronger foundation for discovery.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

A shopper can ask for a product suited to a very particular need: material, size, compatibility, durability, or use environment. If those attributes only appear in an image or are buried in unclear copy, the product is harder to evaluate.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Make the product facts explicit

Review specifications, variants, compatibility, availability, and shipping or return information. Keep visible information and applicable structured data consistent.

02 / Answer the comparison

Build guides around real use cases and tradeoffs. Explain which buyer each product serves, what it does not do, and what evidence supports performance claims.

03 / Measure beyond discovery

Review identifiable AI referrals, product engagement, carts, purchases, and returns where available. Changes in traffic quality need to be assessed against your own margin and customer experience.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • Which [product] works best for [specific use] and [constraint]?
  • What is the difference between [material or feature A] and [B]?
  • Will [product] fit or work with [existing item]?

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • A product-content and access review.
  • Buying-guide and comparison briefs.
  • Clear product-page recommendations.
  • A purchase-funnel measurement plan.

Before you get started.

Will every product need a separate guide?

No. Group products by genuine buying decisions. Useful category guidance can serve multiple products without repeating near-identical pages.

Do market conversion statistics predict our results?

No. Industry observations can show an opportunity, but your own products, traffic, and economics determine performance.

Can we combine AEO with paid campaigns?

Yes, when the business and products are eligible. Organic discovery and sponsored campaigns have separate scopes and measurements.
