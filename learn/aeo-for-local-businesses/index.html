Be easier to find when the question includes a place.

Local AEO helps a business communicate where it operates, what it does, and why a customer should consider it. Accurate profiles, specific service pages, and real customer evidence form the foundation.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

A local buyer often includes urgency, geography, and a precise need in the same question. A generic city page cannot answer all three. Conflicting hours or an exaggerated service area can turn visibility into a poor customer experience.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Make location facts consistent

Check the business name, contact details, hours, service area, and appointment process across owned pages and relevant profiles. Correct outdated information at the source.

02 / Explain the actual service

Answer scope, preparation, price drivers, availability, and fit. Use original work examples and permissioned customer evidence where relevant.

03 / Connect the inquiry to the job

Make calling, requesting a quote, or booking straightforward. Distinguish inquiries, qualified opportunities, booked work, and completed revenue in reporting.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • Who provides [service] in [actual service area]?
  • What should I ask before hiring a [provider]?
  • Does [business] handle [specific job or requirement]?

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • A business-fact and profile consistency review.
  • Service and location content priorities.
  • Quote or booking journey improvements.
  • A measurement plan for qualified local inquiries.

Before you get started.

Should we create a page for every nearby city?

Only when the business genuinely serves the area and the page offers distinct, helpful local information. Changing a city name in repeated copy is not enough.

Do reviews matter to buyers?

Authentic reviews help people evaluate a business. Use legitimate collection practices and never invent or selectively rewrite customer feedback.

Can you guarantee appearing in local AI answers?

No. We improve accurate, accessible information and observe results. Inclusion remains the platform's decision.
