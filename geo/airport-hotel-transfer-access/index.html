GEO for airport hotels comparing late-arrival transfers

For an airport hotel, scope GEO around the journey from the arrivals hall to reception. If late arrivals are the bookings you want, your content partner needs to know when a guest can board the transfer and what has to happen beforehand.

Anchor the clock to a place

A final pickup time has meaning only alongside a boarding point. Identify the terminal, whether passengers must move to another stop and whether the driver needs a call or a reservation. Make clear whether the published cutoff applies to calling dispatch, meeting the vehicle or leaving the airport.

Compare complete journeys

Airport distance alone cannot settle a choice between a hotel reached on foot and one served by a shuttle. Describe the usable walking connection or the transfer steps you can substantiate. If the shuttle contractor changes its timetable by season, attach the relevant period to the comparison.

A late flight into the international terminal

Suppose the desk manual permits check-in overnight, but the transport contractor's schedule shows a final departure at eleven from the domestic terminal. A terminal map places international arrivals in a separate building. The hotel can describe overnight reception and the transfer between terminals, while advising passengers who miss the departure to arrange their own ride. If dispatch confirms a prebooked international pickup after eleven, the writer can add that option with its reservation deadline and precise meeting point.
