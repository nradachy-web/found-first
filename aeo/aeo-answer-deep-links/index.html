AEO deep links for individual buyer questions

Give a buyer question a direct section link when its answer belongs on an existing page and still makes sense at the landing point. A short, shareable answer rarely needs a new page just to give sales a link.

Choose a landing point that includes the condition

Put the link target on the question heading. Read from that heading down, ignoring everything above it. If an essential condition appears only in the introduction, bring it into the answer or send readers to the start of the relevant section. A link that lands on “Yes, in that case” has skipped the very fact a recipient needs.

Keep the address through copy edits

Ask the publisher whether revising a heading also changes its section identifier. Open a previously shared address after the revision and check where it lands on a narrow screen. A move to a dedicated page makes sense when the question has its own prerequisites, supporting materials and next action.

A link production managers can forward

Suppose a print shop has an artwork-preparation guide, a folded-leaflet template and a preflight checklist. Account managers need to share the answer to “Where do I mark the fold?” Link to the guide heading that names folded leaflets and points to the marked template. If the shop adds custom folds that require an approved drawing before artwork is prepared, give custom folds a separate instruction page. Sending both jobs to the standard template would invite the wrong submission.
