Field notes/Measuring AI marketing results

Nice charts. Where is the business?

AI marketing results should mean something outside a presentation.

AI marketing results deserve a harder question than whether the chart went up. What changed for your business? Found First keeps that question in view, with a clear distinction between attention and actual commercial outcomes.

By Found First/Updated November 12, 2026

On this page
  • You cannot pay the team in impressions
  • Big claims should survive a small question
  • Commercial ambition is the point
  • Questions before we talk

You cannot pay the team in impressions

A chart is not a customer.

Attention has a role. So does visibility. But you run a business with customers, obligations and goals that exist outside a dashboard. You should be able to talk about marketing in that language and expect the people doing the work to understand it.

Big claims should survive a small question

What does that number actually mean? It is a reasonable question. So is asking what it does not tell you.

Found First is comfortable with both. We do not dress a mention up as a sale or give a confident label to an unknown. The work should stand up to a direct question from the person paying for it.

Commercial ambition is the point

You want to be considered, chosen and remembered. You want the reputation you have earned to matter in more places. That is a business goal worth taking seriously.

Found First keeps the purpose in view. Your market, your customers and the company you want to become belong at the center of the discussion. A great-looking chart should never be the most impressive thing about the relationship.

Questions about what results mean

Is an AI mention a lead?+

No. Being named is different from receiving an inquiry, and an inquiry is different from a completed sale.

Can you attribute every sale to AI?+

No. We do not claim to know the source of every sale or give unknown outcomes a convenient label.

Do you guarantee a return?+

No. Found First does not guarantee mentions, rankings, leads or revenue.
