Writing ChatGPT ads for gifts the recipient customizes

Lead with what the gift buyer can settle today when the recipient will make the personal choices later. Feature the recipient’s experience when the giver already understands how to purchase and needs a reason to choose this gift.

Assign each choice to a person

Read the order screen and the redemption instructions side by side. Who selects the model, supplies a measurement, books the appointment or pays for optional extras? A photo of a fully customized item can imply that the giver must specify every detail. Use wording that tells them which choices can wait until after the gift is opened.

Keep both versions attached to the gift

A recipient-led description can still speak to the purchaser. Describe what their recipient gets to choose and keep the purchase action visible. Sending that version to a redemption form would test a different transaction and could leave prospective givers looking for a code they do not have.

A made-to-measure belt without guessing a size

Suppose a leatherworker sells a gift kit with a presentation card and measuring strip. The order form asks for a shipping address, while the enclosed card lets the recipient choose leather and buckle later. Test “Give a fitted belt without guessing their size” against copy about choosing the leather and buckle, with both ads linking to the kit. If a limited leather must be reserved at purchase, name that choice in the giver’s message and picture a finish the recipient can still select.
