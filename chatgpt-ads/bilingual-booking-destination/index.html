ChatGPT advertising · Landing page decisions after the ad

Which language should a bilingual business use after a ChatGPT ads click?

Link each language version of your ChatGPT ad to the same offer in that language. Follow the path through the form, confirmation and first staff reply before deciding which action to feature on the page.

Preserve the choice the visitor already made

Use the ad’s own language as the starting point. Browser settings are a poor substitute for that explicit choice on a shared device. Keep a language switch visible and have it open the equivalent offer page. Sending someone to the other language’s homepage makes them find the service all over again.

Handle the handoff in plain language

A translated booking button can hide a change of language at the vendor’s checkout. Check field labels, error messages and the confirmation email with a fluent reviewer. If part of the process runs in another language, explain where the switch happens. Feature an assisted option only if someone can actually handle those requests in the advertised language.

Spanish enrollment for a music school

Consider a music school with a Spanish lesson page, an English-only scheduler and a bilingual coordinator. Its Spanish ad can open the lesson page with a Spanish callback form as the primary action. Families comfortable using English can still choose the labeled scheduler. If the vendor adds Spanish booking, the school can promote direct scheduling after its reviewer successfully corrects a form error, books a lesson and receives the Spanish confirmation.
