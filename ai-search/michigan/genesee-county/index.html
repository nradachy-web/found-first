Questions worth asking.

Should we use Flint as shorthand for all of Genesee County?+

It can be a regional reference, but it should not replace accurate location and coverage information. Buyers in other communities need to understand their relationship to the offer rather than infer it from a city association.

Do city and township names matter to buyers?+

They can, especially when similar names refer to distinct municipalities. A company should preserve an accurate address while describing its service area separately. The customer needs practical clarity rather than a geography lesson before requesting work.

Can we expand countywide without opening another office?+

Yes, when the service model supports the additional reach. Coverage and physical locations are different claims. The business should make the real operating arrangement understandable and avoid implying a local establishment in every community it serves.
