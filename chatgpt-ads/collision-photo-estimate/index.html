ChatGPT ads for collision estimates from photos

Advertise a photo estimate in ChatGPT only if an estimator uses the images to produce the stated deliverable. If photos help the shop choose an appointment but the customer receives no estimate, sell an estimator review and explain what the call will settle.

Read the reply that follows the upload

An intake form asking for pictures does not establish what the shop sends back. Check a blank estimate form and the estimator’s reply template. A written preliminary scope based on visible damage gives the owner something to compare. A request to bring the vehicle in gives them an appointment. Each can be worth advertising under its own name.

Keep insurer approval out of the shop’s promise

Name the shop as the author of its estimate. If the owner has opened a claim, ask whether they want the estimator to review an existing document. A shop’s opinion on visible damage and an insurer’s decision about payment answer different questions.

A bumper photo leaves the mounting area out of view

Suppose a body shop’s estimator marks visible bumper-cover damage on a preliminary worksheet and flags concealed areas for inspection. Its ad can offer that written photo estimate and explain the limit. Now suppose the owner sends only a close-up with no view of the affected panel as a whole. The estimator requests a wider image or schedules an inspection. A shop whose worksheet always requires an in-person inspection should advertise the inspection from the outset, even if it collects the same photos for preparation.
