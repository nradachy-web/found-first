Brake inspections or pad replacements in auto repair ChatGPT ads?

Buy ChatGPT advertising for a brake inspection when customers usually arrive with a symptom. Advertise a pad replacement when the shop can define the supported vehicle, axle and scope well enough to accept that specific job.

Use the authorization line to name the offer

Read what the customer signs at check-in. If it authorizes the technician to examine the vehicle and report findings, the shop is selling diagnostic time. A driver who books a “brake job” may expect parts to be ready and repairs to begin immediately. Tell that driver who will explain the findings and when they can approve further work.

Decide how to treat someone else’s estimate

A parts list from another garage can make an inquiry more specific. It only supports a replacement booking if your service adviser accepts that evidence under shop policy. When the technician must make an independent assessment, offer to review the document at the inspection.

One sedan, two different repair orders

Consider a service adviser looking at a message about a squeal and a blank brake inspection form. The adviser reserves diagnostic time and leaves the repair authorization open. Now give the adviser the shop’s own recent inspection for that sedan, a written recommendation for rear pads and confirmation that matching parts are available. A paid offer inviting owners to schedule previously quoted brake work fits this second case. The adviser can retrieve the quote and ask the owner to authorize the specified repair.
