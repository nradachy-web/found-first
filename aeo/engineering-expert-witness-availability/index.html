AEO for engineering expert witness availability inquiries

An engineering firm's expert witness page should explain how to check a specialist's availability for a particular assignment. Commission AEO copy that answers what the inquirer can establish today, especially when a lawyer has a hearing date and needs a named person.

Match the question to the specialist

Identify the technical subjects the expert actually covers and the engagement types the firm accepts. An approved biography might establish experience with equipment failures, while an intake sheet distinguishes private technical consulting from testimony. A list of publications alone gives a buyer little indication of which requests the firm will consider.

Say what the date field accomplishes

If submitting a date prompts staff to check a calendar, label the action “Request an availability check.” Specify whether an estimated window is useful when a date is unsettled. Only describe a date as reserved at the step where the specialist has actually accepted it under the firm's procedure.

A lawyer has a hearing date involving a pump failure

Imagine the mechanical engineer's profile documents pump failure analysis, and the assignment sheet calls for separate confirmation of hearing attendance. Give the lawyer a route to supply the technical subject, hearing date and requested role. Staff can then ask the named engineer about that assignment. If the lawyer needs only an internal technical briefing, ask for the briefing deadline instead. Requiring a hearing date would exclude a request the same engineer could consider.
