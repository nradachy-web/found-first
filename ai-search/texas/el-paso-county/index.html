Questions worth asking.

Should a county service page simply repeat the city description?+

No. The county can represent a wider operating relationship, while the city identifies a more specific place. The business should explain any meaningful difference in reach, service availability or customer arrangements rather than treat the labels as interchangeable.

Can regional infrastructure make a narrow provider relevant?+

Yes, when the provider's actual capability serves a particular customer need connected to that location. Proximity alone is not the advantage. The important distinction is the role the company can perform reliably within the wider engagement.

What if our service area follows customer facilities rather than city boundaries?+

That can be a sensible operating model. The public identity should make the relationship understandable without pretending the company offers every service throughout each surrounding city. Specific reach can be more useful than indiscriminate regional coverage.
