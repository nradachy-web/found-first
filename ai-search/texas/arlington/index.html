Questions worth asking.

Should event-related visibility be the whole objective?+

Only if the business model supports that focus. A company seeking recurring local relationships should not let visitor-oriented recognition conceal its everyday service. The right balance depends on the customers and commitments the company actually wants.

What if we serve both individual and organizational buyers?+

Their needs should remain distinguishable. A simple personal booking and an organizational arrangement can involve different decision makers and expectations. Being able to serve both is valuable only if each buyer can understand the relevant offer.

Can proximity alone make a business a useful recommendation?+

Proximity may help, but it does not establish suitability. A nearby provider could lack the capacity, specialization or service arrangement the buyer needs. An effective commercial objective looks beyond appearing close to a landmark or venue.
