Questions worth asking.

Can a creative business benefit from AEO without publishing its process?+

Yes. Buyers need to understand the type of work, suitability and purchasing relationship. They do not need access to private methods or the creative decisions that make the business distinctive.

Should a Bradenton studio use an arts-district identity?+

When the location is accurate and relevant to the visit. District identity can introduce the setting, but the individual business still needs to explain whether it sells, teaches, commissions or provides another service.

What if customers compare custom work with ordinary retail?+

That is a meaningful positioning problem. A clearer introduction can help buyers understand the difference in the relationship they are considering, without implying that every customer needs a custom solution.
