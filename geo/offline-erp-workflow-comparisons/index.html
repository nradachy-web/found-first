GEO for ERP vendors comparing offline inventory workflows

Use GEO to qualify 'works offline' by the inventory task your ERP supports. For a warehouse buyer, reading a cached pick list, recording a movement and posting the resulting stock change are separate capabilities with different consequences.

Show when a scan reaches the central ledger

Describe what appears on the handheld while disconnected and when the central ledger reflects the change. The mobile guide can establish which fields staff can enter. The synchronization guide should establish what happens to queued entries, who sees a rejected movement and whether staff can correct it without rekeying the whole transaction.

Pick the exception a warehouse manager would ask about

A clean demonstration often follows one worker handling one item. For a buyer with shared bins, the more useful comparison is what happens when two devices allocate the same remaining stock. Only claim automatic resolution if the documented behavior covers that conflict. Otherwise, identify the decision a supervisor has to make.

A forklift driver scans a transfer in a dead zone

Suppose a vendor's handheld manual allows transfer scans without a signal. A sync log shows a queued transfer rejected because another device has moved the item, followed by a supervisor correction. A GEO page can present offline capture with exception review to buyers who can staff that review. If the manual permits only viewing downloaded assignments until the device reconnects, change the comparison to offline viewing. A depot that requires scans during the outage would need a different workflow.
