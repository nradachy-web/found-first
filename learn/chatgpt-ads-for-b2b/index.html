Start with the buyer problem, not the size of ChatGPT

A large platform audience does not establish demand for your particular offer. The useful question is whether a prospective customer could encounter a relevant business problem and recognize your service or product as a credible next step. That requires a more specific proposition than being a technology company, consultant or supplier.

For example, a hypothetical business selling scheduling software and one providing outsourced finance operations face different buying decisions. One prospect may need to compare product capabilities; another may need to trust a service relationship. Both are B2B, but the same ad promise and destination would not serve them equally well. Your offer has to make its own case.

Business use does not mean access to every business user

OpenAI says ads may appear on eligible Free and Go experiences. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts do not have ads. That makes total ChatGPT usage a poor substitute for the audience your campaign can actually reach.

A campaign also cannot be treated as a list of guaranteed conversations. OpenAI describes contextual ad selection and says advertiser context hints do not guarantee placement in a specific conversation. If your acquisition plan depends on reliably reaching named accounts or particular job titles, that requirement needs to be examined before this channel is given a role. Found First evaluates the available channel against the buyer you need, rather than assuming B2B reach from the platform's popularity.

Match the destination to the decision the ad creates

Someone exploring a business problem may be ready to compare options without being ready for a contract. A destination that immediately demands a sales meeting can create friction if it has not explained why that meeting would be valuable. The opposite problem is a page that explains everything except how to speak with the company.

The appropriate next step depends on your sale. A product demonstration, a scoped consultation and a technical inquiry each ask the buyer to make a different commitment. Found First connects the paid message with website and content improvements where needed, so the person arriving can understand the offer and choose a relevant action.

A longer sales cycle changes what early progress means

An inquiry is evidence that someone took an action. It becomes a more useful commercial signal when your team confirms the need, the company's fit and whether there is a realistic next conversation. An opportunity and a signed customer come later. Reporting should preserve those differences.

This is especially important when several people influence a purchase. A person researching options may introduce your company to a colleague who eventually contacts sales through another route. That possibility is a reason to interpret evidence carefully, not permission to credit every later deal to the ad. Early inquiry quality can inform a decision while revenue remains unresolved.

Before you decide.

Is this only for B2B software companies?+

No. Software is one example of a B2B offer. Services and other business products can also be considered where current advertiser eligibility and a credible customer use case support them. The fit conversation starts with the purchase decision, not a software label.

Can ChatGPT Ads work with a long sales cycle?+

A long cycle does not settle the fit question either way. It changes how progress should be assessed. Relevant inquiries and qualified conversations can provide earlier evidence, while opportunity development and revenue need the time and business records appropriate to your sale.

Should we replace our established paid channels?+

This page does not establish a reason to replace a channel already producing valuable business. Evaluate ChatGPT Ads against a specific gap or opportunity in your acquisition mix. Your current results, available budget and ability to follow up should inform that decision.

Does advertising make ChatGPT recommend our business in its answers?+

No. OpenAI states that ads are separate from answers and do not influence them. Found First offers organic AI search visibility as a separate service for businesses concerned with how they appear in independent research and recommendations.
