ChatGPT ads for enterprise AI software security reviews

A security review makes sense as an enterprise AI software offer when technical approval stands between a buyer and a pilot. Name the deployment option under review and tell the evaluator which evidence is available before asking them to arrange a meeting.

Offer materials a reviewer can use

Work from the current deployment diagram, data-flow description and list of components involved in the proposed feature. If some material requires a confidentiality agreement, say what the buyer can inspect before that step. An ad about reviewing a hosted service should lead to evidence for that service, even if the vendor also sells a customer-hosted edition.

Give the meeting an owner and a question

Ask which deployment the buyer is evaluating and what its reviewer needs to decide. Route a question about where files are processed to someone who can explain the relevant path. Product discovery belongs earlier if the buyer has yet to choose a use case or deployment model.

Following an uploaded file beyond the model call

Imagine a vendor preparing a review offer for a hosted document assistant. Its architecture drawing traces the upload and model call but omits temporary files created during conversion. An evaluator asks who can access those files. The vendor holds the broad review offer until the service owner documents that step, then gives reviewers the revised drawing and access description. If all the buyer needs now is to see how the assistant answers questions from a sample document, marketing directs them to a product demonstration.
