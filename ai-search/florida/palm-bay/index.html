Questions worth asking.

Should a Palm Bay company list every related industry it could serve?+

Only where the claim reflects a real offer. A long industry list can make a specialist sound broad without helping buyers judge suitability. Relevant applications matter more than theoretical adjacency.

Can AEO preserve technical distinctions without publishing proprietary information?+

Yes. Buyers need to understand the type of work and the relationship they are considering. That does not require access to private designs, manufacturing methods or operational details.

What if prospects know the product but not the service around it?+

That is a useful visibility question. Supply, design support and ongoing service can represent different offers around the same product. A clearer introduction helps buyers identify which relationship your company provides.
