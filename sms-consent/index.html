SMS Alert Consent Form

The written consent form Found First staff sign before they receive internal alert texts.

Version 3, November 18, 2026

Who signs it.

Found First Internal Alerts go only to Found First's two owner-operators. There is no public sign-up. Each recipient writes their own mobile number on this form and signs it, and an administrator enables the number only after the signed form is on file. Signed copies are kept on file. The copy below is blank.

The form.

Download the blank form (PDF)

The form's fields.

Each recipient completes these fields on the form and signs it. The SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default and optional. This is a preview of the form; staff complete and sign the form itself through Dropbox Sign, and this page does not collect or send anything.

By signing this form and providing my mobile number, I agree to receive SMS account notification and customer care messages from Found First (Be Found First LLC): internal alerts that a client work plan is ready for review or that an internal job needs attention. Message frequency varies, up to 8 messages a day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, and START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. My mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

Once the signed form is on file and the number is enabled, the recipient texts START to the Found First alert number and receives this confirmation:
