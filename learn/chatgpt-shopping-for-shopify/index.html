Start with the connection Shopify already provides

Shopify’s current documentation says eligible stores have a discovery connection through Shopify Catalog and do not need to maintain a separate ChatGPT product feed for that purpose. Eligibility and product restrictions still apply. The business should understand its actual status before buying a project framed as necessary access.

The commercial implication is straightforward: an agency needs to explain what it will improve beyond the platform baseline. A useful engagement might address unclear product positioning or a weak destination. It should not imply that the merchant is absent solely because a bespoke feed has not been commissioned.

The store still carries the buying experience

Shopify describes ChatGPT as a discovery referrer, with purchases completed in the store’s checkout. That keeps the merchant’s own product pages and purchase experience important. A shopper arriving with a particular expectation needs to find the matching product and understand the offer.

Catalog access does not resolve unclear variant descriptions, weak differentiation or contradictory purchase expectations. Those are business and website questions. Found First’s value can lie in connecting organic discovery with the experience the merchant controls, so the product explanation remains useful when the shopper moves from comparison to a purchase decision.

Keep profiles, agents and ads as separate choices

Shopify distinguishes business profiles and Business Agents from ads. It also describes Business Agents as limited to eligible stores based in the United States and says they do not complete purchases. These capabilities should not be bundled into an assumption that catalog participation activates every business feature.

Found First provides organic AI visibility, supporting website improvements and managed ChatGPT advertising. A profile or agent opportunity deserves a separate fit assessment, with the current capability confirmed for the store. The discussion should establish the buyer benefit and actual responsibility before promising a new customer conversation experience.

Choose work that improves the merchant’s real position

A merchant can have catalog access while still being difficult to distinguish from similar sellers. The useful question is whether the product, brand and offer are represented accurately and whether suitable shoppers reach a coherent destination. Baseline availability is different from a guaranteed appearance for every shopping question.

A conversation with Found First can clarify the gap between what Shopify already supplies and what the business needs. The scope should focus on the remaining commercial problem, whether that is product clarity, organic visibility or a separate advertising objective. It should not create another integration simply to make the project sound more technical.

Before you decide.

Do I need another product feed just to be discoverable?+

Shopify says eligible products already use Shopify Catalog for ChatGPT discovery, without a separately maintained feed for that purpose. Your store and product eligibility should still be checked. Additional work needs a distinct objective beyond duplicating that connection.

Does the Business Agent take payment for Shopify customers?+

Shopify’s current guidance says the Business Agent supports discovery and product questions but does not complete purchases. The documented shopping path uses the merchant’s own checkout. These functions should be evaluated separately.

Can Found First guarantee that Shopify products appear?+

No. Catalog eligibility does not guarantee selection for every shopper or question. Found First can assess product representation and the website experience, while treating actual appearances and commercial outcomes as observations rather than promised entitlements.
