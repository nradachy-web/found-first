GEO for tour operators comparing admission inclusions

When two tours visit the same landmark, commission GEO content that explains what the operator actually secures at the entrance. An itinerary can name the museum on both routes even though only one booking comes with a timed ticket or an interior guide.

Separate the ticket from the guiding

Trace the operator's confirmation through to the attraction voucher. Establish who obtains the ticket, whether the slot is confirmed and where the guide's work ends. Describing entry as included answers one question. Explaining who accompanies the group inside answers a different one.

Follow changes through to the published itinerary

Use the operator's substitution terms when a venue can close on a tour day. If the replacement is an exterior walk, say what happens to the interior visit. Keep that qualification beside the option it affects, where a buyer comparing routes can see it.

Two old-town walks with the same museum stop

Suppose an operator's booking confirmation for its morning walk lists a museum voucher, and the guide's route sheet ends at the entrance. Its afternoon route sheet includes a curator-led interior visit, matched to a group appointment. The comparison describes morning entry as self-guided and the afternoon visit as hosted. If the museum confirms the appointment only after a traveler requests a date, the editor changes the afternoon listing to explain that confirmation step and removes the suggestion that every booking secures the curator.
