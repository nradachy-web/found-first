AEO for commercial roofers bidding a specified system

If your commercial roofing firm bids from specifications, use AEO to explain what happens when the owner's bid form names a system. The reader should be able to tell whether your estimator can quote the requested scope and how to ask about another option.

Use the buyer's bid form as the starting point

Compare the service description with an invitation your firm was prepared to answer. A list of systems your crews have installed gives buyers background. It leaves open the more immediate question of whether you will complete their base bid as written. Explain what estimating needs to check, including the specification section and any addendum that changes it.

Describe how an alternative appears in the response

Where the invitation allows alternatives, describe how your firm identifies the proposed departure so the buyer can find the requested base scope. A project photograph showing a similar roof is a poor basis for calling two systems interchangeable. Leave acceptance of the proposed change with the party named in the invitation.

The alternate line on a distribution center bid

Suppose a roofer's sample bid form names a membrane system and has a separate line for proposed alternatives. Its estimator has a redacted response that keeps the specified system in the base bid. The writer can illustrate that distinction with the two labeled entries. If the next owner's instructions require the named system and omit an alternate route, the page should direct that owner to a review of the specified package instead of inviting a substitute by default.
