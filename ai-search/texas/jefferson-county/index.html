Questions worth asking.

Should a Jefferson County provider name every industry it could serve?+

Only where that accurately represents its capabilities and intended work. Theoretical application is different from proven suitability. A focused explanation of relevant expertise can be more persuasive than a broad list that leaves the buyer uncertain about depth.

Can a company be regionally visible without offering every service locally?+

Yes. Geographic reach and service breadth are separate facts. A specialist can accept selected work across the county while maintaining a narrow capability. The public identity should keep those distinctions understandable.

What if purchasing staff and technical staff evaluate us differently?+

Both need to recognize the same business. The initial explanation should be intelligible to the purchaser while preserving the substance technical evaluators will examine. A broad category alone may be insufficient for either perspective.
