ChatGPT advertising · SaaS product-led and sales-led offers

Should CRM ChatGPT ads offer a trial or a demo?

Use a trial in CRM ChatGPT ads when a new user can complete the sales task the ad describes. If the result depends on an administrator configuring territories, permissions or routing, offer a demo that shows those dependencies.

Try the advertised task from a fresh account

Give someone outside the sales team the ad copy and the standard welcome email. Ask them to carry out the task using only what a trial user receives. Seeing a pipeline full of sample deals is a different experience from moving a deal, assigning its next action and finding that action in the new owner's queue.

Choose the offer before defining success

For a trial offer, distinguish account creation from completion of the advertised task. For a demo offer, have sales record whether the buyer actually reviewed the workflow. OpenAI's conversion reporting depends on the event and attribution setup. A signup count alone tells the marketing team little about which path buyers could use.

A rep can test follow-up, but who can test routing?

Suppose a CRM vendor has a starter workspace, a routing setup sheet and a demo environment with two territories. In the starter workspace a rep can advance a sample deal and schedule a follow-up. The vendor can sponsor that task with a trial. If the offer changes to routing a reassigned account across territories, the setup sheet shows that territory rules require admin access. The vendor switches that offer to a guided demo and shows what happens to the open follow-up after reassignment.
