Questions worth asking.

Should an Orlando mailing address be described as a city office?+

The actual location should determine the claim. Mailing labels and municipal boundaries can differ. A business can accurately use its address while explaining its service area without implying a different physical location.

Can AEO help a business serving unincorporated Orange County?+

Yes. The relevant issue is whether buyers can recognize genuine service eligibility and suitability. An unincorporated location does not need to be presented as a separate office or forced into an inaccurate city identity.

Should countywide positioning replace neighborhood recognition?+

No. A community name can help a buyer orient themselves, while the county describes a wider footprint. The strongest position makes those references work together in a clear account of the actual service.
