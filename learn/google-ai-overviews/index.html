Build for Google AI Overviews and AI Mode.

Google says the same core search practices apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode. A page must be indexed and eligible for a search snippet. Found First focuses on accessible content, clear answers, internal links, and credible evidence.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

It is easy to buy a new acronym while leaving ordinary search problems unresolved. An important page may be excluded from indexing, weakly linked, or missing the answer itself. Resolve those issues before assuming a special AI treatment is needed.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Review eligibility and access

Check indexability, canonical URLs, robots directives, and whether the important content is actually available on the page. Avoid conflicting instructions that make the preferred URL unclear.

02 / Help the reader compare

Use informative headings, direct explanations, and supporting details. Connect related pages through useful internal links and maintain accurate visible facts.

03 / Interpret the available reporting

Review Search Console and on-site outcomes. Google's guidance groups AI feature traffic into the Web search type, so do not label every organic click an AI referral.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • Can this page be indexed and shown with a snippet?
  • Does the page answer a question beyond the basic keyword?
  • Which follow-up question should the reader be able to answer next?

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • Indexability and content access checks.
  • Page improvements and internal-link recommendations.
  • A reporting note that distinguishes direct observations from aggregate search data.

Before you get started.

Do I need an llms.txt file for Google AI?

Google says no new AI text file or special markup is required for its AI features. We prioritize documented requirements and useful content.

Will these changes guarantee inclusion?

No. Eligibility and good content do not guarantee indexing, ranking, or use in an AI response.

Can you separate every AI Overview visit?

The reporting described in Google's guidance does not provide that simple separation. We explain the available data rather than inventing attribution.
