You did not build this to be an afterthought

The best business nobody has heard of still has a problem.

There is a reason your best customers choose you. There is a reason you are pushing the business forward. You have put too much into the company to accept being the overlooked option as its permanent position.

Your reputation should travel further

The work, the standards, the experience: they mean something. You should be able to look at your presence in the market and recognize the business behind it.

Found First is interested in the names that deserve a bigger stage and the owners prepared to go after one. We want your presence to carry the weight of what you have built.

Bring a business worth talking about

What makes you hard to replace? What do customers appreciate once they know you? What should the rest of the market understand before it makes a decision?

Those questions deserve your own answers. Your company has strengths a competitor cannot simply borrow. We want to hear them, understand where you want to go and decide whether Found First is the right team beside you.

Questions about getting named

Can Found First guarantee a ChatGPT mention?+

No. Found First does not guarantee mentions, rankings or leads.

Is a mention the same as an endorsement?+

No. A business can be named in different contexts. We do not treat every mention as praise or as a customer won.

Where do we discuss the work and pricing?+

Directly with us. Scope and commercial terms are discussed privately and provided before you commit.
