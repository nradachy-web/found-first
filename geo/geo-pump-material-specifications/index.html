GEO for distributors publishing pump material specifications

Give your GEO editor a source for each component named in a pump material claim. A buyer comparing pumps for a process line can reasonably read “stainless steel pump” as a statement about every surface the fluid touches.

Start with the part a buyer can order

Ask the editor to trace a listing from your stock code through the manufacturer's ordering key to the materials schedule for that build. A family brochure can establish which options exist. A photograph might help someone identify the housing. Neither settles which impeller or seal comes with the item in your catalog.

Give the material a component name

A comparison table can carry separate entries for casing, impeller and seal, along with the manufacturer's document title and revision. Where the schedule leaves a component open, say what information your applications desk needs to identify it. Keep chemical suitability as a separate inquiry that takes the fluid and operating conditions into account.

One casing, two possible impellers

Suppose your branch lists a pump as stainless because its casing appears that way in a supplier feed. The current materials schedule confirms a stainless casing and shows two impeller options. Your purchasing record contains the suffix for the polymer impeller, so the editor can publish that exact combination. If purchasing drops the suffix when replenishing stock, retain the casing description and route impeller questions through a build-code check before quoting.
