ChatGPT advertising · Catalog campaigns and feed readiness

Which compatibility claims belong in a ChatGPT ads feed?

Limit fit claims in a ChatGPT ads catalog to the model and revision your evidence covers. A supplier listing that names an entire product family can overstate compatibility when a familiar model name spans different connectors or dimensions.

Find the detail that divides compatible from incompatible

Ask the category buyer for the manufacturer's parts diagram, revision chart or fit table for the item on hand. Identify the detail a customer can check before ordering, such as the model suffix on a label. Carry that qualifier into the merchant's catalog description and destination. A family name in the title is little help if the essential exception is buried in an installation manual.

Know when a lookup is the offer

Some parts require a serial-number check by the seller before anyone can confirm fit. If the product cannot be identified from the information a buyer has at checkout, consider a compatibility inquiry or promote other items with documented fit. A paid catalog entry does not resolve an ambiguous parts record.

Two mixer revisions share a product name

Suppose a kitchen equipment dealer sells a bowl guard for a countertop mixer. The supplier sheet names the mixer family, but an exploded parts diagram shows a different attachment on the later revision. The dealer includes the guard only after the listing names the supported suffix and shows buyers where to find it. If the diagram is inconclusive for units sold during the changeover, the dealer sends those buyers through a fit check before offering the part.
