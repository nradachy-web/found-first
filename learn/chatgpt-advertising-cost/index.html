A number cannot tell you who to trust

A price tag is not a reason to believe.

It can tell you what someone is asking. It cannot tell you whether they understand your business or whether you want them representing your brand. Your company deserves more consideration than a quick comparison between three boxes on a pricing page.

Bring the business behind the budget

You are choosing a team to put beside a business you have worked hard to build. The people matter. Their judgment matters. So does whether they understand what makes your company worth choosing.

We want to know what you have built, why your customers care and what you expect next. It gives both sides a reason to decide whether there is a fit.

Straight answers belong at the table

Private pricing does not mean vague terms. If we propose an engagement, you receive its scope and commercial terms before making a commitment.

Found First is for owners who take their brand seriously and expect the same from the people beside them. We are comfortable talking directly about the work and its value. You should be comfortable with the decision you make.

Questions about the investment

How much does Found First charge?+

Pricing is discussed privately after we understand the business and the proposed engagement. We do not publish a rate card.

Will I know the terms before committing?+

Yes. The proposed scope and commercial terms are set out before you commit.

Can you promise a cost per lead or a return?+

No. Found First does not guarantee a cost per lead, lead volume or return.
