The internet has enough filler

If your name can be swapped for a competitor's, the copy has failed.

Your business has opinions, experience and standards that a generic paragraph cannot fake. Another page of interchangeable claims is a small ambition for something you have spent years building. You have a point to make. Make it yours.

Sound like the company you intend to become

The safe version of your brand may be comfortable. It may also be forgettable. A stronger presence calls for the confidence to say something that belongs to you.

We care about the difference people should notice: the conviction behind your offer, the authority behind your name and the reason a buyer would remember you tomorrow.

Leave an impression worth coming back to

Your content should carry the same confidence as your best conversation with a customer who finally gets it. Clear, recognizable and worth their time.

Found First brings that standard to AEO content strategy. We want your presence to feel like a company that knows who it is, what it stands for and why it deserves to be taken seriously.

Questions about the direction

Is this for a new brand or an established business?+

Either can be a fit. What matters is a real offer and the willingness to present the business with conviction.

Do you publish your content playbook?+

No. The work for an engagement is discussed privately with the business it is for.

Can content guarantee AI mentions or sales?+

No. Found First does not guarantee mentions, rankings or leads.
