ChatGPT advertising · Creative and message testing

Testing ChatGPT ads with one approved message

A single approved ChatGPT ad can reveal what happens while one message runs. Use those observations to decide what to change next. Choosing a winning headline requires a credible comparison with another headline.

Keep a record of what buyers actually saw

Save the approved wording, image, destination and offer terms together. Record the inquiry or purchase you meant to earn. If a customer later asks for something different, a dated copy of the ad and page lets the team trace where that expectation could have arisen.

Read the evidence at the right level

OpenAI’s conversion reporting depends on the events an advertiser sets up and on attribution. A reported submission can help count responses. To learn whether the request matched the offer, review the resulting inquiry where your own records support the connection. Sparse or unlinked inquiries leave the reason for the result open.

One approved line for a music licensing library

Imagine a music library whose rights team has approved one sponsored line offering tracks for podcasts. Its license sheet covers recorded episodes only. If inquiries linked to the offer ask about livestream rights, inspect the ad and the linked rights summary. If the page clearly states the restriction and the ad leaves it open, seek approval for a second line specifying recorded episodes. If the page itself calls the license an all-media license, correct the page first. The original run gives the team a question worth testing.
