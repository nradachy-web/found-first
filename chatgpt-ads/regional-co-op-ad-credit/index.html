ChatGPT advertising · Regional campaigns and multibranch operations

Sharing ChatGPT ads costs when another branch wins the job

Agree how branches will share regional ChatGPT ads costs before a buyer crosses a territory line. One branch may contribute to the campaign, another may take the first call, and a third may deliver the work. Decide which of those facts governs reimbursement under your co-op agreement.

Choose a basis the records can support

A pooled brand campaign might use an agreed contribution from each participant. An arrangement based on fulfilled jobs needs a reliable record of the fulfilling operator and a way to handle cancellations. If your CRM overwrites the original branch when a job transfers, a charge based on first receipt will be hard for either operator to verify.

Settle a transfer once

A regional form submission and a later booking can describe stages of the same purchase. OpenAI’s conversion reporting depends on the event and attribution setup. Use campaign reports to examine advertising activity, then follow the shared inquiry reference through dispatch when resolving which branch earned credit for the work.

The warehouse belongs to a different branch

Suppose two cleaning franchisees fund a regional campaign. A prospect calls the north office, but its warehouse falls under the south operator. The dispatch log records the transfer and the signed work order names south. Under a fulfilled-job agreement, south receives job credit and north retains the intake record. If the agreement instead pools regional costs by a fixed contribution, the same transfer documents the outcome without changing either operator’s share.
