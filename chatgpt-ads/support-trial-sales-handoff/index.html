When should a help desk trial from ChatGPT ads go to sales?

Offer sales help at the point where a help desk trial user needs a decision about deployment. A buyer asking how to move a live support queue has a reason to speak with someone. A user sorting sample tickets may simply need better trial instructions.

Let the trial user name the next decision

Place a route to sales beside the rollout task. Give the user space to identify the queue, the team that owns it and the question blocking the next step. Leave ordinary setup help accessible there too. Sending every failed setup attempt to a rep makes a broken instruction look like buying interest.

Send the rep a useful introduction

Pass along the offer the buyer accepted, the details they supplied and the question they want answered. Keep the source of each detail clear. OpenAI says advertisers do not receive private chats, so a rep preparing for a call should rely on the buyer's request and the vendor's own trial records.

A trial owner wants to bring in the evening shift

Suppose the trial guide covers assigning tickets to sample agents. A manager follows it, then requests a rollout discussion about rotating a live queue between employees and contractors. The rep receives the manager's question and the completed trial checklist, and brings the role-permissions chart to the call. If the same manager instead reports that an invitation email never arrived, the trial team resolves delivery and lets the manager resume testing. The stuck invitation supplies no reason for a sales call.
