ChatGPT advertising · Conversion measurement and sales outcomes

Can sales from ChatGPT ads cover fulfillment and ad spend?

Before scaling ChatGPT ads on the strength of sales revenue, calculate what those orders contributed after the costs of fulfilling them. Compare that amount with the campaign spend for the same acquisition period.

Use the mix of orders the ads actually brought

Pull the matched order lines and the net sales your business retained. Subtract costs that travel with those orders, such as the goods, packaging and freight your business covered. Apply the same cost convention across the review. A companywide average can paint the wrong picture if the campaign attracts mostly bulky items or unusually small shipments.

Make the comparison at campaign level

Include spend on the clicks that produced no sale. Assigning spend only to matched buyers understates what it took to acquire the group. If fulfillment bills are outstanding, label the contribution provisional and identify which missing cost could reverse the decision. What is left after ad spend also needs to support the business's overhead.

The sale that requires a dedicated delivery

Consider a ceramics supplier advertising sample displays to retail buyers. The sales export looks promising until the owner matches the orders to packing slips and carrier invoices. Custom crates and dedicated delivery absorb the contribution needed to cover the ads. The owner revises the shipping offer before increasing spend. If buyers collect the displays and the pickup records confirm the supplier incurred no freight charge, the owner reruns the calculation with the remaining packing costs and considers an increase if the orders clear its contribution target.
