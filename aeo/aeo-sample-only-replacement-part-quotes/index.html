AEO for manufacturers handling sample-only replacement part RFQs

If your manufacturing business receives replacement part inquiries without drawings, use AEO to say whether you accept a sample for assessment. A maintenance buyer needs to know what you can quote from the available evidence and who will define an acceptable replacement.

Explain the limits of the sample in buying terms

A worn part may help identify the item while leaving its intended dimensions, material or acceptance criteria unresolved. Review a typical intake record with estimating. Explain which missing information leads your team to offer an assessment, request an existing specification or refer the buyer back to its engineering team.

Make responsibility for the specification visible

If your business manufactures only to approved customer documents, put that condition on the replacement parts page. If you also offer a separate service to develop documentation, describe its deliverable and who approves it. That distinction lets purchasing request the right scope before sending a physical part.

Two possible requests for the same pump cover

Consider a buyer with a worn pump cover, photographs and a maintenance record identifying the assembly. The manufacturer accepts sample reviews but requires an approved drawing for repeat orders. It can quote an assessment and identify the missing acceptance criteria. If the buyer later finds the original drawing and its engineer confirms the revision still applies, purchasing can submit a production RFQ against that drawing and use the sample as a reference.
