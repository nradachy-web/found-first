ChatGPT ads for tile sold by the carton

Use a finished installation to sell the look of a tile, then name the unit a shopper can buy. When your ChatGPT ad features a wall of patterned tile, a carton offer needs both the correct pattern and its documented coverage per carton.

Read the label for the pictured format

Carton coverage belongs to a particular tile size, packing quantity and product. Similar colors in a field tile and a mosaic sheet can have different coverage. Take the figure from the label for the advertised item and repeat the unit next to the quantity selector. This lets the shopper interpret what adding one to the basket means.

Identify the pieces that make the pattern

A checkerboard photograph might show a factory-mounted sheet or an installer’s arrangement of two colors from separate cartons. The two installations can look identical in an ad. The shopper needs to know which one the retailer stocks before using the photograph to plan an order.

One checkerboard photograph, two carton labels

Consider a tile retailer with a black-and-cream entryway photograph, the installer’s material schedule and two carton labels. The schedule identifies separately boxed solid colors. The retailer uses the image in an ad that names both tiles and points to a selection where customers can order each color by the carton. If a supplier introduces a single mixed carton, the retailer can feature that product directly after checking its color mix, layout and coverage against the pictured floor.
