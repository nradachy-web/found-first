Field notes/AEO vs. SEO vs. GEO

AEO, SEO and GEO: choose the work your business needs.

Clear definitions. A practical buying decision. One coherent public presence.

SEO means search engine optimization: improving how a website can be discovered and evaluated through search. AEO means answer engine optimization, and GEO means generative engine optimization. Both are commonly used for work on visibility in AI-generated answers. Found First treats AEO and GEO as one organic discovery discipline, connected to useful websites and established search fundamentals rather than three unrelated products a business must buy.

What changes when an answer becomes part of discovery

A traditional search result directs attention to a page. An AI answer can also summarize a topic, name businesses or point to supporting sources before a person visits a website. That changes what a business wants to understand about its visibility: not only whether a page appears, but whether the offer is represented accurately and for a relevant buying situation.

The distinction is commercial. A specialist described as a generalist can lose the reason a customer would choose it. A product can be cited as a source without being recommended as a purchase. AEO and GEO are useful labels when they focus attention on those questions rather than simply rename existing activity.

The search foundation still matters

Google says its AI Overviews and AI Mode use established SEO fundamentals. It does not require a special AI file or special schema for inclusion. A business should therefore be skeptical of a pitch that treats AI discovery as disconnected from the accessible, useful website people already need.

This does not make every AI visibility concern identical to a conventional search question. It means the work should build on the foundation and investigate the actual gap. Existing technical and editorial investment can remain valuable while the business examines how its identity and offers are presented in answers.

Compare what the engagement is meant to improve, not just the acronym on the proposal.

Paid ChatGPT advertising is separate. Buying an ad does not buy influence over an organic answer.

Choose the gap before choosing another agency

If the website is inaccessible or unclear, a new visibility report will not repair the destination. If buyers misunderstand a well-built product page, positioning may deserve attention. If the business does not know how it appears, an assessment can be more useful than immediately expanding content.

Ask who will interpret findings, approve facts and implement changes. An existing SEO team may already own part of that work. A useful additional engagement has a specific responsibility and a clear relationship to what is already being done.

Questions about the comparison

Do we need separate AEO and GEO engagements?+

Not simply because both labels exist. Found First treats them as overlapping names for organic AI visibility work. A proposal should explain the business problem and responsibilities clearly enough that you can recognize duplication.

Does AEO replace SEO?+

No. Established search work can support the same public presence. The useful question is whether an additional perspective addresses a gap in how buyers discover and understand the business, rather than whether one acronym has replaced another.

Which approach guarantees a recommendation?+

None. Platforms control their answers, and a named business is not necessarily endorsed. Evaluate observations, accuracy and commercial outcomes separately rather than accepting a guarantee attached to any of these labels.
