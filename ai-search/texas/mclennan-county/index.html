Questions worth asking.

Does a technical firm need to simplify everything to improve AI visibility?+

No. Accessibility and accuracy can coexist. The objective is to make the relevance understandable without pretending the specialty is simpler than it is. A serious buyer should recognize why a detailed conversation is warranted.

What if our company contributes only one part of a larger project?+

That role can be a strength when it is clearly understood. The introduction should explain the kind of responsibility the company accepts instead of implying that a specialist assignment includes the entire project.

Is this relevant to McLennan County firms with customers elsewhere?+

Yes. The company's location and its customer reach are different parts of the offer. A specialist can be rooted locally and considered for work across a wider market when the delivery arrangement genuinely supports that reach.
