Give your reputation somewhere to go

Your best customers know why they chose you. A buyer encountering your company for the first time needs that same distinction to be understandable. A specialist reduced to a broad category, a regional provider mistaken for a single-location shop, or a product brand described by yesterday's range can lose the point of the sale before a conversation begins.

Found First works on that gap. We bring the business, its website and its content into a clearer account of what it does and who it serves. The work connects marketing judgment with technical capability, so a positioning problem can lead to useful changes rather than another report nobody implements.

The commercial objective is stronger consideration for the work you actually want: relevant inquiries, better-informed buyers and a clearer path from discovery to a real conversation. An appearance in an answer matters most when it supports that objective.

What an AEO and GEO engagement should change

Start with a question a buyer actually asks your sales team. For a landscape company, “Can you install the planting plan my designer already drew?” is a different inquiry from “Can you design my yard?” AEO work gives each buyer a clear path to the appropriate service. See the landscaping AEO example. For a measured example from hospitality, see our audit of 12 airport hotel websites.

GEO work addresses the next decision: why would the buyer include your business on a shortlist? A contractor can identify who designed a pictured project and who installed it. That distinction gives customers and generative search systems evidence about the role the company actually performed. Read the GEO example on portfolio credits.

When this is the right conversation

AEO is most useful when there is a real business distinction worth making visible.

  • Your specialty gets lostCustomers value something specific about your expertise, products or service, but an unfamiliar buyer could mistake you for a generalist.
  • Your business has changedYou serve a different customer, sell a stronger offer or operate across a wider market than your public presence suggests.
  • Referrals carry the explanationPeople who know you make the case well. You want buyers outside that network to understand the same reason to choose you.
  • Your team needs an owner for the workYou want marketing and technical decisions connected, with a partner who can help move improvements beyond discussion.

Questions before you choose an AEO agency

Are AEO and GEO different services?+

They are overlapping names for work on visibility in AI-generated answers. Found First treats them as one organic discovery offer. The useful distinction is the business problem being solved, rather than buying separate packages for different acronyms.

Can you work with our existing website?+

Yes. The existing site, your offer and the changes needed determine the appropriate work. A new website is not an automatic requirement. We will discuss where the current experience supports the sale and where it gets in the way.

Does this replace our SEO or Google Ads?+

It does not automatically replace either. Organic AI visibility belongs in the context of your existing acquisition activity. A business already receiving useful inquiries should understand how new work supports its goals before changing established channels.

Can you guarantee that AI will recommend us?+

No. Platforms control their answers, and a named business is not necessarily being endorsed. We keep observations, business outcomes and expectations distinct. The engagement should be judged against agreed objectives, without treating any single answer as a permanent position.

How much does an engagement cost?+

Scope and investment depend on the business and the work being considered. The call is an opportunity to discuss fit; proposed commercial terms are agreed before work begins.
