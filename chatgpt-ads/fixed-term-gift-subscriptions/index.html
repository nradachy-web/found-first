ChatGPT advertising · Offer qualification and buying readiness

ChatGPT ads for gift subscriptions with a fixed end date

A fixed-term gift is a distinct paid acquisition offer when the giver can make one purchase and the recipient can use the whole term without taking on a bill. Before testing ChatGPT ads, establish whose commitment the campaign is meant to win.

Follow the gift from payment to redemption

Read the gift certificate, delivery instructions and renewal terms side by side. Check who supplies the address, when the term starts and what happens to an unredeemed gift. A giver shopping for a surprise may accept an emailed code but abandon an order that requires the recipient's shipping details immediately.

Give the initial purchase its own economics

Decide whether the prepaid term is worth acquiring even if it ends as scheduled. Count a later subscription under the person who chose to start it. OpenAI's conversion reporting depends on attribution and event setup. A reported gift purchase gives no evidence that the recipient has authorized a recurring charge.

A puzzle club sends a code before it sends a box

Imagine a puzzle publisher's gift certificate covers a complete mystery series. The giver receives a printable code, and the recipient chooses a start month and delivery address. Advertise the complete series as a prepaid gift. If fulfillment instead starts the term on the giver's checkout date, narrow the offer to buyers who know the address and want delivery to begin then. A promise that the recipient can start whenever they like would change what the publisher owes.
