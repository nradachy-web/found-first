Which drawing revision governs an RFQ? AEO for contract shops

A contract shop's AEO content should tell purchasing how the governing drawing revision gets confirmed and where it will appear on the quote. This helps repeat customers who know the part number but have more than one version in circulation.

Make the revision part of the answer

Look at a recent quote template and the fields on your upload form. If the estimator records the drawing number and revision separately, use the same terms on the website. Tell the buyer what to include when a change notice accompanies the drawing and where to send a correction after submitting the request.

Describe the point where estimating pauses

A file name, email subject and drawing title block can disagree. Explain whose confirmation your team needs to settle that conflict. Choosing whichever file arrived last can leave two suppliers quoting different versions of the same part, making a bid comparison unreliable.

A new attachment on an old email thread

Imagine a buyer replies to an earlier request. The subject says revision B, the attached drawing says revision C and the purchasing schedule still calls for B. The estimator identifies the conflict and asks the buyer to confirm the revision for this order. If C applies only to future releases, today's quote should identify B. If purchasing confirms the change takes effect now, the quote can name C and reference the corresponding drawing.
