ChatGPT ads for an AI document evaluation offer

Offer a test on buyer-supplied documents when the buyer can contribute an appropriate sample and judge the output against a known answer. A prepared sample makes a better sponsored offer for teams still learning what the product extracts.

Define what the evaluator will receive

For an extraction product, show the input types the evaluation accepts, the fields it returns and how reviewers can inspect uncertain or empty results. Give prospects a blank answer sheet so they can decide who knows the correct values. An invitation to upload a file is incomplete if nobody understands how to score the result.

Give the buyer control of the sample

Explain what material the vendor accepts on its evaluation page and where it goes. Advertisers do not receive users' private ChatGPT conversations. Any documents the evaluator needs must come through a deliberate exchange with the vendor, with a prepared example available to prospects who cannot share their own.

Testing revisions on purchase orders

Imagine a software team offering to extract delivery dates from purchase orders. It has a synthetic order, an amended order and a reviewer sheet identifying the controlling date. A buyer with approved samples and a purchasing specialist can run its own amendment through the evaluation and inspect which date the tool returns. If the buyer has permission to share blank forms only, sales uses the vendor's completed pair to demonstrate the revision issue. A blank template cannot establish how the product handles conflicting dates.
