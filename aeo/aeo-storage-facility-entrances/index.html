AEO for storage facilities with separate rental and move-in entrances

When a storage facility has several entrances, ask an AEO writer to match each arrival question to the gate or door the visitor can use. The street address on a lease may identify the property accurately while directing a first-time visitor to a locked loading gate.

Distinguish the first visit from the first load

A prospect coming for a tour needs a public entrance and a clear check-in point. A tenant arriving with a truck needs the approved vehicle entrance and the condition for using it, such as completed enrollment. Both trips can end at the same property even when they start on opposite sides of the block.

Decide which details belong in public copy

Street names, signed entrance labels and a description of where to check in can help visitors choose a route. Keep individual credentials and tenant access instructions in the move-in confirmation. For Google AI features, Google describes ordinary search requirements. Special markup is unnecessary to publish a useful arrival answer.

The street address points to the truck gate

Consider a storage building whose lease uses the industrial road address. The entrance diagram shows a public lobby on Market Street, while the industrial road gate opens only for enrolled tenants. Use the diagram and the office’s tour confirmation to send prospective renters to Market Street. A renter with a completed lease and active vehicle access can follow the industrial road instructions in the move-in message. If the lease is signed but gate access is pending, send that renter to the lobby before they bring the truck around.
