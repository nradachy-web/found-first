ChatGPT advertising · Offer qualification and buying readiness

Testing ChatGPT ads to recruit sellers for marketplace requests

A marketplace can test seller acquisition with ChatGPT ads when it can tell a supplier what a buyer is seeking and how to submit a usable offer. A busy buyer category alone gives a seller little basis for deciding whether to do the work.

Check what the buyer has actually authorized

Read the sourcing brief and its publication permissions. Can the marketplace describe the material, minimum lot, delivery area and decision date? Has the buyer agreed to review submissions? An inquiry still being scoped can justify building a supplier directory, but it is too early to advertise a live sourcing opportunity.

Price the effort in the supplier's time

A mill may have to unroll stock, check dye lots and prepare swatches. Make those requirements clear before asking it to list. Decide whether a response is useful by checking if the buyer can evaluate it as submitted. OpenAI describes ad and landing page content as possible delivery signals. Put the material requirements in the offer itself.

Surplus cloth for a theater wardrobe department

Imagine a fabric exchange has a wardrobe department's written brief for matching wool from a single dye lot. The approved public summary includes the yardage and swatch deadline. Invite mills with a qualifying roll to offer it against that request. If the department takes an option on a roll and stops reviewing alternatives, retire the request-specific offer. Mills might still be worth recruiting for future productions, but promising this review would waste their swatches.
