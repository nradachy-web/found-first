Found First runs that cycle as a managed monthly program. No one can guarantee a place in an AI answer, so we report what we observe.

[ definition ]

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of making a business easy for AI systems to find, understand and describe accurately, so that it is named when people ask those systems for help.

The same work is sometimes called generative engine optimization (GEO). It extends SEO and does not replace it. See how AEO, SEO and GEO relate.

How the work runs

Four steps, repeated. Each cycle starts from what the last one found.

  1. Research the answers

    We put a defined set of your customers' questions to selected AI models, keep every answer, and review where your business is named, how it is described and which sources appear.

  2. Confirm the facts, fix the pages

    Locations, phone numbers, services and availability are checked first. Then existing pages are improved and crawl or indexing problems are fixed, so the right information can be found.

  3. Publish useful answers

    Each question worth winning gets a page that answers it plainly, with evidence, current details and a way to get in touch. Growth also publishes across 3 agreed channels.

  4. Measure again

    We repeat the same research design, review inquiries and report what shipped. AI mentions, paid results and confirmed jobs stay separate.

What Google and OpenAI say a site needs

Google's position is that AI Overviews and AI Mode have no additional requirements and need no special optimization. What it lists instead are fundamentals. OpenAI documents one crawler setting for ChatGPT search.

  • Crawling is allowedIn robots.txt, and by any CDN or hosting infrastructure in front of the site.
  • Pages are indexed and can show a snippetGoogle requires both before a page can appear as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode.
  • Content is easy to findInternal links should lead to every page that matters.
  • Important content is textGoogle asks that it be available in textual form, supported by good images and video where they apply.
  • The page is good to useGoogle lists a great page experience among its fundamentals.
  • Structured data matches the pageMarkup has to say the same thing as the visible text.
  • Business Profile and Merchant Center are currentGoogle lists up-to-date profile and product information among its fundamentals.
  • OAI-SearchBot is allowedOpenAI advises allowing its search crawler in robots.txt. It is a separate setting from GPTBot, which covers model training.

Google's list is from Search Central's guidance on AI features. The crawler setting is from OpenAI's developer documentation. Both checked November 11, 2026.

How results are measured

We report mentions, citations, accuracy, visits and inquiries on separate lines, each with its date and its sample, and Google for its part counts visits from AI Overviews and AI Mode inside overall Search Console traffic, within the Web search type. What no one can promise

AI answers change with the wording of the question, the date, the model and the context of the chat. Our research records what selected model routes returned on a given day. It does not reproduce every consumer app or predict the next answer.

So Found First does not guarantee rankings, mentions or leads. What we commit to is the work and the record: what was researched, what was changed and what we observed afterwards.

Questions about AEO

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will name my business?+

No. Assistants choose their own answers and can vary by wording, date and context. Our work improves the material they can find and use, and we report observed results.

Does AEO replace SEO?+

No. The foundations overlap, and Google says its SEO best practices remain relevant for AI features. Keep useful SEO work and extend measurement to generated answers, business mentions and citations.

Do I need an llms.txt file or special schema?+

Not for Google. Google says you do not need new machine readable files, AI text files or special structured data to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Structured data you already use should match the visible text on the page.

Do ChatGPT ads improve organic AI visibility?+

No. OpenAI states that ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers. We manage paid campaigns and organic visibility as separate work and measure them separately.
