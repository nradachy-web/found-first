Questions worth asking.

Does countywide coverage imply the same response everywhere?+

No. Service type, access and scheduling can affect what a company offers at a particular location. A credible description distinguishes routine coverage from selected projects or special arrangements rather than relying on one broad availability claim.

Should a Galveston County business describe island and peninsula reach separately?+

When those distinctions affect the service, they can be useful. The county's own access information shows why a single place label does not explain the route. The business should represent the actual arrangement customers can expect.

Can a specialist justify travel that a routine provider cannot?+

Yes, depending on the work and the business model. A substantial project can make wider travel practical while small recurring tasks require a tighter footprint. The visibility objective should reflect that commercial difference rather than equate more territory with more value.
