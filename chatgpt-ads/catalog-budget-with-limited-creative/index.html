Funding ChatGPT ads from a catalog when Meta creative is scarce

If shoppers can choose the right luggage insert from its dimensions and compatibility list, use part of the next creative production budget to test ChatGPT ads. Keep funding a demonstration wherever shoppers need to see the insert installed before they buy.

Find the actual constraint

Ask merchandising to put the product catalog beside the creative calendar. A missed shoot can leave plenty of saleable inventory with accurate photos, dimensions and product pages. It can also leave the business without the one piece of footage that explains a new design. Only the first problem gives the catalog a natural advantage.

Price the choice in usable inventory

OpenAI supports paid campaigns from product feeds for eligible advertisers. For a budget decision, count the in-stock models a buyer could confidently select with the material already on hand. A long file full of near-identical variants adds little if none says which suitcase it fits. Confirm access to the campaign option before moving production funds.

A luggage brand loses its studio date

Suppose the catalog maps each rigid insert to a named suitcase model, and the returns log shows few fit questions for those combinations. The buyer earmarks the rigid range for a paid catalog test and keeps the existing Meta campaign running. The collapsible inserts have attractive photos too, but support tickets repeatedly ask how the frame locks. If those inserts account for the stock the brand needs to move, the buyer rebooks the demonstration shoot instead.
