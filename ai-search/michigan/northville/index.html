Which boundary actually matters?

A field-service boundary

The company needs a truthful account of where work can be performed. The boundary should follow operational reality, with project fit and scheduling considered alongside distance.

A relationship boundary

The business may serve clients farther away when the work does not depend on frequent visits. The buyer needs to understand the nature of the relationship rather than assume the office address defines the audience.
