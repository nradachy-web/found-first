ChatGPT advertising · Landing page decisions after the ad

ChatGPT ads for reservations when the mobile link opens an app

Use a mobile destination where a first-time visitor can complete the advertised reservation on the web if you offer browser booking. An app handoff makes sense when installation is part of the offer and the ad sets that expectation before the click.

Start with a phone the business has never signed into

Staff phones often have the app installed and permissions saved. Open the ad destination on a phone without the app, follow the main button and decline the install prompt if one appears. Check whether the web option opens the advertised activity, carries the location forward and lets the visitor finish the reservation.

Place the fallback where the path breaks

A browser booking link next to the app button gives the visitor a choice before they leave the site. A support link at the bottom of an app store listing is too late for someone who simply wanted a reservation. Where the service requires the app, explain the installation step on the destination and describe what the customer will do inside it.

A climbing gym with two reservation routes

Suppose a gym compares its introductory climb ad, reservation vendor’s web link and mobile app listing. The current button opens the store on a new phone. Since the vendor also sells intro sessions on the web, the gym can make that session’s browser checkout the primary button. If a separate members’ activity requires an in-app pass, its ad can open an installation and sign-in guide specific to that activity.
