AI visibility for software buyers building a shortlist.

For B2B and SaaS, AI visibility should support the buyer's evaluation: use case, integrations, security questions, implementation, and alternatives. Found First builds content and conversion paths around those decisions.

By Found First · September 16, 2026 · AEO & AI marketing

Discuss this with Nick

The decision behind the search.

A buyer may describe a workflow instead of naming your software category. They may need a specific integration or have a constraint your homepage never mentions. Generic feature language leaves those needs unanswered and makes it harder to understand whether your product fits.

A practical way to approach it.

01 / Map role and workflow questions

Distinguish the end user, evaluator, technical reviewer, and budget owner. Each needs different evidence and may use different language to describe the same problem.

02 / Document capabilities precisely

Publish supported integrations, prerequisites, implementation scope, and honest limitations. Explain comparative tradeoffs without inventing competitor weaknesses or security credentials.

03 / Connect research to qualification

Offer the appropriate next step: product documentation, an example workflow, a trial, or a demo. Track qualified opportunities and later pipeline separately from raw demo requests.

Platform guidance: Google Search Central and OpenAI: Ads in ChatGPT. Paid campaigns must satisfy current eligibility and policy requirements.

Questions to bring to the brief.

Use these as starting points and replace the bracketed terms with the specifics of your business.

  • Which [software category] supports [integration] for a [team type]?
  • Compare [product] and [alternative] for [workflow].
  • What does implementing [product] require from our team?

What the work can include.

We agree the scope around your starting point and priorities.

  • Role and use-case question research.
  • Integration and comparison content briefs.
  • Demo or trial landing pages.
  • Qualification and pipeline measurement definitions.

Before you get started.

Should we publish competitor comparison pages?

Where a real buyer needs the comparison and you can keep the claims accurate. Include fit and tradeoffs, with current sources for competitor facts.

Can documentation help discovery?

Accessible, specific documentation can answer detailed evaluation questions. It should be connected to relevant product and use-case pages.

What counts as success?

Define qualified trials or opportunities based on your sales motion. A larger number of low-fit demos may not represent improvement.
