ChatGPT advertising · Offer qualification and buying readiness

Qualifying first wholesale orders from ChatGPT ads

A first-order wholesale offer can work in ChatGPT ads when the seller can recognize an eligible retailer before it accepts the order. Define what counts as the first purchase across every way shops buy from you, including through a rep or at a trade show.

Draw the eligibility line where the business does

Does buying samples start the wholesale relationship, or does the first stock order do that? Is a second shop a new account if the same owner pays both invoices? Settle those questions against your trade terms. Tell retailers enough to self-qualify before they assemble an opening assortment.

Ask for a buying decision a new retailer can make

A shop ready to buy stock can choose a range, meet the opening minimum and give a delivery address. A buyer still comparing paper quality may be ready for samples instead. Keep sample requests distinct from opening orders in the sales ledger even if an ad report registers both as conversions.

A stationery shop already bought at the trade fair

Imagine a card publisher offers a display stand with a retailer's opening stock order. A shop places its first web order, but the rep's signed fair order and the billing ledger show the same retailer already bought a seasonal assortment. Route it to a reorder and explain how existing shops can obtain a stand. If the fair record shows only a sample envelope, the shop still qualifies under these terms. A new web login would give the wrong answer in the first case.
