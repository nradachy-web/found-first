Updating HVAC service coverage in AEO after a branch closes

Keep an HVAC town page if another branch has taken responsibility for the work it describes. Closing a branch changes the answer to where customers can visit immediately. Whether it changes the answer to who can service their address depends on the dispatch handoff.

Check what the receiving branch inherited

A transfer of customer records can cover open jobs and warranty follow-up without transferring every kind of new inquiry. Ask for the handoff notice and the receiving dispatcher’s territory list. Read them against the repair, installation and service-agreement offers on the town page before changing its phone number.

Give returning customers a recognizable route

Someone searching for the old branch by name may have a job already in progress. Explain where that customer can refer to an existing work order. A general quote form asking for equipment details and a desired project date makes them start an unrelated conversation.

A satellite counter closes during a handoff

Suppose the closure notice names the main branch as the new contact for open installations. Its dispatch sheet also accepts new installation estimates in the satellite town, but excludes new repair calls there. Keep the town’s installation answer, change the estimate destination and retire the invitation to visit the old counter. Give open-job customers the service desk identified in the notice. If the receiving dispatcher later accepts repairs in the town, the repair answer can return once the team has a working intake route for those addresses.
