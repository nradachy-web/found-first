Explain who the customer is actually hiring

A prospective solar customer should be able to understand what your company does in the project. Sales, design, installation and ongoing support can be different responsibilities. A broad brand promise should not leave the customer guessing about which role belongs to your business and which may involve another party.

For an installer with direct project responsibilities, those capabilities deserve a clear explanation. If the business has a more limited role, the marketing should reflect it accurately. Found First helps connect the company's actual offering to the decision a customer is making, without inventing affiliations or presenting a referral relationship as an installation capability.

Project fit is more persuasive than a universal savings promise

A customer researching solar may be comparing options before knowing whether a project is suitable. Your website can explain the kind of properties and engagements the company evaluates without announcing the outcome of that evaluation. The service becomes easier to understand when the business states what it offers and how a relevant consultation begins.

We help develop that explanation using approved business facts. Financial returns, incentives and bill effects should not be generalized from an unrelated project or invented to make the page more persuasive. A clear account of the actual installation relationship can establish credibility without promising every customer the same economic result.
