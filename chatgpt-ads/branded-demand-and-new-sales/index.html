ChatGPT advertising · Conversion measurement and sales outcomes

Did ChatGPT ads create demand or bring a buyer back?

When sales attributed to ChatGPT ads come from buyers who already knew your brand, check whether you paid to introduce the business or to help someone finish a purchase. Both can be valuable, but they justify different budgets.

Look for an earlier buying decision

An existing quote, a distributor referral or a request for your product by name can establish that interest predates the ad. A later branded search on its own tells you much less. Someone who discovered you through an ad may search your name to find you again. Build the sequence from records you hold, including the buyer's own account of how they heard about you.

Set a higher bar for a claim about extra sales

Attribution assigns credit under a reporting rule. Estimating how many sales the ads added requires a credible comparison with what would otherwise have happened. If your team can run a comparison, agree on comparable audiences or markets, keep other promotions consistent and assess total new sales across channels. A referral campaign running in only one market can spoil that comparison.

The wholesale buyer who already had a sample

Consider a coffee roaster reviewing a first wholesale order associated with ChatGPT ads. A dated sample receipt and a tasting appointment show that the café was already evaluating the roaster. The owner records an existing opportunity that returned and reviews the ads against a follow-up objective. If the café instead identifies the ad as its introduction and requested its first sample afterward, the owner includes it among plausible new discoveries while reserving the extra-sales claim for the comparison.
