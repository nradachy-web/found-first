AEO for temporary notices that change a standing offer

Put a temporary exception beside the offer it changes. Use a sitewide notice only when readers elsewhere need to change what they do, and identify the event that will make the ordinary answer accurate again.

Write the exception against the usual answer

Read the service page as someone arriving directly from a referral. Identify the exact sentence that has become misleading and qualify it there. “Contact us for updates” leaves the buyer to discover both the problem and its scope. A notice that names the affected option and the available alternative lets the buyer take the right next step.

Choose a reliable signal for removal

A scheduled removal fits a change with a confirmed end. When reopening depends on an inspection, delivery or other event, arrange to check that event before restoring the regular wording. Review the relevant page with the banner dismissed. A returning reader might never see the banner twice.

A bindery temporarily changes its drop-off instructions

Take a bindery whose standing instructions allow hand delivery or shipment. During loading-bay repairs, a facilities notice suspends deliveries by freight carrier but confirms that the front desk accepts hand-carried samples. Place the exception beside the freight instructions and leave the sample instructions usable. If the front desk also closes, extend the notice to both routes and publish the receiving coordinator's approved alternative. Restore the usual instructions when the coordinator confirms that receiving has resumed.
