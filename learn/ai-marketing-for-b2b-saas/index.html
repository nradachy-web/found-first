AEO for B2B and SaaS

A buyer building a software shortlist with an AI assistant may never type your category name. They describe a workflow, the tools they already use and a constraint. AEO for B2B and SaaS means publishing the facts those questions turn on, where a crawler can read them without logging in: use cases, integrations, pricing, limits and fair comparisons.

Who is asking, and the page that has to answer

A software purchase has several readers, and each one asks a different question. Put your own product, category and integrations in the brackets. These are example shapes, not measured search data.

The people involved in a software purchase, a question each might put to an assistant, and the page that should hold the answer.
TopicA question they might askThe page that has to answer it
The person with the problemWhat is a good way to [do this workflow] for a [team type] that already uses [tool]?A use-case page named for the job, not the feature: the workflow step by step, who it suits and a worked example.
The evaluatorCompare [product] and [alternative] for [workflow] at a company that needs [constraint].A comparison page with dated, sourced facts on both sides and a plain statement of when the alternative is the better choice.
The technical reviewerDoes [product] integrate with [system], and is that native or through [middleware]?One page per integration, plus public documentation: what syncs, in which direction, the limits and what has to be set up first.
The security reviewerDoes [product] hold [certification], and where is customer data stored?A security page that states only what is current and can be evidenced, and says how to request the report.
The budget ownerWhat does [product] cost for [number] users, and what costs extra?A pricing page with the numbers or, where they cannot be published, the pricing model and what moves the price.
The person who will roll it outWhat does implementing [product] need from our team, and what usually slows it down?An implementation page: the stages, the roles needed on the customer's side and the common causes of delay.

Publish the pricing model, even if you cannot publish the price

An answer about what your product costs can only draw on what is public. If your site says nothing, what is public is other people's guesses: old forum threads, reseller pages and comparison sites.

You do not have to publish a rate card. Say what the price is based on, such as seats, usage or modules. Say what the entry plan includes, what triggers a custom quote and what is charged separately, such as onboarding or a support tier. Date the page. A buyer who can rule you in or out on budget before the demo makes for a better demo.

Rules for a comparison page you can defend

  • Source and date every competitor fact

    Link to the competitor's own documentation or pricing page and show the date you checked. Remove what you can no longer confirm.

  • Say when the other product wins

    Name the buyer for whom the alternative is the better fit. A page that concedes nothing reads as an advertisement.

  • Compare on the buyer's criteria

    Use the constraints buyers bring: integrations, deployment, limits, support and price model. Not your feature list with ticks down one side.

  • Invent nothing

    No claim about a competitor that you cannot source, and no security or compliance badge that you do not currently hold.

Make documentation part of the answer

Documentation holds the specifics a technical reviewer asks about. It only helps if a crawler can reach it.

  • Docs are publicA page behind a login or a form cannot be crawled, so Google cannot index it and OpenAI's search crawler cannot read it.
  • The docs site allows the right crawlersA documentation subdomain has its own robots.txt. Check that it allows Googlebot and OAI-SearchBot, and that no CDN or bot rule blocks them.
  • Content is text in the pageGoogle asks that important content be available in textual form. Limits and steps shown only in screenshots or video are not.
  • Each integration and limit has an addressOne URL per integration, with rate limits, quotas and prerequisites stated, so an answer has a specific page to cite.
  • Pages show a date or versionA changelog and a last-updated date tell a reader which statement is current.
  • Docs link to the productEach technical page links to the use case or plan it belongs to, and the product pages link back.

Crawler guidance from OpenAI's developer documentation and Google Search Central, checked November 11, 2026.

What our research does and does not show for software

Our published study is of a plumbing business, not a software company, and we have not published software research. We cite the study for one thing it showed: inside a single business, changing the customer's problem changed how often the business was named, so test a software buyer's questions separately by role, stack and constraint.

Several people research a purchase over time, and only some of what they do leaves a record. Be exact about which is which.

Questions from software companies

Should we publish competitor comparison pages?+

Yes, where buyers are already making that comparison and you can keep every claim accurate. Source and date each competitor fact, and say plainly when the alternative is the better fit.

Should we gate documentation or pricing behind a form?+

Not if you want it to inform an answer. A page that needs a login or a form cannot be crawled, so neither Google nor OpenAI's search crawler can read it. Gate what is worth an email address, such as a trial or a detailed security report, and leave the facts public.

Can ChatGPT ads reach B2B buyers?+

Only some of them. OpenAI says ads appear on the Free and Go plans and not on Business, Enterprise or Edu accounts, and its ad policies say that during the initial test period ads are primarily limited to consumer verticals, with digital products among the examples. Confirm that your category is eligible and treat any campaign as a small, capped test.

Do review sites and directories matter?+

They can appear among the sources an answer draws on, and our research records which ones do. A source appearing in the evidence does not prove that buying a listing there will change what an assistant says, so check your existing profile and its accuracy first.

What counts as success?+

Qualified opportunities and pipeline, defined by your own sales motion. More demo requests from buyers who do not fit is not an improvement.

Can you guarantee we appear on AI shortlists?+

No. Assistants choose their own answers, and those answers vary with wording, date and context. We improve the public material they can draw on and report what we observe.
