Window tint AEO for quotes on already dark glass

Your tint shop's AEO content should answer a common quote question about dark rear windows. The glass may have come that way from the factory, or an earlier installer may have applied film. That distinction affects whether the estimate needs a removal line.

Ask which windows the driver wants changed

Someone asking to “match the back” may only want the front doors tinted. Someone asking to fix bubbles in the rear window is describing a different job. Explain how the shop evaluates an appearance match, and ask buyers to identify the panes they want worked on. Darkness in a photo alone does not establish what material is on the glass.

Make removal a visible part of the estimate

If an old installation needs to come off, explain when the shop assesses its condition and confirms removal time. Rear glass with defroster lines warrants its own discussion with the installer. Publish the shop's actual inspection practice so the customer knows whether to request a fitting or bring the vehicle by for a look.

The rear windows stay dark either way

Suppose an SUV owner asks for a front-door match. The shop has an anonymized estimate and photos of a similar SUV with factory privacy glass. The writer marks the two front panes on the photo and shows what that estimate included. The owner can request a quote for those doors alone. If they also want bubbling film replaced on the liftgate, the form adds a rear-glass inspection before the shop confirms a combined appointment.
