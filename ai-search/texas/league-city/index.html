Questions worth asking.

Should a marine business appear for all repair requests?+

Only where the work matches its capabilities. Equipment type, service setting and job scope can change suitability substantially. Broad category recognition is useful only if it brings customers who can actually benefit from the business.

Can a specialist compete with a provider that appears closer?+

Potentially, when expertise matters enough to justify travel or coordination. Distance is one factor in the decision. A clear explanation of relevant capability can help the customer assess that tradeoff without assuming every nearby provider is equivalent.

What if our mobile and workshop services differ?+

Those differences should remain understandable. A company may offer a narrow range onsite and more extensive work at its facility. Buyers should not infer identical capability from the shared brand or a broad statement that the business offers repairs.
