GEO for product manuals that span model revisions

If your company sells several revisions under one product name, scope GEO work around the buyer’s way of identifying each revision. A manual excerpt earns its place on the public product page when a reader can tell which units it describes.

Find the identifier a buyer actually has

Engineering might use a board revision while a reseller lists only the name on the box. Look for a visible label, suffix or port arrangement that connects the two. Put that identifier beside the feature claim and link to the relevant section of the manual. A publication date alone tells a used-equipment buyer little about the unit on the shelf.

Keep the older answer available

An archived manual still serves owners and secondhand buyers. Give its landing page the revision name and explain how to reach the current edition. If both manuals use the same illustration, check the revision notes before relying on that image to distinguish them.

One audio dock, two connector layouts

Imagine an audio brand whose original dock accepts audio over USB. Its replacement adds USB charging, and both boxes say Studio Dock. The manuals and rear-label drawings establish that the charging model carries a C suffix. The brand can publish the charging answer with the suffix and a link to its manual. If an early run of the charging model lacks that suffix, the page needs the alternate identifier from support before it can answer for those units.
