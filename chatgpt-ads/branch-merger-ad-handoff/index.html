ChatGPT advertising · Regional campaigns and multibranch operations

Updating regional ChatGPT ads when two branches merge

Move a closing branch’s ChatGPT ads traffic to its successor once you know which new requests the successor can fulfill. Buyers with an existing booking may need a different contact. A blanket redirect can hide the very details that prompted someone to choose the old branch.

Check what moves with the name

Review the outgoing ad, advertised address and booking terms against the successor’s intake sheet. A renamed office at the same premises may require little more than an explanation of the name change. A move across town can change walk-in access, pickup arrangements and which neighborhoods the operator serves. Retire copy that still invites a visit to the closed counter.

Separate outstanding bookings from new demand

Use the old location page to explain who handles work already booked and where new buyers can go. Test the phone greeting as well as the booking link. A forwarded number is appropriate only if the receiving desk can either help the caller or identify the person responsible for the original order.

Two equipment rental counters combine

Imagine a rental group reviewing a branch closure notice, its pickup manifest and the surviving depot’s fleet list. New equipment rentals can go to the depot, but prepaid weekend pickups still leave from the original yard. The regional campaign names the depot for new rentals and the old page keeps pickup instructions for existing reservations. If the signed transfer manifest moves those reservations too, the group updates the instructions with the new collection address.
