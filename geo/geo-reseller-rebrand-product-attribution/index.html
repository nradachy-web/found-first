GEO for a reseller rebrand when product brands stay the same

Buy GEO for a reseller rebrand when buyers can no longer tell whether a new name identifies the dealer or the equipment it sells. The rewrite should preserve the maker and model a purchasing team would put on a requisition.

Separate the catalog by whose name is changing

A dealer's packages, installation plans and house labels may change with its trading name. Manufacturer model numbers often survive the change. Compare an old quote, a current dealer catalog and the manufacturer's product sheet before asking a writer to rename a whole range.

Be exact about dealer status

Being recognizable under a previous name and being authorized to sell a particular line are different claims. Use the manufacturer's current dealer listing or an approved public statement for the latter. If the listing still shows the old name, the dealer can explain its own name change while it seeks confirmation of the listing.

A dealership renames its installation package

Imagine a commercial laundry dealer adopts a new trading name. Its latest specification sheet and the maker's catalog identify the same washer model, but the dealer's new proposal gives its installation package a new name. A GEO writer should put the maker and model in the machine comparison and cross-reference the two package names on the dealer's service page. If the old proposal included removal of the existing washer and the new one excludes removal, the names alone cannot establish an equivalent package. The comparison should spell out who arranges removal.
