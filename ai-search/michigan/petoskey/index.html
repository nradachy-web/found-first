What happens after the initial interest?

The purchase concludes during the visit

The customer needs clarity about the immediate offer and experience. A strong position should help them judge whether the business fits their purpose without implying a more complex relationship than they need.

The relationship continues at a distance

The buyer needs to understand what support, expertise or purchasing arrangement remains available. The business should distinguish services that travel from those that depend on being physically present.

Questions worth asking.

Should a Petoskey business serve only seasonal visitors?+

Only if that reflects the intended offer. The district also includes offices and serves a wider regional community. Each company should define its own customer relationships instead of treating a destination identity as a complete business strategy.

Can a specialist attract customers who rarely visit Petoskey?+

Yes, when the service model supports a continuing relationship. The buyer should understand which parts of the offer can be arranged remotely and which require a visit. Expertise can travel farther than an address without making every service remote.

What if our product is easier to understand than our advice?+

The advisory value may deserve a stronger introduction. Buyers can recognize a product category while missing the expertise that improves their choice. A useful position connects that expertise to the decisions the company is prepared to help customers make.
