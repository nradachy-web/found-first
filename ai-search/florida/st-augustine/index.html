Questions worth asking.

Should every St. Augustine contractor emphasize historic work?+

No. The positioning should follow actual experience and the services offered. A general provider can be useful locally without claiming a specialization that belongs to a different kind of engagement.

Can AEO explain project context without giving approval advice?+

Yes. Buyers can be helped to recognize the importance of context and provider suitability. Specific approval questions belong with the relevant authorities and qualified professionals reviewing the actual project.

What if our expertise is known only through referrals?+

That expertise may be difficult for an unfamiliar buyer to recognize independently. An AI visibility discussion should examine whether the initial description communicates the specific work that makes your business worth considering.
