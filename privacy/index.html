Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and who it goes to.

Effective November 6, 2026

Who we are.

Found First is the brand of Be Found First LLC, a Michigan company. Questions about this policy go to jeff@befoundfirst.ai.

Our website.

befoundfirst.ai doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. The visibility check form doesn't send anything to us by itself. It lets you copy your message and opens your own email app, and we only receive what you choose to send.

The form doesn't save what you type in your browser or add it to a web address, and it clears its fields when you leave the page.

Optional measurement is off. The site has a switched-off option to measure whether an inquiry follows a ChatGPT ad, using OpenAI's measurement pixel. While it's off, nothing loads from OpenAI, no cookies are set and nothing is measured. If we ever turn it on, the site will ask you first and measure only if you agree, and you'll be able to send an inquiry either way.

Our website host may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.

Working with us.

If you email us or become a client, we collect your contact details, the business details you share, such as your website, services and service areas, and records of the work we do for you.

If you use our client portal, we also keep your account details and use cookies to keep you signed in. If an advertising account is connected, we read its performance data to show it in your dashboard.

How we use it.

We use this information to provide our services, prepare your reports and plans, send account and service emails, keep our systems secure, and meet our legal obligations.

Text messages.

We send operational text alerts to Found First staff who have agreed in writing. We don't currently text clients or the public. If that changes, we'll ask for consent first. Mobile numbers are used only for these alerts. See our SMS Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Our messaging provider and phone carriers handle a number only to deliver the messages.

Who we share it with.

We don't sell personal information. We use service providers to run the business, for hosting and databases, email and text delivery, and the AI tools we use to research and prepare client work. They receive what they need for that work.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people or our rights.

How long we keep it.

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide our services and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Your choices.

Email us to ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it. Clients can also edit their business facts in the portal. We may need to keep some records for legal or accounting reasons.

Security.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children.

Our services are for businesses and aren't directed at children under 13.

Changes.

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
