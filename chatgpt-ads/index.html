ChatGPT ads

A sponsored placement gives a buyer a chance to act on an offer. A contractor might offer an estimate, a software company a demo, or a retailer an in-stock product. What customers see after the click determines whether they can take the next step.

Start with one offer and the action you can measure. The examples below cover campaign fit, creative, product feeds and lead quality. OpenAI controls ad eligibility and delivery. Sponsored placements are separate from organic answers.
