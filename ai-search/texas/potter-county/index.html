Does an Amarillo identity explain all Potter County service needs?+

It provides a useful geographic reference, but the business's actual footprint still matters. The county and city are different concepts, and customers should understand where the provider operates rather than infer coverage from the most familiar name.

Can a regional supplier have a focused local service area?+

Yes. Product customers may come from farther away while onsite support remains concentrated. The public identity should keep those relationships distinct so wider sales reach does not imply identical service availability throughout the region.
