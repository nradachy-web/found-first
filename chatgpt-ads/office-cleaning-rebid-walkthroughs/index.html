Office cleaning rebids in ChatGPT ads: quote or walkthrough?

A commercial cleaning firm can advertise a written quote when the facilities buyer has a current schedule of work that bidders can price on the same basis. Sell the walkthrough when the paperwork leaves a material part of the work in doubt.

Find the gap a walkthrough can close

Square footage alone will not tell a supervisor which tenant suites are in scope, whether meeting rooms are available before the crew leaves or who replenishes washroom supplies. Ask for the room list, task frequencies and access window. A walkthrough earns its place in the offer if the supervisor will return an exceptions sheet the facilities manager can use to amend the bid instructions.

Make comparisons possible at rebid

A facilities manager who already has a complete specification may simply need a comparable submission. Offer to return the buyer's schedule with inclusions and exceptions marked against each line. If a quote request counts as an advertising conversion, retain the distinction between requests received and specifications your estimator could actually price. OpenAI's reporting depends on how the conversion event is set up.

The floor plan and cleaning schedule disagree

Suppose a bidder receives a floor plan showing a shared training suite, but the cleaning schedule lists only the tenant offices. The bidder would request a joint walkthrough and document who cleans the suite after evening classes. If the manager supplies a revised schedule assigning that work to the building crew, the bidder can quote the remaining office work from the pack and spare the manager a visit.
