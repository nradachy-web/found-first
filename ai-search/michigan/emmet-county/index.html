Questions worth asking.

Should a Petoskey-based company also target the wider county?+

When the company can support the relevant service relationships. Petoskey can remain its base while the offer explains coverage elsewhere. A broader identity should reflect actual capability rather than imply a physical location in every community.

Is working for an absent owner the same as routine local service?+

Not necessarily. The communication, authorization and coordination expectations can differ. A business should make clear which arrangements it supports so a buyer can judge fit before assuming the company offers a particular level of responsibility.

Can one provider serve both households and property managers?+

Yes, when both offers are genuine and their differences remain understandable. The same technical skill can support different commercial relationships. The company’s identity should preserve those distinctions rather than make every inquiry sound like the same purchase.
