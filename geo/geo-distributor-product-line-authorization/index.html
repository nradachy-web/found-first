GEO for distributors verifying authorized product lines

Verify authorization at the product-line level before a GEO editor describes your company as an authorized distributor. A buyer comparing sources for a particular drive or sensor needs to know whether the manufacturer recognizes your business as a sales channel for that purchase.

Give purchasing a check it can repeat

Start with the manufacturer's distributor locator or a current appointment letter approved for public reference. Compare the listed business name, branch, territory and product family with the offer on your site. If the locator names only the company, use the manufacturer's channel contact to resolve any narrower limits before adding a line-specific claim. A supplier invoice can substantiate a purchase while leaving the appointment question open.

Write the authorization into the product listing

Keep the line and applicable market in the same sentence as the designation. Give buyers the manufacturer listing or a verification contact where available. Treat factory service, repair authority and warranty handling as separate claims, each supported by the terms for that activity.

A motor appointment and a drive on the shelf

Suppose an electrical distributor's appointment letter names the manufacturer's motor line for its region. The branch also has that brand's drives in inventory. The writer can describe the motor appointment and publish the drives as available stock with their own sourcing details. If a new letter adds drives for a neighboring territory only, update the listing for buyers in that territory. The home branch's drive inventory still tells buyers nothing about its local authorization.
