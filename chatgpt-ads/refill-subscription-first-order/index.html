When to test a refill subscription in ChatGPT ads

Offer a refill subscription in ChatGPT ads when a first-time buyer can estimate how quickly they will use the product. If fit, dose or household usage is still an open question, a one-time starter order gives the buyer a way to answer it before agreeing to repeat shipments.

Establish a plausible reorder date

Read the use instructions with the pack contents and available shipment intervals. Could someone who knows their routine work out when they would run out? A skip option helps with a vacation. It does little for a buyer who cannot yet tell whether a pack will last weeks or months.

Keep the two offers commercially distinct

A starter pack should make sense at its stated terms even if the customer never subscribes. For direct subscriptions, check whether another shipment is accepted when it comes due. If checkout fires the same conversion event for both offers, the platform total merges two different commitments. Use order type and shipment status to interpret it.

Filter size is known, replacement cadence is uncertain

Suppose an aquarium supplier sells rolls of filter floss in a refill plan. Its compatibility chart establishes fit, but maintenance logs show that planted tanks and heavily stocked tanks foul the material at different rates. Advertise a single roll to owners still learning their maintenance routine. For maintenance firms with service logs and a known roll count per visit, the same product supports a scheduled refill offer. They already have the facts needed to choose an interval.
