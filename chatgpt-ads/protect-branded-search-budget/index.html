ChatGPT advertising · Channel comparison and media allocation

Should branded search pay for ChatGPT ads?

Keep the search budget that profitably captures named-course demand while enrollment is open. A ChatGPT ads trial has a clearer purpose when it reaches people still choosing a course and draws its funding from a search segment whose next booking is less valuable.

Separate a course name from a course decision

Someone searching the exact title printed on an employer's training allowance may already have approval. Someone weighing an intensive course against evening study still has a decision to make. Read the actual search terms and match paid enrollments to them where records allow. A blended acquisition cost can conceal how much the account depends on people who arrive with the course name in hand.

Establish what a cut would give up

A paid booking on a brand term does not establish that the ad created the booking. If a prior reduction in branded coverage left total paid enrollments steady, that history can support a small transfer. Check whether the course schedule and availability at the time resemble the coming term.

An exam prep provider opens fall registration

Imagine the buyer has the search-term export, an employer reimbursement sheet naming the course and the registrar's paid roster. Branded ads bring in approved learners close to the cutoff. Broad study-guide terms mostly bring free downloads. The buyer moves the broad-term allocation into a ChatGPT ads test for working adults comparing study formats. If the roster instead ties those downloads to subsequent paid cohorts, the buyer preserves that search allocation and waits for a separate test budget.
