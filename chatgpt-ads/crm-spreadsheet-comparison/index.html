Where should ChatGPT ads comparing a CRM with spreadsheets lead?

Send a CRM comparison ad to a side-by-side account of the same sales task in the product and a spreadsheet. Choose a task with a genuine tradeoff, so a buyer who could reasonably keep the sheet learns something from the comparison.

Keep the starting conditions fair

Use the same sample account, the same handoff and the same definition of completion. Give the spreadsheet its ordinary advantages, including filters, shared editing and any reminder mechanism in the setup you describe. Identify the person responsible for each manual step. Otherwise a comparison may merely document that one example received more setup than the other.

Show what a buyer would give up

If a team can add an exception column in its sheet but needs an administrator to add a CRM field, show both paths. Include the underlying sample rows or screen views. ChatGPT ads appear separately from answers, so present the judgment as the vendor's comparison and make the basis for it inspectable.

Reassigning a deal before a rep goes on leave

Suppose a vendor builds a shared sheet, a reminder rule and a CRM test account around the same renewal. Both tools change the owner. The sheet's reminder still points to the departing rep until someone edits its rule. The tested CRM transfers the reminder with the renewal. That difference earns a place in the ad destination. If a prospect already maintains an owner-based reminder in its sheet, the seller drops that claimed advantage and asks which handoff still costs the team work.
