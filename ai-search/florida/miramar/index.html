Which internal buyer needs clarity?

The initial researcher

They need a credible reason to include your company. A precise description helps them recognize fit without requiring them to become an expert in your industry first.

The accountable decision maker

They need to understand the scope of the relationship and why your capability matches the organization’s need. General visibility is insufficient if that justification disappears during the handoff.

Questions worth asking.

Is AI visibility relevant when our customers use a formal purchasing process?+

It can influence early discovery and understanding, but it does not replace the customer’s procurement requirements. A useful business description helps the right people identify a potential provider before their formal evaluation begins.

Should a Miramar supplier emphasize its business park location?+

When it helps explain access, identity or service context. The location alone should not imply zone participation, regulatory status or capabilities the business has not established.

What if different people describe our service differently?+

That can signal an important positioning issue. A buyer may use an operational term while a purchaser uses a broader category. The business needs a coherent identity that makes sense across those perspectives without changing the underlying promise.
