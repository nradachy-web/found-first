Questions worth asking.

Should planned projects and urgent service share one coverage claim?+

Only if the company can support the same footprint for both. The timing and operating demands can differ. A clear identity allows customers to recognize the appropriate service without assuming every request follows the same arrangement.

Does easy road access justify wider marketing?+

It may be relevant to operations, but the business still needs to assess assignment fit and service responsibility. A road connection is not by itself a reason to pursue every nearby market or promise the same availability throughout it.

What if we want to move away from small urgent jobs?+

Then the desired project relationship should become more recognizable. AEO can be evaluated against that business direction. More general visibility may preserve the old inquiry pattern unless the company’s actual offer and intended customer are understood differently.
