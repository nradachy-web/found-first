What to check when ChatGPT ads show zero impressions

A zero-impression report is a reason to ask whether the ad had a chance to run. Before commissioning new creative, get the campaign record and the report that contains the zero.

Find the actual zero

Ask for the impression count beside the campaign name or identifier and the dates covered. A blank cell in an agency workbook might mean the campaign was left out of the extract. Even a genuine zero has a narrow meaning. It tells you how many impressions the report recorded for that campaign in that period. It tells you very little about demand for the offer.

Establish the opportunity to run

Compare the period with the planned schedule and any dated record of a pause or other delivery restriction. A screenshot taken today tells you the present state. If nobody can establish the earlier state, record that gap. When the dates line up, the campaign appears able to run and the report still reads zero, give those records to whoever can investigate delivery.

A class announcement ahead of enrollment

Suppose a pottery studio's owner sees zero impressions for a class announcement. The dated launch email says to hold the campaign until enrollment opens, and the report ends the evening before. The owner requests a report covering enrollment. If the same zero covers open enrollment and the manager can document that the campaign was active then, the owner requests a delivery investigation before paying for another ad concept.
