AEO for collision shops coordinating camera calibration

A collision shop can use AEO to explain who handles camera calibration when a repair requires it. Say who checks the requirements for the vehicle, who arranges the work and whether someone who had a repair elsewhere can book calibration on its own.

Connect the answer to a repair the shop handles

Use the estimator's explanation for a vehicle and repair the shop knows. A camera near a part being replaced is a reason to check the applicable repair information. Its presence alone cannot tell a website visitor which procedure their vehicle needs. Ask for the vehicle details and work being performed before confirming the next appointment.

Describe the handoff all the way to pickup

Where a partner performs the calibration, identify who books it, who has custody of the vehicle and how the owner receives confirmation of completed work. If the owner must make a separate visit, put that information next to the repair description. It affects transportation plans as well as the estimate.

A service on an invoice that cannot be booked alone

Consider a shop whose sample repair file contains a partner appointment and a completion report for a forward camera. For its own collision customers, the shop arranges the partner visit and reviews the report before pickup. The writer can answer their scheduling question directly. A driver who had work done elsewhere and wants only a calibration needs a different answer. Under this shop's intake policy, the driver contacts the calibration provider directly.
