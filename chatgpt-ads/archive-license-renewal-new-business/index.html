ChatGPT advertising · Offer qualification and buying readiness

Separating archive license renewals from new sales in ChatGPT ads

Test an archive licensing offer for new business only if the publisher can distinguish a new license holder from a returning one. A new contact, email address or checkout can belong to an institution that already has rights under an existing agreement.

Use the contract to define a new sale

Choose the commercial unit before the campaign starts. If rights attach to a network, another producer at that network may already be covered. Additional rights under a current agreement can be valuable expansion revenue. A renewal extends existing rights. Each calls for a different offer and a different interpretation of acquisition cost.

Decide what a first-time buyer needs

A prospect evaluating first access needs to see the collection scope and permitted uses. A renewing buyer needs their current terms and expiry date. Advertisers do not receive private ChatGPT conversations. Establish license status using information the buyer supplies and the publisher's own agreements.

A producer asks about sound effects for a new podcast

Suppose an audio archive receives an inquiry after advertising a first network license. Its rights schedule covers all shows owned by an existing licensee, including this producer's new show. Direct the producer to the existing access administrator and leave the inquiry out of the new-business count. If the same producer is buying for an independent production company outside the agreement, offer a first license to that company. The show title and the contact are identical. The party exercising the rights changes the sale.
