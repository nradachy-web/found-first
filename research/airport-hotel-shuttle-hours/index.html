Can you catch the airport shuttle at 12:30 a.m.?

Found First reviewed 12 hotels across three airports. Eleven advertised a shuttle. Five published hours that answered a late-arriving guest’s question.

Found First Research · November 25, 2026

A late arrival changes the question.

A guest landing at half past midnight has to decide whether to book the room or budget for another ride. All 12 hotels in our sample stated whether they offered an airport shuttle. Of the 11 that offered one, only 5 gave hours precise enough to settle that decision on the pages we reviewed.

What a traveler could actually decide

At Atlanta, the difference was one line of copy. Hilton Atlanta Airport listed service until 2 a.m. Hampton Inn & Suites Atlanta Airport North I85 listed an 11:45 p.m. cutoff. Both advertised a complimentary shuttle. A traveler arriving at 12:30 a.m. had a reason to make a different booking decision at each hotel. Hilton Atlanta Airport hours · Hampton Atlanta North hours.

In Detroit, Hilton Garden Inn mentioned its free airport shuttle and its 24-hour snack shop in the same introduction. The overnight hours belonged to the shop. Its transportation page confirmed a shuttle but supplied no timetable. We recorded the overnight answer as unclear. Hotel overview · Transportation details.

For a hotel team working on answer engine optimization (AEO), the useful edit is small. Put the first and last shuttle runs beside the airport name, then say whether the arriving guest needs to call and where pickup takes place. A traveler can make a decision from those facts. An answer engine also has a precise statement it can cite. Hotel GEO guide for airport transfers.

How we counted

On November 25, 2026, we took the first four distinct properties shown in each of Hilton’s standard airport directories for Detroit Metro, Chicago O’Hare and Atlanta. Using one hotel group gave us comparable transportation fields. We kept all twelve properties, including the one that reported no shuttle. Detroit · Chicago · Atlanta.

We read each hotel’s English-language overview and official location page, 24 pages in total. We first classified the overview, then checked whether the location page changed the answer. The second page did not change the classification for any of the twelve. We counted a stated 24-hour service or a published time window as sufficient. A pickup instruction, frequency or reference to another 24-hour amenity left the hours unresolved.

This is a dated audit of first-party page text from one hotel group and three airports. The retrieved copies ranged from the day of review to approximately two weeks old. Other pages, hotel staff or updated timetables may provide additional details. The audit measured what a reader could establish about operating hours. Actual departures, AI responses, rankings and reservation outcomes were outside its scope.
