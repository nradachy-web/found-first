How to get named by ChatGPT

No submission or payment secures a recommendation from ChatGPT: OpenAI says placement in ChatGPT search is not guaranteed and that ads do not influence answers. What a business can do is let OpenAI's search crawler read its site, publish pages that answer the questions customers ask, and keep its facts and public evidence consistent wherever they appear.

What ChatGPT does when someone asks who to call

OpenAI says ChatGPT may search the web automatically when a question would benefit from current information. When it works with other search providers, it typically rewrites the question into one or more targeted queries, and it may use the person's approximate location to return local results. Responses that use web search may include citations to the pages they drew on.

So when ChatGPT searches, an answer about who to hire is assembled from what that search returns for the rewritten question on that day. OpenAI says results are ranked on multiple factors intended to surface relevant, reliable information. Nothing in its guidance describes a list a business can join.

It can show up in more than one way. In our first published study the same business appeared as a shortlist entry, as the source of practical advice and as a caution. That is why we say a business is named or mentioned in an answer. Whether a mention is an endorsement depends on the sentence around it.

Five things that give ChatGPT a reason to name you

In this order, because each one limits the next. Evidence cannot help a page the crawler is not allowed to read.

  1. Let the search crawler in

    Allow OAI-SearchBot in your robots.txt file and confirm that your host or content delivery network accepts traffic from OpenAI's published search crawler addresses. This setting is separate from GPTBot, which covers model training, so you can allow one and refuse the other.

  2. Make your facts agree

    Settle one version of your business name, address, phone number, hours, service area and list of services. Put it on your site in plain text, then make every profile and listing you manage say the same thing.

  3. Give each customer problem its own page

    A home page cannot answer a specific question about a hidden leak, an unusual requirement or a comparison. Write one useful page per problem you want to be found for. How to choose those questions is its own subject.

  4. Add evidence a stranger can check

    Licenses, memberships, named staff, photos of real work, and reviews from real customers on the sites where your trade is reviewed. Your own claims carry more weight when another source says the same thing.

  5. Ask the questions again and keep the record

    Put the same customer questions to ChatGPT on a later date and compare the answers with the first set. One answer is an anecdote. A dated series is something you can act on.

Why asking about your own name tells you little

Most owners test this by asking ChatGPT about their own company. The question that matters is the one a customer asks before they know your name, and in our first published study the answer changed sharply with the problem the customer described.

How to check where you stand today

An hour of careful checking tells you more than a month of guessing. The AI visibility audit page covers a fuller review.

  • Read your robots.txtOpen yoursite.com/robots.txt and look for a Disallow rule that applies to OAI-SearchBot, either under its own name or under a rule for all crawlers.
  • Ask your host about bot blockingSecurity settings at a host or content delivery network can turn crawlers away even when robots.txt allows them. OpenAI publishes the addresses its search crawler uses.
  • Write down the questions firstFive to ten questions a customer would ask before knowing your name. Describe the problem, the place and the need, not your brand.
  • Test signed outOpenAI says web search works without signing in, and that saved memories and location can shape the search ChatGPT runs. A signed-out window gives a cleaner reading than your own account.
  • Save every answerKeep the date, the exact wording, the full answer and the sources ChatGPT shows. A screenshot of one good answer is not a record.
  • Read the mention, not just the nameWas the business listed as an option, quoted for advice or used as a caution? Are the name, phone number, area and services right?
  • Open the cited pagesOpenAI warns that search results and citations can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Check that each source supports what the answer says about you.
  • Repeat on another dayUse the same questions and wording. Compare the sets before deciding what to fix.

Questions about being named by ChatGPT

Can I pay ChatGPT to recommend my business?+

No. OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers and that advertisers cannot shape, rank or alter responses. An ad is a clearly labeled placement shown below the answer. Being named in the answer itself is separate work, measured separately.

Is there a way to submit my business to ChatGPT?+

The step OpenAI documents for site owners is crawler access: allow OAI-SearchBot and make sure your host or content delivery network lets it through. OpenAI says that makes a site eligible and that placement is not guaranteed.

Do I have to allow GPTBot as well?+

No. OpenAI says each crawler setting is independent. A site can allow OAI-SearchBot so it can appear in ChatGPT search and disallow GPTBot, which covers the use of crawled content for model training.

Why does ChatGPT name a competitor and not us?+

An answer alone cannot tell you. Our research records what appeared and does not establish why. The useful checks are whether the crawler can read your site, whether you have a page that answers that exact question, whether your facts agree across the web, and which sources the answer cited.

Why did my customer get a different answer from the one I got?+

Answers vary with the wording, the date, the model and the person. OpenAI says ChatGPT may use approximate location and, if memory is enabled, saved memories when it rewrites a question into a search. Judge a series of saved answers, not one.

How long does it take?+

OpenAI says a robots.txt change can take about 24 hours to reach its search systems. Beyond that there is no schedule anyone can promise, and Found First does not guarantee mentions, rankings or leads. We report what we observe each time we repeat the questions.

Should I publish hundreds of AI-written pages?+

No. Publish a page when it helps a distinct buyer decision and contains accurate, first-hand material. Repeating the same content with different keywords is not a content strategy.
