Before we talk

Clear answers.
A better first call.

What does Found First actually do?+

We provide organic AI search visibility work, often called AEO or GEO, and managed ChatGPT advertising. Website, content and measurement work connect those services to the business behind them. We discuss the right engagement after understanding your goals.

Are ChatGPT ads the same as appearing in an AI answer?+

No. Paid placements and independent answers are separate. Buying an ad does not buy an organic recommendation. We help you decide which opportunity fits and keep the outcomes distinct.

Do I need a new website?+

Not automatically. Your existing site may already provide a strong foundation. We look at whether it explains the offer and supports the next customer action before discussing changes.

Who will I speak with?+

Your first call is with Nick Radachy, founder of Modern Apex Strategies. Nick and Jeffrey Davis, co-founder of Frontier App Labs, are the co-founders of Found First. The agency operates as Be Found First LLC and is based in Harrison Township, Michigan.

What happens on the call?+

We spend 30 minutes discussing your business, customers and current acquisition activity. Bring your website and questions. The purpose is to determine whether AI visibility, ChatGPT advertising or another priority makes sense for you.

Can you guarantee rankings, mentions or leads?+

No. Platforms control their answers and placements. We agree the work and the business objective clearly, and keep observed activity separate from confirmed customer outcomes.
