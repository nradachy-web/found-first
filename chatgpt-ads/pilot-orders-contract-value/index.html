Valuing pilot orders from ChatGPT ads before a rollout

Value a pilot sale from ChatGPT ads using what the buyer has actually ordered. Keep the potential rollout in the forecast until the buyer commits to it, and tell finance whether the figure you are presenting is an order value or a payment received.

Read the purchasing document behind the win

A sales rep may mark an account won when a buyer approves a trial. The proposal can still include optional locations, extra units or future service that purchasing has yet to approve. Check the accepted quantities and the issued purchase order. If the document leaves the quantities unclear, ask the account owner to confirm them before using the larger figure in a spending decision.

Give expansion its own date

If a rollout follows, keep the original acquisition source on the account history and record the additional order when it arrives. That lets the owner evaluate what pilot customers eventually buy while preserving what was known at the time of the original campaign review. Use receipts for a cash payback decision.

A pilot locker installation for a gym group

A locker supplier might win a paid trial after a gym operator responds to its ChatGPT ad. The proposal lists every facility, but the signed order covers only the test site and rollout depends on the operator's inspection. The supplier reports the test order and leaves the other sites in its forecast. If purchasing issues a firm order for the full installation with delivery in phases, the supplier can report the committed rollout value and show collections separately as invoices are paid.
