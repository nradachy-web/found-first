How can AI discovery matter after an out-of-town referral?+

A generated answer may help the new buyer determine whether the recommendation fits their location and request. The useful objective is a clear account of the actual service arrangement, so a trusted introduction does not become an assumption of identical availability across the county.

Does a Smith County recommendation imply coverage throughout East Texas?+

No. County identity, regional reputation and actual service territory are different facts. The company may accept selected work farther away while limiting routine visits. The buyer needs to understand which arrangement applies to the work being considered.

Should a specialist firm try to appear relevant to every related request?+

No. A clear specialty gives the buyer a better reason to consider the company. Broader recognition should not blur the boundaries that make the firm valuable or create expectations around work it is not equipped to accept.
