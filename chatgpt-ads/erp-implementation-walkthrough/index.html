Selling an ERP implementation walkthrough with ChatGPT ads

For an ERP campaign aimed at buyers worried about cutover, sponsor a walkthrough of one transaction that crosses the switch. Give buyers enough implementation detail to see which work their own team would inherit.

Choose a transaction with unfinished business

A clean purchase order entered after launch says little about implementation. A partially received order raises questions a project owner can use. Which balance comes over? Where does the receiving clerk see it? Who reconciles a mismatch? Ask the agency to connect the ad to material that follows the same order through those steps.

Make the evidence portable

A redacted mapping sheet, a rehearsal record and an exceptions list can travel to the buyer's implementation meeting. Identify the product edition and the division of work between vendor, partner and customer. If the artifacts come from a demonstration environment, label them as such. Language about a seamless launch adds nothing a project lead can check.

An open order at the warehouse door

Imagine an ERP vendor assembling a walkthrough from a sample receiving log, a field map and the receiving clerk's screen. Its rehearsal carries the remaining quantity forward, but a unit mismatch sends one line to an exception queue. The vendor advertises the cutover walkthrough and includes the clerk's correction and the finance owner's signoff. If the intended buyer also needs lot history and the rehearsal covers quantities only, sales scopes that requirement before presenting the walkthrough as evidence of fit.
