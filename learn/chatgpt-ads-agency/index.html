ChatGPT ads management

Managing ChatGPT ads means confirming a business is eligible, building campaigns around the conversations where its offer is relevant, sending each click to a page that continues that conversation, and counting inquiries, not clicks. Found First does this inside a monthly program. The ad account, the billing and approval of every ad stay with the advertiser, and no one can guarantee where an ad will appear.

[ definition ]

ChatGPT ads management is the ongoing work of running paid placements in ChatGPT for a business: confirming eligibility, structuring campaigns and context hints, writing and testing ads, keeping the landing page and conversion measurement working, and reporting what the spend produced.

An ad is a clearly labeled placement below an answer. It does not change what ChatGPT says. For how the channel differs from search advertising, see ChatGPT ads vs. Google Ads.

From eligibility to reporting

Six stages, in this order. The first two are OpenAI's gates, and nothing runs until they are cleared.

  1. Check that you can advertise

    Self-serve Ads Manager requires the legal entity that advertises and is billed to be based in a listed country. OpenAI does not allow political advertising, and it publishes a table of which health, financial and legal categories are allowed, restricted or not allowed.

  2. Open the account in your name

    One person at your business creates the advertiser account, sets up billing and invites us. OpenAI may ask for business verification, which it says can take up to 7 business days.

  3. Build campaigns around conversations

    Each campaign has one objective and one budget. Its ad groups carry context hints that describe the conversations where the offer is relevant, and OpenAI's guidance is to give each offer several distinct titles and descriptions.

  4. Get the landing page ready

    The page has to continue the conversation that led to the click. It must also load for OAI-AdsBot, the crawler OpenAI uses to review landing pages, and carry conversion measurement and tagged URLs.

  5. Pass review, then launch

    Every ad is checked against OpenAI's policies before it can serve. After launch we confirm in Ads Manager that the ads are serving, and watch early delivery against the budget.

  6. Report and adjust

    Ads Manager reports impressions, clicks, spend and conversions. We add the inquiries your business actually received, change one thing at a time and keep a record of what changed.

Who does what

A manager can do most of the work. A few things can only be done by the business whose name is on the ads, and it is better to know which before you start.

What a first test looks like

A first test is small on purpose: one offer, a capped daily budget, which is how OpenAI advises new advertisers to start, one definition of a qualified inquiry and a review date set before launch. OpenAI publishes no benchmarks yet, so the comparison that counts is your own: ChatGPT ads vs. Google Ads sets out how to run it, and ChatGPT advertising costs covers the budget.

What management cannot do

No manager can make an ineligible business eligible, place an ad in a particular conversation or hurry OpenAI's review. Context hints guide matching, and OpenAI says they do not guarantee delivery in specific conversations.

No manager can buy a mention either. OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers and that advertisers cannot shape, rank or alter responses. Being named in the answer itself is the work of answer engine optimization, and we report it separately.

And the platform is in beta. OpenAI says to expect changes to delivery, ad formats and how campaigns are bought and measured, so we re-read its documentation before each build. Found First does not guarantee delivery, leads or a cost per lead.

Questions about managed ChatGPT ads

Do ChatGPT ads influence ChatGPT's answers?+

No. OpenAI states that ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers and that advertisers cannot shape, rank or alter responses. An ad is a clearly labeled paid placement below the answer. Being named in the answer itself is separate work.

Who owns the ad account, and who pays for the media?+

You do. The account is set up in your business's name, OpenAI bills your legal entity for the media, and Found First works inside the account with the access you grant. Management is a separate monthly fee.

Can every business advertise in ChatGPT?+

No. To use self-serve Ads Manager, the legal entity that advertises and is billed must be based in a country OpenAI lists as available. Political advertising is not allowed, and sensitive or regulated categories such as health and financial services face stricter eligibility criteria. We check the current rules for your business before proposing a campaign.

Can I run ChatGPT ads myself?+

Yes. Ads Manager is self-serve where it is available. What a manager adds is time and judgment: structure, context hints, landing pages, measurement and a disciplined test. One caution from OpenAI's setup guide: an ad created during signup can go live once it passes review, so pause the campaign if you are not ready to spend.

How soon can ads start?+

That depends on OpenAI's checks as much as on us. Billing must be set up, each ad is reviewed before it can serve, and some accounts are asked to complete business verification, which OpenAI says can take up to 7 business days.

Do you guarantee results?+

No. OpenAI publishes no performance benchmarks yet, and context hints do not guarantee delivery in specific conversations. We commit to the work, a spending limit you set and a report of what happened.
