ChatGPT advertising · Landing page decisions after the ad

Where should ChatGPT ads visitors check service-area eligibility?

Put the service-area check ahead of the full inquiry whenever location can rule out the advertised job. Ask for the least specific location that reliably settles coverage under your actual dispatch rules.

Choose the geographic detail your dispatcher uses

A list of towns works when every address in each named town qualifies. A postal code works if the boundary follows postal zones. Where a route cuts through a town or postal zone, request a street address at that point and explain why. A billing address can give the wrong answer for a service performed at another property.

Distinguish an uncovered address from an unknown one

If a location is outside the route, tell the visitor before requesting job photos or contact details. A new street missing from an address lookup calls for a different response. Let the visitor request a manual coverage check with just the location and a way to reply. “Address not found” alone leaves both cases unresolved.

A linen pickup route that stops at a river

Take a linen service whose dispatch map covers the west bank while the same postal code spans both banks. Its ChatGPT ad can land on a pickup page that asks where the linens will be collected. A confirmed east-bank address receives the coverage message. A new west-bank apartment missing from the lookup goes to dispatch for review. If the service expands to the whole postal zone, the first check can shrink to a postal code.
