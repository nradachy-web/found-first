AEO vs. SEO vs. GEO

SEO is the work of being found in search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) and GEO (generative engine optimization) are two names for one newer job: being named and described accurately in answers written by AI. All three rest on the same foundations, and the real differences are in what you measure, so judge a proposal by its work and its reporting, not by its acronym.

What stays the same under every name

Four jobs sit under all three names. If you already pay for one of them as SEO, you should not pay for it again as AEO or GEO.

  • Access

    SEO calls it crawling and indexing. For AI answers it is the same check: Google says a supporting link in its AI features must be indexed and eligible for a snippet, with no additional technical requirements. OpenAI documents one crawler rule of its own, OAI-SearchBot, for ChatGPT search.

  • Useful pages

    SEO calls it helpful content. AEO calls it answer pages. A page that settles a real question in plain text is one piece of work, and it serves both.

  • Corroboration

    SEO talks about links and authority. AEO and GEO talk about citations and evidence. Each means credible sources other than you saying what you say about yourself.

  • Consistent business facts

    Local SEO calls it listing consistency. For AI answers it is the same task with a wider reach, because an answer can only repeat the details it finds.

What is new when the result is an answer

Three things change when the result is an answer instead of a list, and none of them depends on the acronym. First, the question replaces the keyword. People describe a situation to an assistant in full sentences, and the answer can turn on the details. Planning by question is a different exercise from planning by search phrase, and AEO content strategy covers it.

Second, the outcome can be a mention with no visit. A person may read the answer, see your name and contact you directly, or never click anything. Traffic reports miss that, which is why mentions and inquiries are tracked as separate lines. See measuring AI marketing results.

Third, the measurement is a sample. An answer changes with the wording, the date and the model, so there is no single position to look up. You ask a defined set of questions, keep the answers and count what you find, with the date and the method stated.

What an AEO measurement looks like

An SEO report gives a position for a query. Research into AI answers gives a count out of a sample, and the count means little until the sample is described: how many answers, from which kind of model route, on which date. This is how two such counts read in our first published study.

Four questions that cut through the acronyms

Ask these of any proposal that uses one of the three terms. Choosing an AEO agency has the fuller list.

  • Which of this work is shared with the SEO we already have?Technical access, content and reputation work should be done once. Ask what is new.
  • What exactly will be sampled?Which questions, which AI systems, how many times and how often. A mention count without a denominator is not a measurement.
  • What will the report keep separate?Search positions, AI mentions, cited sources, visits and inquiries are different signals. A rise in one does not prove a rise in the others.
  • What is promised?Google says serving in its AI features is not guaranteed, and OpenAI says placement in ChatGPT search is not guaranteed. A proposal that guarantees either is promising something the platforms do not.

Platform statements from Google Search Central and the OpenAI Help Center, checked November 11, 2026.

Questions about the three terms

Are AEO and GEO different services?+

Usually not. Agencies use the two terms differently, and most of the work behind them is the same. Compare the deliverables, the AI systems covered, the evidence kept and the reporting method, not the label.

Is GEO just SEO with a new name?+

Not entirely. The foundations are shared, and Google says SEO best practices remain relevant for its AI features. What is new is the unit of work, a question in place of a keyword, and the measurement, a sample of answers in place of a ranking report.

Do I need to buy all three?+

No. You need the shared work done once and the new measurement added. Three line items for the same technical and content work is a pricing choice, not three kinds of value.

Should I cancel my existing SEO work?+

Evaluate it first. Useful content, technical maintenance and earned credibility support both search results and AI answers, and stopping them removes the foundation the newer work depends on.

Is special AI schema or an llms.txt file necessary?+

Not for Google. Google says no new machine readable files, AI text files, markup or special structured data are needed for AI Overviews or AI Mode. Structured data you already use should match the visible text.

Does GEO mean geographic targeting?+

Sometimes. In advertising, geo is common shorthand for location targeting. On this page and in most AI search writing, GEO means generative engine optimization.

Which term does Found First use?+

AEO. We treat GEO as another name for the same work, and we keep SEO fundamentals inside it. Found First does not guarantee rankings, mentions or leads under any of the three names.
