Scoring an AEO pilot when Google shows no AI Overview

Report a Google search with no visible AI Overview as its own observation. It matters to a buyer assessing the opportunity, but it gives you no answer text in which to evaluate a mention or a purchasing detail.

Use a denominator that answers the question

To describe what a prospect might encounter in your sample, include every completed search, including those without an Overview. To assess whether visible Overviews accurately describe your offer, examine only the ones that appeared. State which set you used. Otherwise a change in how often an Overview appeared can look like a change in how well it described the business.

Make the empty observations inspectable

Keep the search wording and the result view for each completed check. Mark a failed capture or an interrupted search as unknown. Google says ordinary search requirements apply to its AI features and inclusion is not assured. One empty result is too little evidence to diagnose a fault on the site or commission a fix.

A supplier disappears from the report

Suppose a bulk paper supplier tests a question about ordering split cartons. The saved Google results show an ordinary listing for its order policy and no Overview. Record the listing separately and mark the Overview as absent. If the capture instead shows an Overview discussing split cartons and naming other suppliers, record an available answer that omitted the business. Only in the second case can the team read that answer's reasoning and examine whether its own policy addresses the conditions buyers were shown.
