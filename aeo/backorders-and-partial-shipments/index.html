Should AEO content promise a split shipment on backorders?

Promise a partial shipment only when the order qualifies under your store's fulfillment rules and the buyer has selected it if required. For AEO, the question worth answering is whether adding an unavailable item changes when the available goods leave.

Test the cart that causes the delay

Review one mixed cart alongside the warehouse release rule and the shipping options buyers actually see. A product detail can truthfully say an item is in stock even when the warehouse holds the entire order until every line is available. State that consequence at the point where buyers can still choose how to order.

Explain changes to an order already placed

A separate shipment available at checkout may be impossible to request after packing begins. Give buyers the relevant handoff point and the contact route for an existing order. If support has to confirm a change, avoid writing a help answer that treats a request as an instruction already carried out.

A fountain is ready and its spare filters are delayed

Suppose a pet supplier's warehouse holds economy orders until complete. Its checkout offers separate dispatch for eligible items, and its pick ticket records the choice. A buyer ordering a fountain with backordered spare filters could elect separate dispatch to receive the fountain first. If the ticket says to ship together, the help answer should explain why the available fountain is waiting. An updated supplier estimate for the filters changes the expected wait, but does not itself release the fountain.
