ChatGPT advertising · Offer qualification and buying readiness

Timing ChatGPT ads for textbook adoption reviews

A textbook publisher can test ChatGPT ads for review copies well before students buy, provided an instructor can still change the assigned text. Course start dates are a poor guide on their own. The decision may be over by the time enrollment opens.

Locate the next decision the instructor controls

A department may select a common text or leave each section's reading list to its instructor. Read the publisher's examination-copy policy against the buyer's adoption instructions. Ask for the course, term and list deadline when they matter to fulfillment. Requests for personal reading tell you little about adoption demand.

Value the copy as a chance to be evaluated

For a title with exercises or supporting files, allow enough time for an instructor to try a lesson before the list closes. A form submission can be recorded as a conversion if configured that way. A later required-book designation is separate evidence, available from the department or its adoption record.

A statistics instructor needs to try the lab files

Suppose a publisher can provide an examination chapter, its accompanying dataset and an instructor guide. The department's course form still permits a new text for next term. Offer the review materials together so the instructor can run a lab before submitting a choice. If the syllabus already mandates a common textbook, a free copy no longer advances that adoption. Reserve the offer for the next curriculum review unless the instructor can independently assign the lab as a supplement and the publisher sells it separately.
