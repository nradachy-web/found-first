ChatGPT advertising · Landing page decisions after the ad

Should ChatGPT ads open a slow configurator or a lighter page?

Load the offer and a usable next step first when a heavy configurator is optional. If the ad invites people to use the tool itself, send them to a working starting state and defer assets they need only after making a selection.

Time the first decision a visitor can actually make

Use a fresh session on a phone and try the page with a slower connection. Watch for the moment you can read the offer, the moment the control appears and the moment it responds. A page can look ready while the button ignores taps. Repeat the test with the preview disabled to see whether the preview is the source of the delay.

Keep the promise attached to the click

A simple list of options can serve a buyer comparing materials. Someone who clicked specifically to visualize their design needs an obvious way into that feature. A lighter introduction earns its place only if it answers a question or makes the first selection usable sooner.

A rug maker weighing a room preview against swatches

Imagine a rug maker with a sample booklet, a swatch request form and an interactive room scene. A mobile test shows the scene blocks every control until its textures load. For the sample ad, the maker can show the swatches and request form immediately. For an ad inviting buyers to build a striped design, it can start with working stripe and color controls, then load the room scene when the visitor chooses to preview the design.
