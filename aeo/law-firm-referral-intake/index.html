AEO for law firms that refer some matters out

If your law firm receives inquiries for a practice it refers elsewhere, use AEO to answer the question that determines whether someone should contact you. Tell the reader who would handle the matter and what, if anything, your intake team can do to make an introduction.

Read the page from the prospective client's side

A practice heading, an attorney biography and a button labeled “Discuss your case” can together imply that the firm takes the work. Ask the writer to read that combination aloud as an answer to “Will someone at this firm represent me?” A biography can accurately describe a lawyer's previous work while giving the wrong answer about what the firm accepts today.

Use a verb the intake team can stand behind

Giving a caller a name, forwarding a request with permission and arranging a meeting are different services. Pick the verb that describes the actual next step. An outside lawyer's ability to take a matter is a separate question for that lawyer.

A business client asks about a trademark filing

Suppose a firm's engagement template covers commercial contracts, its intake sheet lists an outside trademark lawyer, and an older profile mentions trademark experience. For the website, choose “We can share contact information for an outside trademark lawyer” if staff provide a name. If staff instead offer an introduction after the caller agrees, describe how to request one. Either answer gives the marketing team a reason to replace the existing “Start your filing” button.
