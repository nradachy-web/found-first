Questions worth asking.

Should an innovative business emphasize its technology or its service?+

Both can matter, but the customer needs to understand the relationship. A technology description explains what is distinctive; the service explains what the buyer can actually engage the company to do. Neither should remain implicit.

Can AI visibility help an unfamiliar technical category?+

It can be relevant when buyers begin with a problem rather than an established category name. The business needs an intelligible identity that preserves the important specialization, so early exploration can lead to a suitable evaluation.

What if our company collaborates with other specialists?+

The public description should make the company's own responsibility clear. Collaboration can strengthen an offer, but a buyer should not have to infer which capability belongs to which participant or assume the company provides every adjacent service itself.
