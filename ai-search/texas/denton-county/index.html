Questions worth asking.

Does serving a lake area mean serving every surrounding county?+

No. A destination can cross county lines while a company's operating area remains narrower. The service description should reflect actual reach and project suitability rather than rely on the broadest possible interpretation of the place name.

Should a Denton County business target only county-based questions?+

Not necessarily. Customers may describe the same need through a city, destination or regional reference. The business should remain accurately understandable across relevant contexts without turning those references into claims of additional locations.

Can a focused local business accept occasional distant projects?+

Yes. Selective reach and routine coverage can coexist. The distinction should be clear enough that customers understand which kinds of work may justify a wider trip and which depend on the company's regular operating area.
