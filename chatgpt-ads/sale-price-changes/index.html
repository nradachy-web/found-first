Timing sale price changes in a ChatGPT ads catalog

Include a sale price in a ChatGPT ads catalog only for the period and terms your store can honor. Plan the return to the regular price before approving the promotional version of the feed.

Identify which price buyers can actually get

Open the item as a signed-out shopper and compare the catalog price with the product page and cart. An automatic markdown, a coupon entered at checkout and a member price can produce the same final total for some buyers. They are different offers. If the discount depends on a condition the paid offer cannot explain clearly, use the ordinary purchase price or leave the item out of that promotion.

Check both sides of the deadline

Ask for the store's promotion schedule, including its time zone, and the timing of the merchant's catalog exports. An export scheduled just before a sale ends can preserve the promotional price after checkout reverts. If the transition cannot be coordinated, use products with stable prices through that period.

A public linen sale becomes a member offer

Suppose a bedding retailer ends a public linen sale on Sunday evening but extends it for members. The merchandising calendar shows the change, while a logged-in employee still sees the sale price on Monday. A signed-out cart confirms the general price has risen. The team supplies the regular price for the public catalog. If the extension instead applies automatically to every shopper and the page and cart agree, keeping the sale price through the revised end time is supportable.
