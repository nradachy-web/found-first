ChatGPT advertising · Campaign diagnostics and reporting

Which ChatGPT ads campaign drove the increase in account clicks?

To explain growth in ChatGPT ads clicks, account for the change in every campaign that contributed to either period. A new campaign's click total alone cannot explain how much the account grew.

Include campaigns that ended

Start with the account totals for the two periods. List campaign identifiers from both reports, including campaigns that ended before the latest report was prepared. For each one, subtract earlier clicks from later clicks. The changes should add up to the change in the account. Treat a missing row as unresolved until you know whether that campaign was absent, filtered out or genuinely recorded no clicks.

Distinguish contribution from cause

This breakdown tells a buyer where the reported increase occurred. It cannot establish what the older campaigns would have produced had the new one never run. That matters when a supplier uses the account's rise to justify extending a particular campaign.

The exhibition and the archive

Suppose a museum adds an exhibition campaign alongside its archive campaign. The board sees account clicks rise and proposes extending the exhibition buy. With the two campaign reports beside the account summary, the director finds that most of the increase came from the archive. She removes account growth from the case for the extension. If archive clicks were steady and the exhibition clicks reconciled to the increase, she could credit the exhibition with the additional reported clicks and evaluate the extension against its attendance objective.
