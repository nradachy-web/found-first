Questions worth asking.

Should every Marquette business claim countywide service?+

Only when the actual offer supports that reach. Marquette County’s size makes the distinction meaningful. A business can have a strong local position or selective regional project footprint without promising routine availability throughout the entire county.

Can a specialist be regionally relevant from one location?+

Yes. Customers may travel or arrange a defined project when the capability warrants it. The company should distinguish that relationship from recurring on-site service, so wider relevance does not create an inaccurate expectation of coverage.

What if we evaluate distant projects individually?+

That can be a credible operating model. The business should be understood for the kind of assignments it considers and the expertise it brings. Selective availability is more useful to buyers than an unlimited claim the company cannot consistently support.
