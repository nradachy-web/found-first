AEO for drain cleaning and camera inspection requests

If your plumbing company offers both drain clearing and camera inspection, explain what each appointment can establish. A customer with a repeat blockage may need the prior service ticket before your team can suggest the right visit. Describe that handoff in the answer.

Read what the previous visit covered

“Drain cleaned” could refer to the kitchen branch or the line serving several fixtures. Ask which fixtures were affected then, which are affected now and whether the ticket identifies the line that was cleared. A similar symptom at a different fixture is a weak reason to order a repeat of the last job.

Say what comes with the camera visit

Answer “Can you clear the drain on the camera visit?” on the inspection page itself. Explain who confirms access, whether clearing is a separate service and what the customer receives afterward. Someone comparing repair proposals may need a recording with the inspected section identified. Someone with a blocked sink needs to know whether the scheduled technician can get it draining.

One kitchen sink, two possible bookings

Suppose a homeowner's sink is draining today, but their last two tickets both describe a blockage on the kitchen branch. They want to understand the recurrence. The dispatcher sends the tickets to the plumber to confirm whether that branch can be inspected with the company's camera. If the sink is blocked now and the customer primarily needs it usable, the dispatcher schedules drain service and flags the history so the crew can discuss inspection while there.
