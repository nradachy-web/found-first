ChatGPT advertising · Campaign diagnostics and reporting

Can ChatGPT ads reports reveal what buyers asked?

An agency cannot substantiate a claim about what a buyer typed by pointing to ChatGPT ads impressions or clicks. OpenAI says advertisers do not receive private chats. A proposed message can still be worth testing if the agency identifies where the buyer question actually came from.

Follow the wording back to its source

A phrase copied from your sales inbox is evidence that someone asked your business that question. Wording written by a strategist is a hypothesis. An impression count records ad delivery. Although conversational context can inform delivery, the count cannot turn either phrase into a record of the conversation in which the ad appeared.

Ask for a claim the report can support

For a creative decision, you can combine a recurring question in sales records with observed response to an ad that addresses it. Keep those findings distinct in the buying brief. One supports the relevance of the subject to your customers. The other describes response to a particular message.

Where the parts question came from

Imagine a ceramics equipment supplier reviewing a slide that says buyers asked ChatGPT whether replacement kiln elements were in stock. Its only footnote is a click report for a parts ad. The owner removes the claim about chats and treats stock availability as a possible message. If the service desk can also produce inquiries asking about those elements, the owner has customer language to support a test. The revised brief attributes the question to the service desk.
