ChatGPT advertising · Landing page decisions after the ad

Should ChatGPT ads send first-time buyers to a sign-in page?

Keep sign-in out of the main path for a one-time purchase your business can fulfill as a guest order. When the offer depends on a saved design, member entitlement or private order history, explain that dependency before the visitor reaches the account screen.

Find the first step that needs a customer record

Walk through a guest order with the fulfillment team. An email address for a receipt is different from credentials needed to retrieve a private file. If staff can produce, ship and support the item from the order record, offer account creation after checkout and describe the benefit, such as saving the finished specification for a reorder.

Test the return from sign-in

For an account-dependent offer, sign in from the advertised page using a test account that holds a qualifying item. Check where you land. A general dashboard can strand a returning buyer just as effectively as a locked checkout. Return them to the saved item or the specific selection they must make next.

A stationery printer selling a new set or a reprint

A printer might advertise a blank correspondence set and separately offer reprints of approved letterhead. Its packing slip shows that the blank set needs only an ordinary guest order. The reprint, however, relies on an approved proof stored in the customer account. The printer can link that offer to sign-in with a return to saved proofs. A visitor without an existing proof gets a clearly labeled route to submit a new design.
